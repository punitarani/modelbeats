# Brightwater Hospitality Group, Inc. v. Velasco Freight Systems, Inc., et al.

Los Angeles County Superior Court Case No. 26STCV18842, removed to the U.S. District Court for the
Central District of California as Case No. 2:26-cv-06231-RVL (JCx) (Judge Lindqvist).

Our firm represents defendant **Velasco Freight Systems, Inc.** only.

## 1. Parties (from the Complaint and the Notice of Removal)

| Party | Role | Citizenship |
|---|---|---|
| Brightwater Hospitality Group, Inc. | Plaintiff | California corporation, principal place of business Los Angeles |
| Velasco Freight Systems, Inc. ("Velasco") | Defendant (our client) | Texas corporation, principal place of business Laredo, Texas |
| Tidewell Logistics LLC ("Tidewell") | Defendant | Oregon LLC; its two members are citizens of Oregon and Washington |

Claims: breach of contract and negligence arising from a spoiled refrigerated shipment; damages
alleged of $2.4 million. There is no federal claim; the only basis for federal jurisdiction is
diversity of citizenship. No defendant is a citizen of California.

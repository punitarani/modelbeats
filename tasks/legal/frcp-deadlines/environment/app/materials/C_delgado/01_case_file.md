# Delgado v. Ruiz — U.S. District Court, Central District of California, Case No. 2:26-cv-00921-RVL (JCx)

Our firm represents plaintiff **Ana Delgado** and has done so since the complaint was filed.

Defendant Mark Ruiz is a U.S. Customs and Border Protection officer. He is sued in his individual
capacity only, for damages, for acts taken during a secondary inspection at Los Angeles International
Airport while on duty. The Department of Justice granted Ruiz representation (28 C.F.R. § 50.15), and
an Assistant U.S. Attorney has represented him throughout. No other party has ever been named.

## Dkt. 16 — Order Granting Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings (summary of the document)

An 11-page document captioned "ORDER GRANTING DEFENDANT'S MOTION FOR JUDGMENT ON THE PLEADINGS." Pages
1–10 set out the background, the legal standard, and the Court's analysis of why no implied damages
remedy is available against Ruiz on the facts alleged. Page 11 concludes: "For the foregoing reasons,
the Motion is GRANTED. Because amendment would be futile, Plaintiff's claims against Defendant Ruiz
are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE. The Clerk is directed to close this case. IT IS SO ORDERED." No other
document disposing of the case — and no document titled "Judgment" — was ever prepared, signed or
entered.

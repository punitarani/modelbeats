## SCHEDULING AND CASE MANAGEMENT ORDER (Dkt. 30, entered August 14, 2026) [excerpt]

Having considered the parties' Joint Rule 26(f) Report (Dkt. 25), the Court sets the following
schedule. These dates may be modified only by court order on a showing of good cause.

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Last day to amend pleadings or add parties (by motion) | Friday, September 18, 2026 |
| Fact discovery cut-off | Friday, October 23, 2026 |
| Expert discovery cut-off (last day to complete all expert discovery) | Friday, November 13, 2026 |
| Last day to conduct settlement proceedings (L.R. 16-15) | Friday, November 20, 2026 |
| Final Pretrial Conference (10:00 a.m.) | Friday, January 8, 2027 |
| Court (bench) trial (8:30 a.m.); estimated length 4 days | Tuesday, January 26, 2027 |

Expert disclosures. The Court does not set separate dates for expert disclosures; the timing of
initial and rebuttal expert disclosures is governed by Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a)(2)(D).

Dispositive motions. The Court does not set a separate dispositive-motion filing deadline in this
case. No local rule sets a different time for filing a motion for summary judgment.

Pretrial disclosures. The Court does not alter the time for the disclosures required by Fed. R. Civ.
P. 26(a)(3).

Discovery. The discovery cut-off dates are the last dates by which all discovery of the stated type
must be completed. No discovery of any kind may be conducted after the expert discovery cut-off.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

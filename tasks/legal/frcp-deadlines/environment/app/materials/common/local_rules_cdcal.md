# U.S. District Court, Central District of California — Local Civil Rules (Adapted Excerpts)

Adapted excerpt. Restatements in condensed form of selected Local Civil Rules (Chapter I) in effect
during this case. Periods, exceptions and provisos are preserved; wording is paraphrased; rules not
reproduced here are omitted.

---

## F.R.Civ.P. 5 — Service and Filing

L.R. 5-3.1.1 Service of documents not filed. Documents that are not filed with the Court (for
example, discovery requests and responses) must be served in a manner permitted by F.R.Civ.P. 5(b).

L.R. 5-3.2.1 Service of filed documents by electronic means. Upon the electronic filing of a
document, a Notice of Electronic Filing (NEF) is automatically generated by the CM/ECF System and
sent by e-mail to all attorneys in the case who are registered CM/ECF users. Service by NEF
constitutes service under F.R.Civ.P. 5(b)(2)(E) on each person to whom the NEF is sent. A party
that is not a registered user must be served by another method permitted by F.R.Civ.P. 5(b).

L.R. 5-3.3 Certificate of service. A certificate of service is required for every document served
by a method other than the NEF, and must state the date and manner of service.

L.R. 5-4.3.4 Filing date. A document filed electronically is deemed filed on the date and time
stated on the NEF.

## F.R.Civ.P. 6 — Computing Time; Motion Papers

L.R. 6-1 Notice and service of motion. Unless otherwise provided by rule or order, no oral motions
are recognized and every motion must be presented by written notice of motion. The notice of motion
must be filed with the Clerk not later than twenty-eight (28) days before the date set for hearing
and served on each party electronically or, if a party is excepted from electronic filing, by mail
or personal service. If mailed, the notice must be served not later than thirty-one (31) days before
the Motion Day designated in the notice. If served personally or electronically, it must be served
not later than twenty-eight (28) days before that Motion Day. The Court may order a shorter time.
Unless otherwise ordered, the Clerk places each motion on the Motion Day calendar for the date
designated in the notice.

## F.R.Civ.P. 7 — Motions

L.R. 7-3 Conference of counsel prior to filing of motions. Counsel contemplating the filing of any
motion must first contact opposing counsel to discuss thoroughly the substance of the motion and any
potential resolution. The conference must take place at least seven (7) days before the motion is
filed, except as otherwise provided in the Local Rules.

L.R. 7-4 Motions. The Court may decline to consider a motion unless it meets the requirements of
L.R. 7-3 through 7-8.

L.R. 7-5 Moving papers. There must be served and filed with the notice of motion (a) a brief but
complete memorandum in support and the points and authorities on which the moving party will rely,
and (b) the evidence on which the moving party will rely.

L.R. 7-6 through 7-8. (Evidence on motions; form of declarations; presence of declarants.) Omitted.

L.R. 7-9 Opposing papers. Each opposing party must, not later than ten (10) days after service of
the motion in the instance of a new trial motion, and not later than twenty-one (21) days before the
date designated for the hearing of the motion in all other instances, serve upon all other parties
and file with the Clerk either (a) the evidence on which the opposing party will rely in opposition
and a brief but complete memorandum stating all the reasons in opposition and the points and
authorities on which it will rely, or (b) a written statement that it will not oppose the motion.

L.R. 7-10 Reply papers. A moving party may, not later than fourteen (14) days before the date
designated for the hearing of the motion, serve and file a reply memorandum, and declarations or
other rebuttal evidence. Absent prior written order of the Court, the opposing party may not file a
response to the reply.

L.R. 7-11 Continuance of hearing date. Unless the order for continuance specifies otherwise, the
entry of an order continuing the hearing of a motion automatically extends the time for filing and
serving opposing papers and reply papers to twenty-one (21) days and fourteen (14) days,
respectively, preceding the new hearing date. A stipulation to continue must state the date the
opposition and reply papers are due.

L.R. 7-12 Failure to file required documents. The Court may decline to consider any memorandum or
other document not filed within the deadline set by order or local rule. Failure to file a required
document, or the failure to file it within the deadline, may be deemed consent to the granting or
denial of the motion.

L.R. 7-13 Sanctions for late filing. A party filing any document in support of, or in opposition
to, a motion after the time for filing has expired is subject to sanctions.

L.R. 7-15 Oral argument — waiver. Counsel may, with the Court's consent, waive oral argument. The
Court may dispense with oral argument on any motion except where an oral hearing is required by
statute, the F.R.Civ.P., or these Local Rules.

L.R. 7-18 Motion for reconsideration. A motion for reconsideration of an order on any motion or
application may be made only on specified grounds and, absent good cause shown, must be filed no
later than fourteen (14) days after entry of the order that is the subject of the motion.

## F.R.Civ.P. 8 — Pleadings

L.R. 8-3 Response to initial complaint. A stipulation extending the time to respond to the initial
complaint must be filed with the Clerk. If the stipulation, together with any prior stipulations,
does not extend the time for more than a cumulative total of thirty (30) days from the date the
response initially would have been due, the stipulation need not be approved by the judge. The
stipulation must state when the complaint was served, when a response currently is due, and when it
will be due following the filing of the stipulation.

## F.R.Civ.P. 16 and 26 — Pretrial

L.R. 16-2 Meeting of counsel before final pretrial conference. At least forty (40) days before the
date set for the Final Pretrial Conference, lead trial counsel must meet in person to discuss
settlement, exhibits, witnesses and the other matters listed in L.R. 16-2.1 through 16-2.9.

L.R. 16-3 Disclosure of graphic and illustrative material. Graphic or illustrative material not
already disclosed must be disclosed at least eleven (11) days before trial.

L.R. 26-1 Conference of parties; report. The parties' Rule 26(f) report must address, among other
things, a proposed discovery cut-off date, expert-discovery dates, and a proposed trial date.

## F.R.Civ.P. 37 — Discovery Motions

L.R. 37-1 Pre-filing conference of counsel. Before filing any discovery motion, counsel must confer
in a good-faith effort to eliminate the necessity for hearing the motion; the conference must take
place within ten (10) days after the moving party serves a letter requesting it.

L.R. 37-2 Joint stipulation. A discovery motion must be presented in a single joint stipulation.

## F.R.Civ.P. 54 — Costs and Fees

L.R. 54-2 Application to tax costs. A party entitled to costs must file an Application to the Clerk
to Tax Costs within fourteen (14) days after the entry of judgment.

L.R. 54-7 Filing date for requests for attorneys' fees. Any motion or application for attorneys'
fees must be served and filed within fourteen (14) days after the entry of judgment or other final
order, unless otherwise ordered by the Court. Such motions and their disposition are governed by
L.R. 7-3 et seq.

## F.R.Civ.P. 78 — Motion Days

L.R. 78-1 Motion days. Judges hold law and motion calendars on various days of the week and times of
day; consult each judge's procedures.

# General Orders and Notices — U.S. District Court, Central District of California

---

## A. GENERAL ORDER No. 26-19 (entered October 30, 2026) — CM/ECF Scheduled Maintenance

CM/ECF will be unavailable for scheduled maintenance from 9:00 p.m. on Saturday, November 7, 2026
until 3:00 a.m. on Sunday, November 8, 2026. The Clerk's Office intake windows are closed on weekends
in any event. This maintenance window does not render the Clerk's Office inaccessible on any day on
which a filing deadline falls.

---

## B. GENERAL ORDER No. 27-04 (entered March 18, 2027) — Emergency Closure

A severe atmospheric-river storm caused flooding and a total power failure at the First Street
Courthouse and at the Court's primary data center. As a result, the Clerk's Office — including the
public intake windows, the night depository, and the CM/ECF electronic filing system — was
inaccessible for the entire day on Wednesday, March 17, 2027.

IT IS ORDERED that the Clerk's Office is declared inaccessible on March 17, 2027 for purposes of
Fed. R. Civ. P. 6(a)(3) and Fed. R. App. P. 26(a)(3). The Clerk's Office and CM/ECF resumed normal
operation at 12:01 a.m. on Thursday, March 18, 2027. The Court does not otherwise order a different
computation of time, and this Order does not continue any hearing.

---

No other general order affecting the accessibility of the Clerk's Office was entered in 2026, 2027
or 2028.

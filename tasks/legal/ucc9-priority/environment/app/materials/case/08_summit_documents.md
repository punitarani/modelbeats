## EXHIBIT F-1: ACCOUNTS PURCHASE AGREEMENT (excerpts)

**ACCOUNTS PURCHASE AGREEMENT** dated November 3, 2025 between
KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC. ("Seller") and SUMMIT RECEIVABLES FUNDING, LLC ("Buyer").

**1. Sale.** Seller sells, assigns, and transfers to Buyer, and Buyer purchases, all of Seller's
right, title, and interest in the accounts listed on Schedule A (the "Purchased Accounts") and
all proceeds of them. The parties intend a true sale and not a loan.

**2. Purchase Price.** The purchase price is 85% of the face amount of the Purchased Accounts,
$201,025.00 in total, payable on the date of this Agreement.

**3. Credit Risk; No Recourse.** Buyer assumes the risk of nonpayment of the Purchased Accounts
due to the insolvency or financial inability of an account debtor. Seller has no obligation to
repurchase any Purchased Account or to pay any shortfall, except for Seller's indemnity for its
own breach of the warranties in Section 5 (no such breach is asserted).

**4. No Other Interest.** This Agreement transfers only the Purchased Accounts. Buyer acquires no
interest in, and Seller grants no security interest in, any other account or property of
Seller.

**5. Warranties.** Seller warrants that each Purchased Account is a bona fide obligation for
goods actually sold and delivered, is not subject to any defense or set-off, and is owned by
Seller.

**6. Collection.** Buyer may notify account debtors to pay Buyer directly and may collect the
Purchased Accounts in its own name.

**7. Filing.** Seller authorizes Buyer to file a financing statement describing "all accounts" of
Seller.

## EXHIBIT F-4: LETTER FROM SUMMIT TO THE AGENT

SUMMIT RECEIVABLES FUNDING, LLC, September 26, 2026

Re: Kestrel - Summit Statement of Position

Summit purchased the Purchased Accounts outright on November 3, 2025, paid $201,025.00 for them,
and notified the account debtor. Under UCC § 9-318(a), Kestrel retained no interest in them.

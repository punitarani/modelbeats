## EXHIBIT I-1: LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpts)

**LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated as of September 1, 2020 between KESTREL PRECISION
COMPONENTS, INC. ("Borrower") and IRONWOOD CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. ("Lender").

**1.1 Term Loan.** Lender shall make a term loan to Borrower in a single advance on the date of
this Agreement in the principal amount of $1,500,000 (the "**Ironwood Term Debt**"). Amounts
repaid may not be reborrowed.

**3.1 Grant of Security Interest.** To secure the Ironwood Term Debt and all other obligations
of Borrower to Lender, Borrower grants to Lender a security interest in the following, whether
now owned or hereafter acquired (the "**Collateral**"): (a) all Equipment, including all
machinery, machine tools, tooling, and accessions; (b) the deposit accounts maintained with
Northbridge Bank, N.A. (no. ending 4471) and with Cobalt Savings Bank (no. ending 0932), and
every other deposit account of Borrower other than payroll accounts; and (c) all proceeds of the
foregoing. The Collateral does not include Inventory, Accounts, or General Intangibles.

**3.2 Control Agreements.** Borrower shall cause each bank at which a deposit account included in
the Collateral is maintained to enter into a deposit account control agreement in form
satisfactory to Lender.

**3.4 Filing.** Borrower authorizes Lender to file financing statements and continuation
statements describing the Collateral.

**6.7 Negative Pledge.** Borrower shall not grant any Lien on the Collateral other than (a) Liens
in favor of Lender, (b) purchase-money Liens on Equipment securing not more than $1,500,000 in
the aggregate, and (c) Liens in favor of a senior revolving lender on Inventory and Accounts.

**11.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and the other Loan Documents constitute the entire
agreement of the parties with respect to their subject matter.

## EXHIBIT I-2: DEPOSIT ACCOUNT CONTROL AGREEMENT (Northbridge account)

**DEPOSIT ACCOUNT CONTROL AGREEMENT** dated September 15, 2020 among KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS,
INC. ("Company"), IRONWOOD CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. ("Secured Party"), and NORTHBRIDGE BANK, N.A.
("Depositary").

1. **The Account.** Depositary maintains deposit account no. ending 4471 in the name of Company
   (the "Account"). Company is and shall remain Depositary's sole customer with respect to the
   Account.

2. **Control.** Depositary agrees to comply with instructions originated by Secured Party
   directing disposition of the funds in the Account without further consent by Company.
   Until Depositary receives a notice of exclusive control from Secured Party, Depositary may
   also follow Company's instructions.

3. **Depositary's Rights.** Except for Depositary's agreement in Section 2, nothing in this
   Agreement limits, waives, or subordinates any right of recoupment or set-off, or any lien or
   security interest, that Depositary now has or may later acquire with respect to the Account.

4. **Statements.** Depositary shall send copies of all Account statements to Secured Party.

5. **Termination.** This Agreement terminates only upon Secured Party's written notice of
   termination. Depositary may resign on 30 days' notice; no such resignation has occurred.

6. **Governing Law.** Franklin law governs this Agreement and the Account, and Franklin is the
   Depositary's jurisdiction for purposes of the UCC.

Signed: Kestrel Precision Components, Inc.; Ironwood Capital Partners, L.P.; Northbridge Bank,
N.A. (Treasury Services).

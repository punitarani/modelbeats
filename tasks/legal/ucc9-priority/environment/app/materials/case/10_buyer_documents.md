## EXHIBIT B-1: GANTRY PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**GOODS PURCHASE AGREEMENT** dated July 29, 2026 between KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC.
("Seller") and GANTRY FREIGHT LINES, INC. ("Buyer").

**Recital.** Buyer is a motor carrier that has provided freight services to Seller. Seller owes
Buyer $186,000.00 on the freight invoices listed on Schedule 1, all of which are more than 60
days past due.

**1. Sale.** Seller sells to Buyer, and Buyer buys, 450 Kestrel KV-150 valve assemblies (the
"Goods"), which Buyer intends to resell through a broker.

**2. Purchase Price; Payment.** The purchase price is $135,000.00. Buyer shall pay the purchase
price by issuing to Seller, on the date of this Agreement, a credit memorandum in that amount,
applied against the freight invoices listed on Schedule 1, which shall thereupon be reduced by
$135,000.00. No cash shall be paid.

**3. Title.** Title to the Goods passes to Buyer upon execution of this Agreement and Seller's
tagging of the Goods for Buyer at Seller's warehouse.

**4. Pickup.** Buyer shall arrange pickup of the Goods by its own trucks within 30 days.

**5. As-Is.** The Goods are sold as is. Seller's standard Terms and Conditions do not apply.

Schedule 1: Gantry invoices G-25-3310 through G-26-0412 (14 invoices), aggregate $186,000.00.

Executed July 29, 2026. Credit memorandum CM-26-0729 for $135,000.00 issued by Gantry the same
day. Kestrel Warehouse Tag Log, July 29, 2026, 4:10 p.m.: 450 KV-150 assemblies tagged
"GANTRY 7/29." Pickup had not occurred when Kestrel ceased operations on August 3, 2026.

**Gantry letter to the Agent (September 24, 2026).** Gantry bought the KV-150 assemblies in good
faith, paid the full price, and has owned them since July 29. Gantry claims all Net Proceeds of
LOT-GANTRY.
/s/ Ray Kowalski, Controller, Gantry Freight Lines, Inc.

## EXHIBIT N-1: LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpts)

**LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated as of May 17, 2021 between KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS,
INC. ("Borrower") and NORTHBRIDGE BANK, N.A. ("Bank").

### ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

"**Borrowing Base**" means, at any time, the sum of (a) 85% of Eligible Accounts plus (b) the
lesser of 50% of Eligible Inventory and $2,000,000, minus Reserves.

"**Collateral**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Eligible Accounts**" means Accounts arising from the sale of Inventory in the ordinary
course that are not more than 90 days past invoice date and that satisfy the criteria in
Section 2.6, excluding Accounts that Borrower has sold or agreed to sell.

"**Eligible Inventory**" means finished goods Inventory owned by Borrower, located at the Plant,
and not subject to any Lien other than Bank's Lien.

"**Excluded Accounts**" means deposit accounts used exclusively for payroll, payroll taxes, or
employee benefits and identified as such on Schedule 5.9.

"**Excluded Assets**" means (a) any Equipment that is subject to a Lien permitted under Section
7.3(c) securing Permitted Purchase Money Debt; (b) any lease, license, or other contract to the
extent the grant of a security interest in it is prohibited by its terms and the prohibition
is enforceable under the UCC (including Sections 9-406 through 9-409); and (c) Excluded
Accounts.

"**Lien**" means any mortgage, pledge, security interest, encumbrance, lien, or charge of any
kind, including any judgment or execution lien.

"**Permitted Purchase Money Debt**" means Indebtedness incurred to finance the purchase price of
Equipment, secured only by the Equipment so financed, in an aggregate principal amount not
exceeding $750,000 outstanding at any time.

"**Plant**" means Borrower's facility at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Riverton, Franklin.

"**Revolving Commitment**" means $6,000,000.

"**UCC**" means the Uniform Commercial Code as in effect in the State of Franklin.

### ARTICLE 2. LOANS; SECURITY INTEREST

**2.1 Grant of Security Interest.** To secure the payment and performance of all Obligations,
Borrower grants to Bank a continuing security interest in all of Borrower's right, title, and
interest in, to, and under the following, whether now owned or hereafter acquired or arising
and wherever located (collectively, the "**Collateral**"): (a) all Accounts; (b) all Inventory;
(c) all Equipment; (d) all General Intangibles; (e) all Deposit Accounts; (f) all Chattel
Paper, Documents, and Instruments; (g) all Investment Property; (h) all Commercial Tort Claims
listed on Schedule 2.1; (i) all Supporting Obligations; (j) all books and records relating to
any of the foregoing; and (k) all Proceeds and products of any of the foregoing; **provided**,
that the Collateral shall not include any Excluded Assets.

**2.2 Term Loan.** Bank shall make a single term loan to Borrower on the Closing Date in the
amount of $4,000,000.

**2.3 Revolving Loans.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Bank shall make revolving loans
to Borrower from time to time until the Maturity Date in an aggregate outstanding amount not
exceeding the lesser of the Revolving Commitment and the Borrowing Base.

**2.4 Authorization to File.** Borrower authorizes Bank to file financing statements describing
the Collateral as "all assets" or words of similar import. Bank has filed, or will file on or
about the Closing Date, a financing statement with the Franklin Secretary of State.

**2.5 Deposit Accounts.** Borrower's operating account no. ending 4471 is maintained with Bank.
Borrower shall not open any deposit account other than with Bank, except the accounts listed
on Schedule 5.9, without Bank's consent.

### ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS

**5.9 Deposit Accounts.** Schedule 5.9 lists all of Borrower's deposit accounts.

*Schedule 5.9:* (1) Northbridge Bank, N.A., operating account no. ending 4471; (2) Cobalt Savings
Bank, money-market reserve account no. ending 0932; (3) Cobalt Savings Bank, payroll account
no. ending 0517 (Excluded Account).

**5.12 Existing Liens.** Schedule 5.12 lists all Liens existing on the Closing Date.

*Schedule 5.12:* Ironwood Capital Partners, L.P.: liens on Equipment and on deposit accounts
nos. ending 4471 and 0932, securing the Ironwood Term Debt (original principal $1,500,000).
Deposit account control agreements dated September 15, 2020 in favor of Ironwood are in place
for both accounts.

### ARTICLE 6. AFFIRMATIVE COVENANTS

**6.2 Reporting.** Borrower shall deliver a Borrowing Base Certificate quarterly and annual
audited financial statements within 120 days after fiscal year end.

**6.11 Chattel Paper.** Borrower shall mark conspicuously each item of Chattel Paper with a legend
stating that it has been assigned as collateral to Bank.

### ARTICLE 7. NEGATIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Indebtedness.** Borrower shall not incur Indebtedness other than (a) the Obligations,
(b) the Ironwood Term Debt, (c) Permitted Purchase Money Debt, and (d) trade payables in the
ordinary course.

**7.3 Permitted Liens.** Borrower shall not create or permit any Lien on any of its property
other than: (a) Liens in favor of Bank; (b) Liens in favor of Ironwood securing the Ironwood
Term Debt on Equipment and on the deposit accounts listed in Schedule 5.12; (c) Liens securing
Permitted Purchase Money Debt; (d) Liens of Calder Hydraulics, Inc. on Inventory supplied by
it and the proceeds thereof; (e) Liens for taxes not yet due; and (f) judgment Liens not
constituting an Event of Default under Section 8.1(g).

**7.5 Dispositions.** Borrower shall not sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of any of its
property except (a) sales of Inventory in the ordinary course of business, (b) dispositions of
obsolete Equipment not exceeding $100,000 per year, and (c) with Bank's prior written consent.
Without limiting the foregoing, Borrower shall not sell, factor, or otherwise transfer any
Account without Bank's prior written consent.

### ARTICLE 8. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**8.1** Each of the following is an Event of Default: (a) failure to pay any Obligation when
due; ... (g) entry of one or more judgments against Borrower aggregating more than $250,000
that remain unsatisfied for 30 days; ... (k) cessation of Borrower's business operations.

### ARTICLE 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.4 Amendments.** No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by
Borrower and Bank.

**10.9 Intercreditor Matters.** Nothing in this Agreement constitutes a subordination by Bank of
any of its Liens to any other Person.

## EXHIBIT N-2: AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpt)

Dated as of June 30, 2022.

1. **Benchmark Replacement.** Each reference in the Agreement to "LIBOR" is replaced by
   "Term SOFR," and the Applicable Margin is increased by 0.11448% (the credit spread
   adjustment).
2. **Reporting.** Section 6.2(b) is amended to require monthly (rather than quarterly) Borrowing
   Base Certificates.
3. **No Other Changes.** Except as expressly amended, the Agreement remains in full force.

---

## EXHIBIT N-3: AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpt)

Dated as of March 15, 2023.

1. **Excluded Assets.** Clause (a) of the definition of "Excluded Assets" in Article 1 is
   amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

   "(a) any item of Equipment that is subject to a Lien permitted under Section 7.3(c) securing
   Permitted Purchase Money Debt, but only for so long as, and only to the extent that, the
   agreement governing such Permitted Purchase Money Debt prohibits Borrower from granting a
   Lien on such item of Equipment in favor of Bank;"

2. **Borrowing Base.** Clause (b) of the definition of "Borrowing Base" is amended by replacing
   "$2,000,000" with "$2,500,000".

3. **Notices.** Bank's address for notices is changed to 400 Commerce Street, Riverton,
   Franklin.

4. **No Other Changes.** Except as expressly amended, the Agreement remains in full force.

## EXHIBIT N-4: AMENDMENT NO. 3 TO LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpt)

Dated as of May 15, 2025.

1. **Permitted Purchase Money Debt.** The definition of "Permitted Purchase Money Debt" is
   amended by replacing "$750,000" with "$1,500,000".
2. **Financial Covenant.** The minimum Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio for the fiscal quarters
   ending June 30, 2025 and September 30, 2025 is reduced to 1.05 to 1.00.
3. **Fee.** Borrower shall pay Bank an amendment fee of $15,000.
4. **No Other Changes.** Except as expressly amended (including by Amendments No. 1 and No. 2),
   the Agreement remains in full force.

## EXHIBIT N-5: AMENDMENT NO. 4 TO LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpt)

Dated as of January 12, 2026.

1. **Capex Advance.** Bank shall make a single advance to Borrower in the amount of $150,000 (the
   "**Capex Advance**") to finance part of the purchase price of one DMG Mori NLX 2500/700 CNC
   turning center being purchased by Borrower from Brennan Machine Tool Sales, Inc. The Capex
   Advance shall be disbursed by Bank directly to the seller. The Capex Advance is a Loan and an
   Obligation for all purposes of the Agreement and is secured by all of the Collateral.

2. **Repayment of Capex Advance.** Borrower shall repay the Capex Advance in eight equal
   quarterly installments of principal commencing February 1, 2027. Interest on the Capex Advance
   is payable monthly at the rate applicable to Revolving Loans.

3. **Application of Payments.** A new Section 2.9 is added to the Agreement: "**2.9 Application
   of Payments.** Until an Event of Default occurs, all payments and collections received by Bank
   shall be applied first to fees and accrued interest, then to the principal of Revolving Loans,
   then to scheduled installments of the Term Loan. No payment shall be applied to principal of
   the Capex Advance except a scheduled installment under Amendment No. 4."

4. **No Other Changes.** Except as expressly amended (including by Amendments No. 1 through 3),
   the Agreement remains in full force.

As you know, Northbridge holds a first-priority, perfected, blanket security interest in all
assets of Kestrel under our Loan and Security Agreement dated May 17, 2021, perfected by our
financing statement filed May 3, 2021 and timely continued. Our Allowed Claim is $2,500,000.00.

Northbridge's position is as follows:

1. Northbridge is entitled to every Pool, ahead of every other Claimant, until its Allowed Claim
   is paid, except (a) INV-CALDER-1, as to which we acknowledge Calder's purchase-money priority

   Tristate's purchase-money priority for the unpaid balance of the loan that financed Machine B.

2. In EQ-C, Northbridge itself financed $150,000 of the price of Machine C. Our purchase-money
   security interest was perfected by our 2021 financing statement before the machine was even
   ordered, nearly five years before Brennan filed. Northbridge therefore ranks first in EQ-C.

   filed in 2022, more than a year after ours. Pinecrest has never filed against Kestrel, and its
   filing ceased to be effective four months after the merger. Northbridge ranks first.

4. Ironwood's financing statement lapsed in 2025.

Northbridge reserves all rights, including with respect to the proceeds of the Okuma machining
center sold by Tristate, as to which we have separately made demand on Tristate.

Very truly yours,

/s/ Dana Whitcomb

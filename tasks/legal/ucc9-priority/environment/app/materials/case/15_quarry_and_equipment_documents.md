## EXHIBIT Q-3: LETTER FROM QUARRY'S COUNSEL TO THE AGENT

Hallett & Moore LLP, September 25, 2026

Re: Kestrel - Quarry Electrical Contractors, Inc. Statement of Position (Pools EQ-HAAS and
EQ-HURCO)

Quarry's Allowed Claim is $182,000.00. Quarry's execution lien attached to the Haas and the Hurco
on June 18, 2026. Quarry acknowledges that the security interests of Northbridge and Ironwood,
perfected by financing statements naming Kestrel filed before the levy, are senior to Quarry's
lien. Pinecrest, by contrast, has never filed a financing statement naming Kestrel. A security
interest that depends on a filing against a different company is subordinated by the UCC to
interests perfected in the ordinary way, and Pinecrest's filing in any event lost its effect four
months after the merger. Quarry therefore ranks ahead of Pinecrest in both Pools.

/s/ Dana Moore, counsel for Quarry Electrical Contractors, Inc.

EXECUTION VERSION

# LIQUIDATION AND DISTRIBUTION PROTOCOL

**Kestrel Precision Components, Inc.**

This Liquidation and Distribution Protocol (this "**Protocol**") is entered into as of
September 18, 2026 by and among Kestrel Precision Components, Inc., a Franklin corporation
("**Kestrel**"); Northbridge Bank, N.A. ("**Northbridge**"); Ironwood Capital Partners, L.P.
("**Ironwood**"); Tristate Equipment Finance, LLC ("**Tristate**"); Calder Hydraulics, Inc.
("**Calder**"); Summit Receivables Funding, LLC ("**Summit**"); Delmar Industrial Supply Co.
("**Delmar**"); Gantry Freight Lines, Inc. ("**Gantry**"); Meridian Equipment Credit, LLC
("**Meridian**"); Westfield Seal Company ("**Westfield**"); Orrin Steel Supply, Inc. ("**Orrin**");
Brennan Machine Tool Sales, Inc. ("**Brennan**"); Pinecrest Bank ("**Pinecrest**"); Quarry
Electrical Contractors, Inc. ("**Quarry**"); and Hollis Asset Recovery Group, LLC, as liquidating
agent (the "**Agent**").
Each of the foregoing other than Kestrel and the Agent is a "**Claimant**."

## RECITALS

A. Kestrel manufactured hydraulic valve assemblies and distributed hydraulic pumps from its
plant at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Riverton, Franklin (the "**Plant**"). Kestrel ceased
operations on August 3, 2026.

B. Each Claimant asserts a security interest in, a lien on, or ownership of some or all of
the assets located at the Plant, the proceeds of certain accounts, or funds on deposit in
certain deposit accounts maintained in Kestrel's name.

C. To avoid the cost and delay of separate enforcement actions and a bankruptcy filing, the
parties have agreed that the Agent will liquidate the assets described on Schedule 1 free and
clear of all interests, and that the net proceeds will be distributed in accordance with the
parties' respective rights determined as set forth in this Protocol.

D. Tristate repossessed one machining center (the Okuma MU-8000V, serial no. 2203-1187,
"**Machine A**") before the date of this Protocol and has disposed of it at public auction.
Machine A and its proceeds are not Pool Assets and are not governed by this Protocol, except
as provided in Section 5.5.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Allowed Claim**" means, for each Claimant, the amount set forth for it on Schedule 2,
as reduced from time to time under Section 5.5.

1.2 "**Agent Collections**" means amounts collected by the Agent on accounts that are Pool
Assets.

1.3 "**Deposit Balances**" means the collected balances of the deposit accounts identified on
Schedule 1 as transferred to the Escrow Account under Section 3.3.

1.4 "**Escrow Account**" means the segregated escrow account maintained by the Agent at Firstmark
Trust Company for the benefit of the parties.

1.5 "**Gross Sale Proceeds**" means, for a Sale Pool, the aggregate hammer or contract price
received by the Agent for the Pool Assets of that Pool, without deduction.

1.6 "**Net Proceeds**" has the meaning given in Section 4.4.

1.7 "**Pool**" means each group of assets identified on Schedule 1, and "**Pool Assets**" means
the assets in a Pool. A "**Sale Pool**" is a Pool whose Pool Assets are goods sold by the
Agent at the Sale.

1.8 "**Pool Direct Costs**" means out-of-pocket costs of rigging, removal, transport, cleaning,
and lotting that are attributable solely to a particular Sale Pool, as shown in the Agent's
cost ledger.

1.9 "**Remitted Collections**" means (a) amounts collected by Summit on the Purchased Accounts
(as defined in the Accounts Purchase Agreement) and remitted by Summit to the Escrow Account under
Section 3.4, and (b) amounts collected by Meridian on the Purchased Contracts (as defined in the
Chattel Paper Purchase Agreement), together with the price received by Meridian on its resale of
the Purchased Contracts, remitted by Meridian to the Escrow Account under Section 3.4A.

1.10 "**Sale**" means the Agent's auction and negotiated sales of the Sale Pool assets
conducted on September 22, 2026 (the "**Sale Date**").

1.11 "**UCC**" means the Uniform Commercial Code as in effect in the State of Franklin.

1.12 Terms used and not defined in this Protocol that are defined in the UCC have the meanings
given in the UCC.

## ARTICLE 2. APPOINTMENT OF AGENT

2.1 The parties appoint the Agent to take possession of, market, sell, and collect the Pool
Assets on the terms of this Protocol. The Agent acts solely as a stakeholder and makes no
determination of any Claimant's rights.

2.2 The Agent shall maintain books and records of all receipts and costs by Pool and shall
deliver a report of the Sale and collections (the "**Agent's Report**") to all parties.

2.3 The Agent shall carry insurance on the Pool Assets while in its custody. Insurance
premiums, Plant security, utilities, and other costs not attributable solely to a particular
Pool ("**General Costs**") shall be paid by Northbridge, shall not be deducted from any Pool, and
shall not be added to Northbridge's Allowed Claim.

## ARTICLE 3. SALE; COLLECTIONS; DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS

3.1 The Agent shall sell the Sale Pool assets at the Sale free and clear of all security
interests, liens, claims, and ownership interests of the parties, each of whom consents to the
Sale. Each Sale Pool shall be lotted and accounted for separately.

3.2 The Agent shall collect the accounts identified on Schedule 1 as Agent Collections Pools
through September 30, 2026 (the "**Collection Cutoff**").

3.3 Northbridge and Cobalt Savings Bank have each been instructed by all parties holding any
interest in the deposit accounts identified on Schedule 1 to transfer the collected balance of
each such account to the Escrow Account, and each did so on September 21, 2026.

3.4 Summit shall remit to the Escrow Account all amounts it has collected or collects on the
Purchased Accounts through the Collection Cutoff. Remittance is without prejudice to Summit's
contention that it owns the Purchased Accounts.

3.4A Meridian shall remit to the Escrow Account all amounts it has collected on the Purchased
Contracts through the Collection Cutoff and the price it receives on the resale of the Purchased
Contracts, without prejudice to Meridian's contention that it owns the Purchased Contracts.

3.5 No party shall exercise any right of set-off or recoupment against any Pool Asset or any
amount in the Escrow Account.

## ARTICLE 4. AGENT COMPENSATION; NET PROCEEDS

4.1 **Sale Commission.** The Agent shall earn a commission equal to four percent (4%) of the
Gross Sale Proceeds of each Sale Pool.

4.2 **Collection Fee.** The Agent shall earn a collection fee equal to two percent (2%) of
Agent Collections.

4.3 **No Other Compensation.** The Agent shall earn no commission or fee on Deposit Balances or
on Remitted Collections, and no compensation other than as stated in Sections 4.1 and 4.2.

4.4 **Net Proceeds.** "Net Proceeds" means: (a) for a Sale Pool, the Gross Sale Proceeds of that
Pool less (i) the Pool Direct Costs of that Pool and (ii) the Sale Commission on that Pool;
(b) for an Agent Collections Pool, the Agent Collections less the Collection Fee; (c) for a
Deposit Account Pool, the Deposit Balance; and (d) for a Remitted Collections Pool, the
Remitted Collections.

## ARTICLE 5. DETERMINATION OF RIGHTS; DISTRIBUTION

5.1 **Attachment to Net Proceeds.** Every security interest, lien, and ownership interest in
the Pool Assets of a Pool attaches to the Net Proceeds of that Pool with the same validity,
extent, and priority that it had in those Pool Assets immediately before the Sale Date,
determined under the UCC and other applicable law. The Sale shall not improve or impair the
position of any party.

5.2 **Priority Ranking.** The priority of interests in each Pool shall be determined as if the
Net Proceeds were the Pool Assets, applying the UCC, any agreement among Claimants that is
enforceable under UCC § 9-339, and other applicable law. No party shall be entitled to
marshaling.

5.3 **Distribution Sequence.** The Agent shall distribute the Pools in the order in which they
are listed on Schedule 1.

5.4 **Application within a Pool.** The Net Proceeds of each Pool shall be applied to the
interests in that Pool in order of their priority, as follows:

(a) a security interest or lien that secures an obligation shall receive the lesser of the
Net Proceeds then remaining and the holder's Allowed Claim then outstanding;

(b) where an interest in the Pool does not secure an obligation owed to its holder (including
the ownership interest of a person to which Kestrel transferred the Pool Assets before the Sale
Date, the interest of a consignor, and a seller's right to reclaim goods) (a "**Non-Lien
Interest**"), its holder shall receive all Net Proceeds remaining when that interest is reached
in order of priority, and no interest ranking below it shall receive any amount;

(c) any Net Proceeds remaining after the application of subsections (a) and (b) shall be paid
to Kestrel; and

(d) Where an interest in a Pool has a different priority as to different parts of the
obligation it secures, each such part is treated as a separate interest for purposes of this
Section 5.4 and is applied in its own order of priority, up to the amount of that part then
outstanding; the aggregate applied to all parts of a Claimant's interest may not exceed its Allowed
Claim then outstanding.

5.5 **Reduction of Allowed Claims.** Each Claimant's Allowed Claim shall be reduced by every
amount it receives on account of its claim from any source, including (i) prior distributions
under this Protocol and (ii) any amount applied to its claim by Tristate from the proceeds of
Machine A. For the avoidance of doubt, Tristate shall apply the proceeds of Machine A in
accordance with Part 6 of Article 9 of the UCC, and any amount so applied to a Claimant shall
be treated as received by that Claimant before the first distribution under this Protocol.

5.6 **Disputes.** If any party objects to the Agent's proposed distribution of a Pool, the Agent
shall hold the disputed amount pending agreement of the affected parties or a final order of a
court of competent jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE 6. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

6.1 Except as expressly provided in this Protocol, nothing in this Protocol waives, releases,
or modifies any claim, defense, or agreement among any of the parties, including any
subordination agreement, control agreement, or security agreement.

6.2 Nothing in this Protocol constitutes an admission by any party of the validity, extent,
perfection, or priority of any interest asserted by any other party.

6.3 Nothing in this Protocol affects any claim of any party against Kestrel for a deficiency,
or any claim that is not secured by an interest in a Pool.

## ARTICLE 7. STIPULATIONS

7.1 The parties adopt the Joint Stipulation of Facts attached as Exhibit A (the
"**Stipulation**"), which binds every party for purposes of this Protocol.

7.2 Each document attached to or identified in the Stipulation is a true and complete copy of
what it purports to be, and was signed or otherwise authenticated by each person shown as
signing it on the date shown. Where a party's correspondence, memorandum, report, or summary
describes a document, filing, or event differently from the document itself or from the
certified search report, the document itself or the certified search report controls.

## ARTICLE 8. MISCELLANEOUS

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Protocol is governed by the law of the State of Franklin.

8.2 **Conflicts.** In the event of any conflict between this Protocol and the Agent's Report or
any other report or summary, this Protocol controls.

8.3 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Protocol may be executed in counterparts and by
electronic signature.

8.4 **Amendments.** This Protocol may be amended only by a writing signed by all parties.

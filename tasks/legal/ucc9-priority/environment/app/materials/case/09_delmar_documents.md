## EXHIBIT D-4: FRANKLIN REVISED STATUTES, TITLE 23, CHAPTER 14 (EXECUTION) (adapted excerpts)

**§ 23-1401. Lien of execution.** Goods and chattels of a judgment debtor that are levied upon
under a writ of execution are bound by a lien in favor of the judgment creditor from the time
of the levy, and not before. The lien attaches only to property described in the officer's
return of levy in which the judgment debtor has an interest at the time of the levy. Issuance
of a writ does not create a lien.

**§ 23-1402. Levy on bulky property.** When goods cannot be readily removed, the officer may levy
by affixing notice to the goods and serving written notice of the levy on the judgment debtor,
and may leave the goods in the custody of the judgment debtor as keeper. The levy is complete
when both acts have been performed.

**§ 23-1405. Duration of lien.** The lien of execution continues until the judgment is
satisfied, the judgment creditor releases the levy in a signed writing, or the court vacates
the levy. The creditor's forbearance from scheduling a sale does not release the levy.

**§ 23-1409. Relation to other interests.** The priority of an execution lien in relation to a
security interest is determined under Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code.

## EXHIBIT D-5: LETTER FROM DELMAR'S COUNSEL TO THE AGENT

Pruitt & Shaw, September 25, 2026

Re: Kestrel - Delmar Statement of Position

Delmar's execution lien attached to the equipment at the Plant on June 10, 2025. Ironwood's
financing statement lapsed on September 1, 2025 and was not refiled until October 20, 2025, so
Ironwood's security interest is deemed never to have been perfected. Delmar's lien is therefore
senior to Ironwood's in Pool EQ-GEN. Delmar acknowledges that Northbridge's security interest,
perfected in 2021, is senior to Delmar's lien in EQ-GEN. To the extent Delmar's judgment is paid
from the proceeds of the Okuma machining center, Delmar will reduce its claim accordingly.

## EXHIBIT P-1: LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (ASHBY) (excerpts)

**LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated as of August 10, 2022 between ASHBY VALVE WORKS, INC., a
Franklin corporation ("Borrower"), and PINECREST BANK ("Lender").

**1.1 Term Loan.** Lender shall make a term loan to Borrower on the date of this Agreement in the
principal amount of $750,000 (the "Ashby Term Loan").

**3.1 Grant of Security Interest.** To secure the Ashby Term Loan and all other obligations of
Borrower to Lender, now existing or hereafter arising, Borrower grants to Lender a security
interest in all of Borrower's Equipment, whether now owned or hereafter acquired, together with
all accessions to and proceeds of the foregoing (the "Collateral"). "Equipment" has the meaning
given in the Uniform Commercial Code as in effect in Franklin.

**3.3 Filing.** Borrower authorizes Lender to file financing statements describing the
Collateral.

**7.4 Fundamental Changes.** Borrower shall not merge or consolidate with any person without
Lender's prior written consent.

**11.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds Borrower and its successors and assigns.

## EXHIBIT P-4: LETTER FROM PINECREST TO THE AGENT

PINECREST BANK, Commercial Lending, 88 Ridge Avenue, Ashby Falls, Franklin

September 25, 2026

Re: Kestrel Precision Components, Inc. (successor by merger to Ashby Valve Works, Inc.)

Pinecrest's Allowed Claim is $491,750.00, less any amount we receive from Tristate's disposition
of the Okuma machining center, as to which we have made written demand.

Pinecrest's security interest covers all equipment of the debtor, now owned or hereafter
acquired, and has been perfected since we filed on August 15, 2022. When Ashby merged into
Kestrel, Kestrel became bound by our security agreement as successor. Our filing predates
Ironwood's October 2025 filing, Quarry's 2026 levy, and every other interest in equipment except
Northbridge's filing and the purchase-money interests of Tristate and Brennan. Pinecrest therefore
claims priority in every equipment Pool ahead of Ironwood, Delmar, and Quarry, and in EQ-ASHBY
ahead of all Claimants.

/s/ Graham Ellery, Senior Vice President

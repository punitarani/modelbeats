EXHIBIT A TO LIQUIDATION AND DISTRIBUTION PROTOCOL

# JOINT STIPULATION OF FACTS

The parties to the Liquidation and Distribution Protocol dated September 18, 2026 stipulate as
follows for all purposes of the Protocol and of Tristate's application of the proceeds of
Machine A.

## A. Debtor and filings

1. Kestrel is a corporation organized solely under the law of the State of Franklin and has
   been since 2009; its name has not changed. Franklin has enacted Articles 1, 2 and 9 of the
   Uniform Commercial Code in the form of the 2010 Official Text, without any nonuniform
   amendment relevant to this matter. The UCC as enacted in Franklin governs the attachment,
   perfection, effect of perfection or nonperfection, and priority of every security interest
   and lien at issue, including in both deposit accounts (each bank's jurisdiction for UCC
   purposes is Franklin).
2. The Franklin Secretary of State's certified UCC search report dated September 16, 2026
   (Exhibit S-1) accurately shows every financing statement and other UCC record filed against
   Kestrel and the date of each filing. Every record in Record groups 1 through 9 was filed in
   the proper office, was authorized by Kestrel (or, for a continuation statement, filed by the
   secured party of record), correctly names the debtor and secured party, and sufficiently
   indicates the collateral it describes. No record against Kestrel has been filed, amended, or
   terminated other than as shown. The records listed under "similar names" were filed against
   other entities and are not records against Kestrel.
3. The certified search report for Ashby Valve Works, Inc. (Exhibit P-3) accurately shows every
   record filed against Ashby. The Pinecrest financing statement shown in it was filed in the
   proper office on the date shown, was authorized by Ashby, correctly names Ashby as debtor and
   Pinecrest as secured party, and sufficiently indicates the collateral it describes. A search
   of the Franklin records under Kestrel's correct name using the filing office's standard search
   logic does not disclose that financing statement. Pinecrest has never filed a financing
   statement naming Kestrel as debtor.
4. No collateral at issue is subject to a certificate-of-title statute. Other than Meridian's
   possession of the Purchased Contracts, no party claims perfection by possession of any
   collateral at issue.

## B. Security agreements, value, and rights in collateral

5. Each of the following was authenticated on the date shown by the debtor party to it, and
   each is in effect: the Ironwood Loan and Security Agreement (Sept. 1, 2020); the Ironwood
   deposit account control agreements (Sept. 15, 2020); the Calder Credit and Security
   Agreement (May 10, 2021); the Northbridge Loan and Security Agreement (May 17, 2021) and
   Amendments No. 1 through 4; the Pinecrest Loan and Security Agreement with Ashby (Aug. 10,
   2022); the Westfield Consignment Agreement (Jan. 15, 2025); the Tristate Working Capital
   Note (Mar. 3, 2025); the Tristate Loan A Security Agreement (May 28, 2025); the Tristate
   Loan B Security Agreement (July 30, 2025); the Summit Accounts Purchase Agreement (Nov. 3,
   2025); the Brennan Purchase Agreement and Retail Installment Contract (Jan. 15, 2026); and
   the Meridian Chattel Paper Purchase Agreement (June 12, 2026).
6. Value was given as follows. Ironwood funded its term loan in a single advance on
   September 1, 2020 and made no later advances. Northbridge made its first advance on May 17,
   2021, and every later advance by Northbridge was made pursuant to commitment under the
   Northbridge LSA, which Northbridge entered into on May 17, 2021. Pinecrest advanced the Ashby
   Term Loan to Ashby on August 10, 2022. Tristate advanced the Working Capital Note to Kestrel
   on March 3, 2025 for general working capital; none of it was used to acquire any collateral.
   Tristate paid the full amount of Loan A to the seller of Machine A on May 28, 2025, and the
   full amount of Loan B ($430,000.00) to the seller of Machine B on July 30, 2025, in each case
   in payment of the machine's price. Calder gave value by selling each shipment on credit.
   Summit paid the purchase price for the Purchased Accounts on November 3, 2025. Section H
   describes value given for Machine C.
7. Kestrel acquired rights in Machine A on May 28, 2025, in Machine B on July 30, 2025, in
   Machine C on January 15, 2026, in the Haas UMC-750 (Pool EQ-HAAS) on February 17, 2026, and in
   the Hurco VM20i (Pool EQ-HURCO) on May 12, 2026 (in each case the date of the purchase
   contract). Kestrel acquired rights in the EQ-ASHBY equipment on December 1, 2025 (Section C).
   Kestrel acquired rights in each shipment of Calder goods no later than the date it received
   that shipment.
8. Tristate's Loan A and Loan B and Kestrel's installment obligation to Brennan each constitute
   "Permitted Purchase Money Debt" under the Northbridge LSA, and the liens of Tristate and
   Brennan securing them are liens permitted under Section 7.3(c) of the Northbridge LSA.
9. Kestrel has not been a party to any transfer in bulk, and no claimant asserts any interest
   under any statute other than the UCC and the Franklin execution statute (Exhibit D-4).

## C. The Ashby merger

10. Ashby Valve Works, Inc. ("Ashby") was a corporation organized solely under Franklin law.
    Effective December 1, 2025, Ashby merged with and into Kestrel under the Franklin Business
    Corporation Act (Exhibit P-2). Kestrel was the surviving corporation and kept its name, and
    Ashby's separate existence ceased. By operation of that Act, Kestrel succeeded to all of
    Ashby's assets and became liable for all of Ashby's obligations, including the Ashby Term
    Loan.
11. Every item in Pool EQ-ASHBY was owned by Ashby on December 1, 2025 and was moved to the Plant
    in January 2026. Pinecrest has not released its security interest in any property.

## D. Specific goods and accounts

12. Machines A, B and C, the Haas UMC-750, the Hurco VM20i, and every item in EQ-ASHBY and EQ-GEN
    are equipment. The Calder pumps and the KV-series valve assemblies are inventory, as is the
    bar stock in INV-ORRIN.
13. All Pool Assets in INV-CALDER-1 were received by Kestrel in the Calder shipment received
    March 2, 2026; all Pool Assets in INV-CALDER-2 were received in the Calder shipment
    received July 15, 2026. Kestrel received no other Calder goods in 2026.
14. Every account in AR-CALDER arose from Kestrel's sale on credit, between March 9 and July 31,
    2026, of a Calder pump received in the March 2, 2026 shipment. No account in AR-CALDER is a
    Purchased Account.
15. Every item of equipment in EQ-GEN was owned by Kestrel and located at the Plant on and before
    June 10, 2025, and appears on the Sheriff's Return of Levy in the Delmar action (Exhibit
    D-3). No other Pool Asset appears on that return. The Haas UMC-750 and the Hurco VM20i appear
    on the Sheriff's Return of Levy in the Quarry action (Exhibit Q-2), and no other item of
    Kestrel's property appears on it.
16. Gantry had, from the date title passed to it under its contract, a right to recover its
    goods from Kestrel under Article 2 of the UCC, and acted in good faith. Gantry had no
    knowledge of any default by Kestrel under any loan agreement before August 3, 2026. The
    goods were never delivered to Gantry or its carrier.

## E. Deposit accounts

17. Account no. ending 4471 has been maintained at Northbridge since 2016, and account no. ending
    0932 has been maintained at Cobalt Savings Bank since 2018, each in Kestrel's name. Kestrel
    has at all times been the bank's only customer on each account.
18. The only persons asserting any interest in either account (or in its Deposit Balance) are
    Northbridge and Ironwood. Cobalt Savings Bank asserts no interest. No set-off or recoupment
    has been exercised against either account.

## F. Machine A

19. Tristate repossessed Machine A on August 10, 2026 without breach of the peace, and sold it
    at a commercially reasonable public auction on September 15, 2026 to an unrelated bidder for
    $612,000.00 in cash. No party challenges the notification of the disposition or any other
    aspect of its conduct.
20. The expenses shown in Exhibit T-7 were actually incurred by Tristate in the amounts shown
    and are reasonable in amount.
21. As of the date of this Stipulation, Tristate holds all of the cash proceeds of Machine A
    and has not distributed any of them. The only communications Tristate has received from any
    person concerning the proceeds of Machine A are those in Exhibit T-8, each received on the
    date stated there. Tristate has not requested proof of any interest from any person.
22. The outstanding balance of Tristate Loan A, as of the auction date and at all later times
    relevant, is $468,300.00, exclusive of any attorney's fees or legal expenses.

## G. Machine B and Tristate's loans

23. The Working Capital Note was unsecured when made. Under Section 2.2 of the Loan B Security
    Agreement, Machine B secures both Loan B and the Working Capital Note. On July 30, 2025
    Tristate opened account no. TEF-2507, under which it billed Loan B and the Working Capital
    Note together. Loan A was billed and paid separately under account no. TEF-2505, and every
    payment on Loan A referenced that account.
24. Interest on Loan B and on the Working Capital Note was billed separately each month and was
    paid in full through July 31, 2026. The twelve payments shown in Exhibit T-9 were principal
    payments. Kestrel made each of them by ACH transfer referencing only account no. TEF-2507,
    and did not otherwise indicate, at or before the time of any payment, how it was to be
    applied as between Loan B and the Working Capital Note. No agreement between Kestrel and
    Tristate provides a method for applying payments as between Loan B and the Working Capital
    Note. Tristate made the application shown in its ledger after receiving each payment.
25. Before July 30, 2025, no principal had been paid on the Working Capital Note. Kestrel made no
    payment on account no. TEF-2507 other than those shown in Exhibit T-9.

## H. Machine C

26. Kestrel bought Machine C from Brennan for $300,000.00 under the Brennan Purchase Agreement and
    Retail Installment Contract dated January 15, 2026 (Exhibit BR-1). The price was paid as
    follows: $150,000.00 by the Northbridge Capex Advance, which Northbridge wired directly to
    Brennan on January 15, 2026; $40,000.00 in cash by Kestrel; and $110,000.00 by Kestrel's
    installment obligation to Brennan, secured by Machine C. Brennan delivered Machine C to the
    Plant on January 22, 2026.
27. No principal of the Capex Advance has been repaid. The unpaid balance of Kestrel's
    installment obligation to Brennan is $95,000.00.

## I. Judgment liens

28. Delmar's judgment against Kestrel was entered May 20, 2025. Delmar's execution lien on the
    property described in the Sheriff's Return (Exhibit D-3) has not been released, satisfied,
    or vacated, and remains in effect.
29. Quarry's judgment against Kestrel was entered April 14, 2026. The Sheriff levied on the Haas
    UMC-750 and the Hurco VM20i on June 18, 2026 under a writ of execution issued in that action,
    in the manner described in § 23-1402 of the Franklin execution statute (Exhibit Q-2). Quarry's
    execution lien has not been released, satisfied, or vacated, and remains in effect.

## J. Chattel paper

30. Each Purchased Contract is an installment sale contract under which a Kestrel customer
    bought a Kestrel KTB-40 hydraulic test bench from Kestrel's inventory and granted Kestrel a
    security interest in the bench. Each is tangible chattel paper, and no other record evidences
    any of them.
31. Meridian is a commercial finance company that purchases equipment installment contracts in
    the regular course of its business. On June 12, 2026 Meridian bought the five Purchased
    Contracts from Kestrel, paid the purchase price in cash that day, and took possession of the
    original of each contract that day. Meridian acted in good faith. Meridian had seen
    Northbridge's financing statement in a search; it had not seen the Northbridge LSA and had no
    knowledge of its terms. Meridian filed no financing statement. Each Purchased Contract bore,
    from its date of execution, the legend shown in Exhibit M-2.

## K. Accounts purchased by Summit

32. Summit bought the Purchased Accounts on November 3, 2025 under the Accounts Purchase
    Agreement. Northbridge did not consent to the sale and did not subordinate its security
    interest in any account to Summit.

## L. Consigned seal kits

33. Westfield delivered seal kits to Kestrel from time to time under the Westfield Consignment
    Agreement (Exhibit W-1) for sale by Kestrel; each delivery had an aggregate value of not less
    than $18,000.00 at the time of delivery, and none of the goods were consumer goods. Kestrel
    sold the seal kits to its distributors under the Kestrel name, as part of its own product
    catalog. Kestrel is not an auctioneer. No creditor of Kestrel other than Westfield knew of
    the consignment arrangement before August 3, 2026, and Kestrel was not generally known by
    its creditors to be engaged in selling the goods of others. Westfield never filed a
    financing statement against Kestrel and sent no notification to any person. All seal kits
    in INV-CONSIGNED were delivered by Westfield between January 2025 and June 2026 and remained
    unsold at the Plant.

## M. Orrin bar stock

34. Orrin sold the bar stock in INV-ORRIN to Kestrel on 30-day credit terms, and Kestrel
    received it at the Plant on July 27, 2026. Kestrel was insolvent at all times from June 1,
    2026. Kestrel received Orrin's written demand for reclamation (Exhibit O-2) on August 4,
    2026. The bar stock remained identifiable and unsold at the Plant until the Sale. Kestrel made
    no written misrepresentation of solvency to Orrin.

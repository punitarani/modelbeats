## EXHIBIT W-1: CONSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (excerpts)

**CONSIGNMENT AGREEMENT** dated January 15, 2025 between WESTFIELD SEAL COMPANY ("Consignor") and
KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC. ("Consignee").

**1. Consignment.** Consignor will deliver FKM seal kits to Consignee on consignment for sale by
Consignee to its customers. Title remains in Consignor until Consignee sells the goods.

**2. Remittance.** Consignee shall remit to Consignor the consignment price of each kit within 15
days after selling it. Consignee has no obligation to pay for unsold goods and may return them.

**4. Branding.** Consignee may sell the kits under its own name and catalog numbers.

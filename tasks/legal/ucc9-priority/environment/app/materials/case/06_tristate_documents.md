## EXHIBIT T-1: SECURITY AGREEMENT (LOAN A) (excerpts)

**SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated May 28, 2025 between KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC. ("Debtor")
and TRISTATE EQUIPMENT FINANCE, LLC ("Secured Party"). Form TEF-SA (rev. 2019).

**1. Loan.** Secured Party has agreed to lend Debtor $520,000 ("Loan A"), evidenced by Debtor's
promissory note of even date (the "Note"), the proceeds of which Secured Party will disburse
directly to Midwest Machine Tool Sales, Inc. in payment of the purchase price of the Collateral.

**2.1 Grant.** Debtor grants Secured Party a security interest in one (1) Okuma MU-8000V 5-axis
vertical machining center, serial no. 2203-1187, together with all attachments, accessions,
tooling, and software delivered with it, and all proceeds thereof (the "Collateral").

**2.2 Secured Obligations.** This Agreement secures only the obligations of Debtor under the Note
and this Agreement. It does not secure any other present or future indebtedness of Debtor to
Secured Party.

**4.1 Location.** Debtor shall keep the Collateral at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Riverton,
Franklin.

**6.2 Negative Pledge.** Debtor shall not create, incur, or permit to exist any lien, security
interest, or other encumbrance on the Collateral other than the security interest granted to
Secured Party. Any attempt to do so is an Event of Default.

**8.1 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Secured Party may exercise all rights of a secured
party under the UCC, including taking possession of and disposing of the Collateral.

**8.3 Expenses.** Debtor shall reimburse Secured Party on demand for all reasonable costs of
retaking, holding, preparing for sale, and selling the Collateral, including rigging,
transportation, storage, and auctioneer's commissions, and such costs shall be secured hereby.

**12.7 Costs of Agreement.** Each party shall bear its own attorneys' fees and legal expenses in
connection with the negotiation, execution, administration, and enforcement of this Agreement
and the Note.

**12.9 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and the Note are the entire agreement of the parties
regarding Loan A and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

## EXHIBIT T-2: SECURITY AGREEMENT (LOAN B) (excerpts)

**SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated July 30, 2025 between KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC. ("Debtor")
and TRISTATE EQUIPMENT FINANCE, LLC ("Secured Party"). Form TEF-SA (rev. 2025).

**1. Loan.** Secured Party has agreed to lend Debtor $430,000 ("Loan B"), evidenced by Debtor's
promissory note of even date, the proceeds of which Secured Party will disburse directly to
Great Lakes Mazak Center LLC in payment of the purchase price of the Collateral.

**2.1 Grant.** Debtor grants Secured Party a security interest in one (1) Mazak Quick Turn 350MSY
CNC turning center, serial no. 340118, with all attachments, tooling and accessions, and all
proceeds thereof (the "Collateral").

**2.2 Secured Obligations.** This Agreement secures (a) the obligations of Debtor under the Loan B
note and this Agreement and (b) all obligations of Debtor under its Working Capital Promissory
Note dated March 3, 2025 payable to Secured Party in the original principal amount of $150,000
(the "Working Capital Note"). It secures no other obligation.

**3.1 Billing.** Secured Party may bill Loan B and the Working Capital Note on a single account
statement.

**6.2 Encumbrances.** Debtor shall keep the Collateral free of liens other than Permitted
Encumbrances. "Permitted Encumbrances" means (a) the security interest of Secured Party,
(b) liens for taxes not yet due, and (c) liens in favor of Debtor's senior revolving lender and
Debtor's term lender, each of which Secured Party acknowledges and consents to; provided that
nothing in this Section subordinates Secured Party's security interest to any other lien.

**8.4 Attorneys' Fees.** Debtor shall pay all reasonable attorneys' fees and legal expenses
incurred by Secured Party in enforcing this Agreement, and such amounts shall be secured hereby.

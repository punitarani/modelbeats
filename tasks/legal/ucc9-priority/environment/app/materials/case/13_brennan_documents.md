## EXHIBIT BR-1: PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND RETAIL INSTALLMENT CONTRACT (excerpts)

**PURCHASE AGREEMENT, RETAIL INSTALLMENT CONTRACT AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** No. BMT-26-0115,
dated January 15, 2026, between BRENNAN MACHINE TOOL SALES, INC., 2600 Mill Road, Danforth,
Franklin ("Seller"), and KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC., 4100 Industrial Parkway, Riverton,
Franklin ("Buyer").

**1. Sale.** Seller sells to Buyer, and Buyer buys, one new DMG Mori NLX 2500/700 CNC turning
center, serial no. NL2507-30215, with standard tooling package and chip conveyor (the
"Equipment").

**2. Price and Payment.**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash price | $300,000.00 |
| Paid at signing by wire from Buyer's lender, Northbridge Bank, N.A. | ($150,000.00) |
| Cash down payment by Buyer | ($40,000.00) |
| Unpaid balance of cash price (Amount Financed) | $110,000.00 |

Buyer shall pay the Amount Financed to Seller in 22 consecutive monthly installments of
$5,000.00 commencing March 1, 2026, plus a finance charge at 9.0% per annum on the unpaid balance,
payable monthly.

**3. Security Interest.** To secure payment of the Amount Financed, the finance charge, and all
other obligations of Buyer under this Contract, Buyer grants Seller a security interest in the
Equipment, all accessions to it, and all proceeds of it. Seller may file a financing statement
describing the Equipment.

**4. Delivery; Location.** Seller shall deliver and install the Equipment at Buyer's plant at
4100 Industrial Parkway, Riverton, Franklin. Buyer shall keep the Equipment at that plant and
insured for its full value.

**5. Default.** If Buyer fails to pay any installment within 10 days after it is due, Seller may
declare the entire unpaid balance due and exercise the rights of a secured party under the UCC.

**9. Entire Agreement.** This Contract is the entire agreement of the parties regarding the
Equipment and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

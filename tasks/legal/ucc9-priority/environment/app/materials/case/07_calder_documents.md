## EXHIBIT C-1: CREDIT AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (excerpts)

**CREDIT AND SECURITY AGREEMENT** dated May 10, 2021 between CALDER HYDRAULICS, INC. ("Calder")
and KESTREL PRECISION COMPONENTS, INC. ("Customer").

**1. Credit Sales.** Calder may from time to time sell hydraulic pumps, pump components, and
related goods to Customer on open account for resale by Customer ("Calder Goods"). Each sale is
governed by Calder's invoice and this Agreement. Payment terms are net 45 days unless the
invoice states otherwise.

**2. Grant of Security Interest.** To secure payment of the purchase price of all Calder Goods
sold to Customer, whenever sold, and all other obligations under this Agreement, Customer grants
Calder a security interest in all Calder Goods now or hereafter in Customer's possession or
control, whether or not paid for, and all proceeds thereof. Customer grants no security interest
in any other property.

**3. Application of Payments.** Customer's payments shall be applied to invoices in the order
Customer designates or, absent designation, to the oldest invoice first.

**4. Finance Charges.** Past-due balances bear a finance charge of 1.5% per month.

**5. Customer Covenants.** Customer shall keep Calder Goods at the Plant, shall maintain records
identifying Calder Goods by serial number, and may sell Calder Goods in the ordinary course of
business.

**9. Entire Agreement.** This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both parties.

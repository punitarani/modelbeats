**Re: Transaction Bonus**

Dear Dana:

In recognition of your leadership of the Company's sale process, the Compensation Committee has
approved a one-time cash transaction bonus on the following terms:

1. **Amount.** Subject to paragraph 2, upon the closing of the merger of the Company with a subsidiary
of Aster Automation Holdings, Inc. (the "**Closing**"), you will receive a cash bonus of $1,250,000,
**reduced (but not below zero) by the amount of any Carve-Out Payment payable to you at the Closing
under the Company's Management Carve-Out Plan**.

2. **Conditions.** You must remain employed by the Company through the Closing.

3. **Payment.** The bonus will be paid at the Closing through the Company's payroll, less applicable withholding.
Under Section 6.10 of the merger agreement, Parent has agreed to bear the bonus; it is not a
Transaction Expense and does not reduce the consideration payable to equityholders.

4. **Other Payments.** This bonus is in addition to, and will not reduce, any amounts payable to you in
respect of your Common Stock or Options.

5. **Section 280G.** This bonus is subject to any waiver you execute in connection with Section 280G of
the Internal Revenue Code.

# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
# 2016 EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN
### (as amended through March 1, 2023; conformed copy)

**1. Purposes of the Plan.** The purposes of this Plan are to attract and retain the best available
personnel for positions of substantial responsibility, to provide additional incentive to Employees,
Directors and Consultants, and to promote the success of the Company's business. The Plan permits the
grant of Incentive Stock Options, Nonstatutory Stock Options, Restricted Stock and Restricted Stock
Units.

**2. Definitions.** As used herein, the following definitions will apply:

(a) "**Administrator**" means the Board or any of its Committees as will be administering the Plan.

(b) "**Award**" means, individually or collectively, a grant under the Plan of Options, Restricted Stock
or Restricted Stock Units.

(c) "**Award Agreement**" means the written or electronic agreement setting forth the terms and
provisions applicable to each Award granted under the Plan, including any notice of grant. The Award
Agreement is subject to the terms and conditions of the Plan.

(d) "**Change in Control**" means the occurrence of any of the following events: (i) a change in the
ownership of the Company which occurs on the date that any one person, or more than one person acting
as a group, acquires ownership of the stock of the Company that, together with the stock held by such
person, constitutes more than fifty percent (50%) of the total voting power of the stock of the
Company; (ii) a merger of the Company with or into another entity in which the stockholders of the
Company immediately before the transaction do not hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving
entity (or its parent) immediately after it; or (iii) a sale of all or substantially all of the
Company's assets. A transaction will not be a Change in Control if its sole purpose is to change the
state of the Company's incorporation.

(e) "**Continuous Service**" means that the Participant's service with the Company or a subsidiary,
whether as an Employee, Director or Consultant, is not interrupted or terminated. A Participant's
Continuous Service will not be deemed to have terminated merely because of a change in the capacity in
which the Participant renders service. An approved leave of absence will not interrupt Continuous
Service.

(f) "**Corporate Transaction**" means a Change in Control or any other merger or consolidation of the
Company with or into another corporation.

(g) "**Fair Market Value**" means, as of any date, the value of Common Stock determined in good faith by
the Administrator in a manner consistent with Section 409A of the Code.

(h) "**Option**" means a stock option granted pursuant to the Plan.

(i) "**Participant**" means the holder of an outstanding Award.

(j) "**Vesting Commencement Date**" means the date specified as such in the Award Agreement or, if none
is specified, the date of grant.

**3. Stock Subject to the Plan.** Subject to Section 13, the maximum aggregate number of Shares that may
be subject to Awards and sold under the Plan is 6,500,000 Shares. If an Award expires or becomes
unexercisable without having been exercised in full, the unpurchased Shares which were subject thereto
will become available for future grant under the Plan.

**4. Administration of the Plan.** (a) The Plan will be administered by the Board or a Committee. (b)
Subject to the provisions of the Plan, the Administrator will have the authority, in its discretion: to
determine the Fair Market Value; to select the Service Providers to whom Awards may be granted; to
determine the number of Shares to be covered by each Award; to approve forms of Award Agreements; to
determine the terms and conditions of any Award, including the exercise price, the time or times when
Awards may be exercised and any vesting acceleration; and to modify or amend each Award (subject to
Section 18), including to correct any defect, omission or inconsistency in any Award. (c) All decisions,
determinations and interpretations of the Administrator will be final and binding on all Participants.
**In the event of any conflict between the terms of an Award as approved by the Administrator
(including any correction or amendment so approved) and any Company record, ledger, spreadsheet or
third-party equity management system entry relating to such Award, the terms as approved by the
Administrator will control.**

**5. Eligibility.** Nonstatutory Stock Options, Restricted Stock and Restricted Stock Units may be
granted to Service Providers. Incentive Stock Options may be granted only to Employees.

**6. Stock Options.**

(a) *Exercise Price.* The per Share exercise price for the Shares to be issued pursuant to exercise of
an Option will be determined by the Administrator, but will be no less than one hundred percent (100%)
of the Fair Market Value per Share on the date of grant.

(b) *Term of Option.* The term of each Option will be stated in the Award Agreement and will be no more
than ten (10) years from the date of grant.

(c) *Vesting.* Unless otherwise provided in the Award Agreement, Options will vest as follows, subject
to the Participant's Continuous Service through each applicable vesting date:

  (i) *Standard four-year schedule ("4Y-1Ycliff-monthly").* Twenty-five percent (25%) of the Shares
  subject to the Option vest on the first anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date, and an
  additional 1/48th of the Shares subject to the Option vest on the same day of each month thereafter
  (each, a "Monthly Vesting Date"), so that the Option is fully vested on the fourth anniversary of the
  Vesting Commencement Date.

  (ii) *Director schedule ("3Y-nocliff-monthly").* 1/36th of the Shares subject to the Option vest on
  each Monthly Vesting Date beginning with the first monthly anniversary of the Vesting Commencement
  Date, so that the Option is fully vested on the third anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date.

  (iii) *Month-end convention.* If the Vesting Commencement Date falls on a day of the month that does
  not occur in a given month, the Monthly Vesting Date for that month (and, where applicable, the
  anniversary date) will be the last day of that month.

  (iv) *Rounding.* The number of Shares vested as of any date will be determined by multiplying the
  total number of Shares subject to the Option by the cumulative vested fraction as of that date and
  rounding down to the nearest whole Share; any fractional Shares will vest on the final vesting date.

(d) *Termination of Continuous Service.* If a Participant's Continuous Service terminates, other than
upon the Participant's death, Disability or termination for Cause, the Participant may exercise his or
her Option within three (3) months following the date of such termination, to the extent that the
Option is vested on the date of termination (but in no event later than the expiration of the term of
such Option as set forth in the Award Agreement). Unless otherwise provided by the Administrator, if on
the date of termination the Participant is not vested as to his or her entire Option, the Shares
covered by the unvested portion of the Option will revert to the Plan on such date. If after
termination the Participant does not exercise his or her Option within the time specified by the
Administrator, the Option will terminate, and the Shares covered by such Option will revert to the
Plan. If a Participant's Continuous Service terminates by reason of death or Disability, the exercise
period is twelve (12) months following termination. If a Participant's Continuous Service is
terminated for Cause, the Option terminates immediately.

(e) *Method of Exercise.* An Option will be deemed exercised when the Company receives a notice of
exercise and full payment for the Shares with respect to which the Option is exercised.

**7. Restricted Stock.** (a) Each Award of Restricted Stock will be evidenced by an Award Agreement that
will specify the period of restriction, the number of Shares granted, and such other terms and
conditions as the Administrator determines. (b) No Restricted Stock is outstanding.

**8. Restricted Stock Units.** No Restricted Stock Units have been granted.

**9. Leaves of Absence.** Unless the Administrator provides otherwise, vesting of Awards will be
suspended during any unpaid leave of absence in excess of ninety (90) days. No Participant in
Continuous Service at the Closing is or has been on such a leave.

**10. Transferability of Awards.** Unless determined otherwise by the Administrator, an Award may not
be sold, pledged, assigned, hypothecated, transferred, or disposed of in any manner other than by will
or by the laws of descent and distribution, and may be exercised, during the lifetime of the
Participant, only by the Participant.

**11. Withholding.** Prior to the delivery of any Shares or cash pursuant to an Award, the Company will
have the power and the right to deduct or withhold an amount sufficient to satisfy federal, state,
local, foreign or other taxes required to be withheld.

**12. Adjustments.** In the event that any dividend or other distribution (whether in the form of cash,
Shares, other securities, or other property), recapitalization, stock split, reverse stock split,
reorganization, merger, consolidation, split-up, spin-off, combination, repurchase, or exchange of
Shares occurs, the Administrator, in order to prevent diminution or enlargement of the benefits
intended to be made available under the Plan, will adjust the number and class of Shares that may be
delivered under the Plan and/or the number, class, and price of Shares covered by each outstanding
Award.

**13. Corporate Transactions.**

(a) *Dissolution or Liquidation.* In the event of the proposed dissolution or liquidation of the
Company, the Administrator will notify each Participant as soon as practicable prior to the effective
date of such proposed transaction. To the extent it has not been previously exercised, an Award will
terminate immediately prior to the consummation of such proposed action.

(b) *Assumption or Substitution.* In the event of a Corporate Transaction, each outstanding Award will
be treated as the Administrator determines, including that Awards may be assumed or substituted by the
successor corporation or its parent.

(c) *Cash-Out.* In the event of a Corporate Transaction, the Administrator may, without the consent of
any Participant, provide that (i) each outstanding Option, to the extent vested as of immediately prior
to the Corporate Transaction (after giving effect to any acceleration of vesting provided in the
applicable Award Agreement), shall be cancelled in exchange for a cash payment equal to the excess, if
any, of the per-share consideration payable in the transaction to holders of Common Stock over the
per-share exercise price of the Option, multiplied by the number of vested Shares subject to the
Option; (ii) any vested Option whose per-share exercise price equals or exceeds such per-share
consideration shall be cancelled without payment; and (iii) all unvested Options shall terminate
without payment.

(d) *No Acceleration Unless Provided.* Except as expressly provided in an Award Agreement, no Award
shall accelerate vesting by reason of a Corporate Transaction.

**14. Date of Grant.** The date of grant of an Award will be the date on which the Administrator makes
the determination granting such Award, or such other later date as is determined by the
Administrator.

**15. Term of Plan.** The Plan became effective upon its adoption by the Board on March 1, 2016 and
will continue in effect for a term of ten (10) years unless terminated earlier; the Plan was extended
by the Board and stockholders on March 1, 2023 through March 1, 2033.

**16. Amendment and Termination of the Plan.** The Administrator may at any time amend, alter, suspend
or terminate the Plan. No amendment, alteration, suspension or termination of the Plan will impair the
rights of any Participant, unless mutually agreed otherwise between the Participant and the
Administrator.

**17. Conditions Upon Issuance of Shares.** Shares will not be issued pursuant to the exercise of an
Award unless the exercise of such Award and the issuance and delivery of such Shares will comply with
Applicable Laws.

**18. Governing Law.** The Plan and all Award Agreements will be governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
# MANAGEMENT CARVE-OUT PLAN
### Adopted by the Board of Directors on February 10, 2025

**1. Purpose.** The Company's preferred stock carries liquidation preferences that, in a sale of the
Company at certain values, could leave little or no proceeds for holders of Common Stock and Options.
This Plan is intended to retain and incentivize key members of management by setting aside a portion
of the proceeds of a Sale Event for Participants.

**2. Definitions.**

(a) "**Sale Event**" means a Deemed Liquidation Event as defined in the Company's Certificate of
Incorporation, as amended from time to time.

(b) "**Carve-Out Proceeds**" means the "Closing Merger Consideration" (or, if the definitive agreement for
the Sale Event uses a different term, the equivalent concept: the net amount payable at the closing in
respect of the Company's capital stock and equity awards after the purchase price adjustments and
deductions provided in such agreement), **computed without deduction of Carve-Out Payments or of any
transaction bonus payable by the Company to any Participant**, but otherwise computed as provided in the
definitive agreement. Amounts placed in escrow, holdback or expense funds are not Carve-Out Proceeds
unless and until released, and any such later release is outside the scope of payments to be made at
the closing.

(c) "**Participant**" means an employee of the Company to whom Units have been awarded by the Board.

(d) "**Unit**" means a unit of participation in the Pool. The maximum number of Units that may be
outstanding at any time is one hundred (100).

**3. Carve-Out Pool.**

(a) Upon the consummation of a Sale Event, the Company shall set aside, out of the proceeds otherwise
payable to its equityholders, a pool (the "**Pool**") equal to six percent (6%) of the Carve-Out
Proceeds.

(b) Notwithstanding Section 3(a), no Pool shall be established and no amount shall be payable under
this Plan if the Carve-Out Proceeds equal or exceed $50,000,000.

**4. Allocation of the Pool.** Each Participant whose Units are outstanding at the consummation of the
Sale Event shall be entitled to a payment (a "**Carve-Out Payment**") equal to the Pool multiplied by a
fraction, the numerator of which is the number of Units held by such Participant and the denominator of
which is one hundred (100) (regardless of the number of Units then outstanding). Any portion of the
Pool attributable to Units that are unallocated or have been forfeited shall not be paid to any person
and shall remain part of the proceeds payable to the Company's equityholders.

**5. Forfeiture.** If a Participant's employment with the Company terminates for any reason prior to the
consummation of a Sale Event, all of such Participant's Units shall be forfeited automatically and
without consideration on the date of termination. Forfeited Units may be re-awarded only by action of
the Board.

**6. Timing and Form of Payment.** Carve-Out Payments shall be paid in cash at the closing of the Sale
Event (or as soon as practicable thereafter through the Company's payroll), less applicable
withholding. Carve-Out Payments shall be treated as transaction expenses of the Company in the
definitive agreement for the Sale Event.

**7. No Offset.** Except as provided in a written agreement between the Company and a Participant,
Carve-Out Payments are in addition to, and shall not reduce or be reduced by, any amount payable to a
Participant in respect of any capital stock, Option or other security of the Company.

**8. Administration.** The Board administers this Plan and its determinations are final and binding.

**9. Amendment and Termination.** The Board may amend or terminate this Plan at any time prior to a Sale
Event, provided that no amendment may reduce the number of Units held by a Participant without such
Participant's consent. This Plan shall terminate automatically upon the earlier of (i) payment of all
amounts payable in connection with a Sale Event and (ii) the Company's initial public offering.

**10. Section 409A.** Payments under this Plan are intended to be exempt from Section 409A of the Code as
short-term deferrals.

**11. Governing Law.** This Plan shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

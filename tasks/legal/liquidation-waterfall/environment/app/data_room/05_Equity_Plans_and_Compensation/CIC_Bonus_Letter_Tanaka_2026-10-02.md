**Re: Change-in-Control Bonus**

Dear Mei:

In recognition of your work leading the operational diligence and integration planning for the
proposed merger of the Company with a subsidiary of Aster Automation Holdings, Inc. (the "**Merger**"),
the Compensation Committee has approved a one-time cash bonus on the following terms:

1. **Amount.** Upon the closing of the Merger (the "**Closing**"), you will receive a cash bonus equal
to one percent (1%) of the "Base Purchase Price" as defined in, and finally determined under, the merger
agreement for the Merger.

2. **Conditions.** You must remain employed by the Company through the Closing.

3. **Payment.** The bonus will be paid at the Closing through the Company's payroll, less applicable
withholding (including any tax required to be withheld under Section 4999(c)(1) of the Internal Revenue
Code). Under Section 6.10 of the merger agreement, Parent has agreed to bear the bonus; it is not a
Transaction Expense and does not reduce the consideration payable to equityholders.

4. **No Reduction or Gross-Up.** The bonus will not be reduced on account of Sections 280G or 4999 of the
Internal Revenue Code, and the Company will not gross you up for any tax.

5. **Section 409A.** The bonus is intended to be a short-term deferral exempt from Section 409A.

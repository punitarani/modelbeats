# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Notice of Action by Written Consent and Notice of Availability of Appraisal Rights
### Given October 29, 2026

To the stockholders of Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "**Company**") who did not consent in writing to the
action described below:

This notice is given under Sections 228(e) and 262(d)(2) of the Delaware General Corporation Law (the
"**DGCL**"). On October 1-3, 2026, the holders of the requisite shares of the Company's capital stock
adopted, by written consent, the Agreement and Plan of Merger among Aster Automation Holdings, Inc.,
Aster Merger Sub, Inc., the Company and Fernhill Stockholder Services LLC. The Merger is expected to
become effective on November 20, 2026.

Appraisal rights under Section 262 of the DGCL are available for any or all shares of each class and
series of the Company's capital stock. A copy of Section 262 is attached as Annex A, together with an
information statement describing the Merger and the allocation of consideration. Any stockholder
wishing to demand appraisal must comply with Section 262, including the time limit for delivering a
written demand set out in Section 262(d)(2). Demands may be delivered by electronic transmission to
appraisal@halvorsenrobotics.com, the information processing system designated for that purpose.

*Certificate of mailing:* This notice was sent on October 29, 2026 by first-class U.S. mail, postage
prepaid, and by email, to each holder of record as of the September 30, 2026 record date who did not
consent in writing to the Merger.

/s/ Hannah Varga, Assistant Secretary

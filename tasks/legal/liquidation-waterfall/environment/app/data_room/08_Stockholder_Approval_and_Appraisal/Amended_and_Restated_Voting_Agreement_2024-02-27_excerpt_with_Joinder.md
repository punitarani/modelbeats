**3. Drag-Along Right.**

3.1 *Definitions.* A "**Sale of the Company**" means either (a) a transaction or series of related
transactions in which a Person, or a group of related Persons, acquires from stockholders of the
Company shares representing more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting power of the
Company, or (b) a transaction that qualifies as a "Deemed Liquidation Event" as defined in the
Company's Restated Certificate. "**Requisite Holders**" means the holders of a majority of the
outstanding Preferred Stock (voting together on an as-converted basis) together with the Requisite
Series C Holders (as defined in the Restated Certificate).

3.2 *Actions to be Taken.* In the event that (i) the Requisite Holders and (ii) the Board approve a Sale
of the Company in writing, specifying that this Section 3 shall apply to such transaction, then, subject
to satisfaction of each of the conditions set forth in Section 3.3, each Stockholder and the Company
hereby agree:

  (a) if such transaction requires stockholder approval, with respect to all Shares that such
  Stockholder owns or over which such Stockholder otherwise exercises voting power, to vote (in person,
  by proxy or by action by written consent, as applicable) all Shares in favor of, and adopt, such Sale
  of the Company;

  (b) to execute and deliver all related documentation and take such other action in support of the
  Sale of the Company as shall reasonably be requested by the Company; and

  (c) **to refrain from exercising any dissenters' rights or rights of appraisal under applicable law at
  any time with respect to such Sale of the Company.**

3.3 *Conditions.* A Stockholder will not be required to comply with Section 3.2 unless (a) the
Stockholder is not required to make any representation other than as to its own title, authority and
the like; (b) the Stockholder's liability for indemnification is several and not joint and capped at
the consideration it receives; (c) the consideration is allocated as provided in the Restated
Certificate; and (d) the consideration for each share of a class or series is in the same form as for
every other share of that class or series.

**7. Miscellaneous.** 7.1 *Term.* This Agreement terminates upon the closing of a Sale of the Company,
provided that Section 3 shall survive with respect to any Sale of the Company approved before such
termination. 7.2 *Governing Law.* Delaware. 7.3 *Specific Performance.* Each party agrees that the
Company and the other parties may enforce this Agreement by specific performance.

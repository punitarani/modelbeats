# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Written Consent of the Stockholders Adopting the Agreement and Plan of Merger
### (Sections 228 and 251 of the Delaware General Corporation Law)
### Record date fixed by the Board: September 30, 2026

The undersigned stockholders of Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "**Company**") hereby consent to, approve
and adopt the following resolutions by written consent:

**1. Adoption of Merger Agreement.** RESOLVED, that the Agreement and Plan of Merger among Aster
Automation Holdings, Inc., Aster Merger Sub, Inc., the Company and Fernhill Stockholder Services LLC (the
"**Merger Agreement**"), and the merger contemplated thereby (the "**Merger**"), are hereby adopted and
approved in all respects;

**2. Deemed Liquidation Event.** RESOLVED, that the undersigned acknowledge that the Merger is a Deemed
Liquidation Event and that the consideration shall be allocated in accordance with Article Fourth, Part
B, Section 2 of the Company's certificate of incorporation;

**3. Sale of the Company.** RESOLVED, that the undersigned, constituting the Requisite Holders under
the Amended and Restated Voting Agreement dated February 27, 2024, approve the Merger as a "Sale of the
Company" for purposes of Section 3 thereof.

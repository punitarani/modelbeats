# Brightwater & Co. LLP: Section 280G Analysis, Project Aster (summary of conclusions)
### October 12, 2026

1. The Company's stock is not readily tradeable on an established securities market. The Company does
   not qualify as a "small business corporation" (it has preferred stock and entity stockholders), so
   the exemption in Section 280G(b)(5)(A)(i) is unavailable; the stockholder approval exemption in
   Section 280G(b)(5)(A)(ii) is available.
2. Disqualified individuals receiving payments contingent on the Merger: Dana Okafor (CEO; >1%
   stockholder), Marcus Lindqvist (CTO; >1% stockholder), Priya Raman (CFO-designate / officer), Amara
   Diallo (VP Sales; highly compensated), Mei Tanaka (COO / officer; joined December 1, 2025).
3. Conclusions: (a) Ms. Okafor's contingent payments, including the transaction bonus, exceed her
   safe-harbor amount; with the transaction bonus waived (subject to stockholder approval) her remaining
   payments are below the safe harbor. (b) The contingent payments of Mr. Lindqvist, Ms. Raman and Ms.
   Diallo are each below their respective safe-harbor amounts and are therefore not parachute payments
   whether or not stockholder approval is obtained. (c) Ms. Tanaka: her only payment contingent on the
   Merger is the change-in-control bonus under her letter of October 2, 2026 (her options are unvested
   and are cancelled without payment). The Company has not sought, and will not seek, stockholder
   approval of her bonus; she has not waived it, and her letter provides for no cutback or gross-up.
   We have established, to the clear-and-convincing-evidence standard, that $450,000 of the bonus is
   reasonable compensation for personal services actually rendered by Ms. Tanaka before the Closing; no
   portion is compensation for services to be rendered on or after the Closing. We have not yet
   computed Ms. Tanaka's base amount. Her compensation history is in folder 10_Payroll. (d) No other
   individual receives payments that would be parachute payments.
4. Ms. Okafor's interests: 4,200,000 shares of Common Stock held directly; the Okafor Family Trust dated
   March 2, 2021 (revocable; Ms. Okafor is grantor, trustee and sole lifetime beneficiary) holds 300,000
   shares of Common Stock; Grant H-101 options.
5. Stockholders related to Ms. Okafor: Rosa Jimenez (250,000 shares of Common Stock) is Ms. Okafor's
   maternal grandmother, and Daniel Osei (150,000 shares of Common Stock) is her half-brother. Neither is a
   disqualified individual or holds any interest through the Okafor Family Trust. No other stockholder
   has any family, trust, partnership, corporate or other relationship with Ms. Okafor.

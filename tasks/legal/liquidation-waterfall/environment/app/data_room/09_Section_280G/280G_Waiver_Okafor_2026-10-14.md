# SECTION 280G WAIVER AGREEMENT
### Dana Okafor and Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., October 14, 2026

1. **Background.** The Company's outside accountants (Brightwater & Co. LLP) have determined that,
absent stockholder approval meeting the requirements of Section 280G(b)(5)(B) of the Internal Revenue
Code and Treas. Reg. § 1.280G-1, Q&A-7, the aggregate present value of the payments and benefits to be
received by Executive that are contingent on the Merger would equal or exceed three times Executive's
"base amount," and that such payments would therefore be "parachute payments."

2. **Waiver.** Executive hereby irrevocably waives her right to receive the cash transaction bonus
described in the letter dated September 30, 2026 (the "**Waived Payment**") unless the Waived Payment is
approved by the Company's stockholders in a vote that satisfies the requirements of Section
280G(b)(5)(B) of the Code and Treas. Reg. § 1.280G-1, Q&A-7. If such approval is not obtained before the
Closing, Executive shall have no right to the Waived Payment, which shall be forfeited.

3. **Other Payments.** The Waived Payment is the only payment subject to this waiver.

/s/ Dana Okafor   /s/ Thomas Ekwueme, CFO

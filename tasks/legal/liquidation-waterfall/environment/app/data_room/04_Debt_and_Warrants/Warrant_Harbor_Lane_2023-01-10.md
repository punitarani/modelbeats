**ARTICLE 1. EXERCISE.**

1.1 *Method of Exercise.* Holder may at any time and from time to time exercise this Warrant, in whole
or in part, by delivering to the Company the original of this Warrant together with a duly executed
Notice of Exercise and, unless Holder is exercising this Warrant pursuant to a cashless exercise set
forth in Section 1.2, a check, wire transfer or other form of payment acceptable to the Company for
the aggregate Warrant Price for the Shares being purchased.

1.2 *Cashless Exercise.* On any exercise of this Warrant, in lieu of payment of the aggregate Warrant
Price, Holder may elect to receive Shares equal to the value of this Warrant, or portion hereof as to
which this Warrant is being exercised. Thereupon, the Company shall issue to the Holder such number of
fully paid and non-assessable Shares as are computed using the following formula: X = Y(A-B)/A, where
X is the number of Shares to be issued to the Holder; Y is the number of Shares with respect to which
this Warrant is being exercised; A is the fair market value (as determined pursuant to Section 1.3) of
one Share; and B is the Warrant Price.

1.3 *Fair Market Value.* If the Company's common stock is not traded in a public market, the fair market
value of a Share shall be determined by the Board in good faith; *provided* that in connection with an
Acquisition, the fair market value of a Share shall be the value of the consideration per Share payable
at the closing of the Acquisition to holders of the Class (determined in accordance with the Company's
certificate of incorporation and the definitive agreement for the Acquisition).

1.4 *Fractional Shares.* No fractional Share shall be issued upon exercise of this Warrant; in lieu of a
fractional Share, the Company shall pay the Holder cash equal to the fraction multiplied by the fair
market value of a Share.

1.5 *Treatment of Warrant Upon Acquisition.* "**Acquisition**" means any sale, license or other
disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company, or any reorganization,
consolidation or merger of the Company where the holders of the Company's securities before the
transaction beneficially own less than a majority of the outstanding voting securities of the
surviving entity after the transaction. Upon the closing of any Acquisition in which the consideration
is solely cash, (a) if the fair market value of a Share (as determined under Section 1.3) exceeds the
Warrant Price, this Warrant shall automatically be deemed exercised in full pursuant to Section 1.2
immediately prior to such closing, without any action by Holder; and (b) otherwise this Warrant shall
expire immediately prior to such closing without consideration.

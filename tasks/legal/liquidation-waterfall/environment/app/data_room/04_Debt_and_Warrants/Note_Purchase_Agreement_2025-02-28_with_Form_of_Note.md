# NOTE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
### dated as of February 28, 2025, among Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. and the Purchasers listed on Exhibit A

**1. Definitions.** "**Notes**" means the convertible promissory notes issued under this Agreement,
substantially in the form of Exhibit B. "**Majority Holders**" means, at any time, the holders of Notes
representing more than fifty percent (50%) of the aggregate outstanding principal amount of all Notes.
"**Change of Control**" means a Deemed Liquidation Event as defined in the Company's Certificate of
Incorporation as in effect from time to time.

**2. Purchase and Sale of Notes.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, each Purchaser agrees to
purchase at a Closing, and the Company agrees to sell and issue to each Purchaser, a Note in the
principal amount set forth opposite such Purchaser's name on Exhibit A. The aggregate principal amount
of Notes issued under this Agreement shall not exceed $6,000,000. The initial Closing shall occur on
March 3, 2025; additional Closings may occur on or before June 30, 2025.

**3. Representations and Warranties of the Company.** *[omitted]*

**4. Representations and Warranties of the Purchasers.** *[omitted]*

**5. Conditions to Closing.** *[omitted]*

**6. Subordination.** The Notes are subordinated in right of payment to the Company's obligations to
Harbor Lane Capital, LLC under the Loan and Security Agreement dated January 10, 2023, as provided in
the Subordination Agreement of even date herewith. The Notes rank senior to all of the Company's
capital stock.

**7. Miscellaneous.**

7.1 *Amendments and Waivers.* Any term of this Agreement or the Notes may be amended or waived only with
the written consent of the Company and the Majority Holders. Any amendment or waiver effected in
accordance with this Section 7.1 shall be binding upon each Purchaser and each holder of Notes.

7.2 *Elections and Notices Under the Notes.* **Any election, notice or determination to be made by "the
Holder" under Section 4 or Section 5 of any Note shall be made exclusively by the Majority Holders, by
written notice to the Company, and shall be binding on all holders of Notes and apply to all Notes
alike. Any purported election by a holder that is not made by or with the written concurrence of the
Majority Holders shall be of no effect.**

7.3 *Governing Law.* This Agreement shall be governed by the internal law of the State of Delaware.

7.4 *Notices.* All notices shall be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of transmission
or by nationally recognized overnight courier to the addresses on Exhibit A.

## EXHIBIT B: FORM OF CONVERTIBLE PROMISSORY NOTE

THIS NOTE AND THE SECURITIES ISSUABLE UPON ITS CONVERSION HAVE NOT BEEN REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES
ACT OF 1933.

**HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.**
**CONVERTIBLE PROMISSORY NOTE**

Principal: $[____]  Issue Date: [____]

For value received, Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), promises to
pay to the holder named below (the "**Holder**") the principal sum set forth above, together with
interest on the unpaid principal balance, as provided in this Note. This Note is one of the Notes
issued under the Note Purchase Agreement dated as of February 28, 2025 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"),
and is subject to its terms, including Section 7.2 thereof.

**1. Interest.** Simple interest accrues on the outstanding principal amount at the rate of six
percent (6%) per annum from and including the Issue Date to but excluding the date on which this Note
is repaid or converted, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.
Interest is payable at maturity, upon repayment, or upon conversion as provided below.

**2. Maturity.** Unless earlier converted or repaid, the outstanding principal and accrued interest are
due and payable on March 3, 2027 (the "**Maturity Date**").

**3. Prepayment.** The Company may not prepay this Note without the consent of the Majority Holders,
except as provided in Section 5.

**4. Qualified Financing Conversion.** If the Company sells shares of its preferred stock in a single
transaction or series of related transactions with aggregate gross proceeds of at least $15,000,000
(excluding conversion of the Notes) before the Maturity Date (a "**Qualified Financing**"), the
outstanding principal and accrued interest of this Note shall automatically convert into shares of the
series of preferred stock sold in the Qualified Financing at a price per share equal to 80% of the
price paid per share by the cash investors. No Qualified Financing has occurred.

**5. Change of Control.**

(a) If a Change of Control occurs before the conversion or repayment of this Note, then, at the
election of the Holder made by written notice delivered to the Company not less than five (5)
Business Days before the anticipated closing date of the Change of Control, either:

  (i) **Repayment.** the Company shall pay the Holder, at and contingent upon the closing of the Change
  of Control, an amount in cash equal to one and one-half (1.5) times the outstanding principal amount
  of this Note, plus all accrued and unpaid interest (the "**Repayment Amount**"); or

  (ii) **Conversion.** the outstanding principal amount of this Note and all accrued and unpaid interest
  shall convert, effective immediately prior to and contingent upon the closing of the Change of
  Control, into that number of shares of Common Stock equal to such principal and interest divided by
  $2.00 (the "**Change of Control Conversion Price**"), and the Holder shall participate in the Change
  of Control in respect of such shares as a holder of Common Stock.

(b) If no valid election is delivered in accordance with Section 5(a), the Holder shall be deemed to
have elected repayment under Section 5(a)(i).

(c) Fractional shares issuable upon conversion under Section 5(a)(ii) shall be treated as provided in
the definitive agreement for the Change of Control or, absent such provision, paid in cash at the
Change of Control Conversion Price.

**6. Events of Default.** *[omitted]*

**7. Miscellaneous.** "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which
banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close. This Note shall be governed by
the laws of the State of Delaware.

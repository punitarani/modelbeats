## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Certain Definitions.** For purposes of this Agreement:

"**Accounting Principles**" means GAAP applied using the same accounting methods, policies, practices,
principles, procedures, classifications, judgments and estimation methodologies used in the
preparation of the Company's audited balance sheet as of December 31, 2025.

"**Aggregate ITM Exercise Price**" means the sum, for all Vested In-the-Money Options, of the number of
shares of Company Common Stock subject to each such Vested In-the-Money Option multiplied by its
per-share exercise price.

"**Base Purchase Price**" means $[__________].

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New
York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing Cash**" means the aggregate amount of cash and cash equivalents of the Company as of 11:59
p.m. Pacific time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the
Accounting Principles, (a) *reduced* by the amount of all issued but uncleared checks, drafts and wires
of the Company, and (b) *excluding* all Restricted Cash.

"**Closing Distributable Amount**" means the Closing Merger Consideration *plus* the Aggregate ITM
Exercise Price.

"**Closing Merger Consideration**" means (a) the Base Purchase Price, *plus* (b) the Closing Cash,
*minus* (c) the Closing Indebtedness, *plus* (d) the Net Working Capital Adjustment Amount (which may be
a negative number), *minus* (e) the Closing Transaction Expenses, *minus* (f) the Escrow Amount, *minus*
(g) the Expense Fund Amount, in each case as set forth in the Estimated Closing Statement.

"**Closing Indebtedness**" means all Indebtedness of the Company outstanding as of immediately prior to
the Closing.

"**Closing Transaction Expenses**" means all Transaction Expenses that are unpaid as of immediately
prior to the Closing.

"**Company Capital Stock**" means the Company Common Stock and the Company Preferred Stock.

"**Company Charter**" means the Company's Sixth Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, as
amended and in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time.

"**Company Options**" means all options to purchase Company Common Stock outstanding under the Company
Equity Plan; "**Company Equity Plan**" means the Company's 2016 Equity Incentive Plan.

"**Company Securities**" means the Company Capital Stock, the Company Options, the Company Warrant and
the Convertible Notes.

"**Company Warrant**" means the Warrant to Purchase Stock issued by the Company to Harbor Lane Capital,
LLC on January 10, 2023.

"**Convertible Notes**" means the convertible promissory notes issued under the Note Purchase Agreement
dated as of February 28, 2025.

"**Escrow Amount**" means an amount equal to ten percent (10%) of the Base Purchase Price.

"**Equityholders**" means the holders of Company Securities.

"**Expense Fund Amount**" means $250,000.

"**Indebtedness**" means, without duplication: (a) all obligations for borrowed money, including all
principal, accrued interest, prepayment premiums or penalties, final payment or exit fees, per diem
amounts and other amounts payable upon repayment in full at the Closing (in the case of the Company's
loan facility with Harbor Lane Capital, LLC, the payoff amount set forth in the Payoff Letter for
payment on the Closing Date); (b) all obligations under leases required to be capitalized in accordance
with GAAP (other than real property operating leases); (c) with respect to each Convertible Note that
is to be repaid at the Closing in accordance with its terms, the Repayment Amount (as defined in the
Convertible Notes) payable at the Closing; and (d) all guarantees of any of the foregoing. Indebtedness
does not include (i) any Convertible Note that converts into Company Common Stock in accordance with
its terms, (ii) any declared but unpaid dividend on Company Capital Stock, which shall be treated
solely through the allocation provided in Section 2.6, (iii) any Transaction Expense, or (iv) any
amount included as a current liability in Net Working Capital.

"**Net Working Capital**" means (a) the current assets of the Company consisting solely of accounts
receivable (net of allowances), inventory, and prepaid expenses, *minus* (b) the current liabilities of
the Company consisting solely of accounts payable, accrued expenses (including accrued payroll and
accrued vacation) and deferred revenue, in each case determined in accordance with the Accounting
Principles. For the avoidance of doubt, Net Working Capital excludes all cash and cash equivalents,
Restricted Cash, income Tax assets and liabilities, Indebtedness (and accrued interest thereon) and
Transaction Expenses (whether or not accrued).

"**Net Working Capital Adjustment Amount**" means (a) if Estimated Net Working Capital is less than the
Lower Collar Amount, the amount (expressed as a negative number) by which the Lower Collar Amount
exceeds Estimated Net Working Capital; (b) if Estimated Net Working Capital is greater than the Upper
Collar Amount, the amount (expressed as a positive number) by which Estimated Net Working Capital
exceeds the Upper Collar Amount; and (c) otherwise, zero. "**Target Net Working Capital**" means
$2,600,000. "**Lower Collar Amount**" means Target Net Working Capital minus $150,000. "**Upper Collar
Amount**" means Target Net Working Capital plus $150,000.

"**Option Cash-Out Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.7(a).

"**Payoff Letter**" means the payoff letter from Harbor Lane Capital, LLC delivered under Section 5.9.

"**Per Share Common Amount**" means the amount payable at Closing, under Article Fourth, Part B,
Section 2 of the Company Charter as applied under Section 2.6, in respect of one share of Company
Common Stock (including a share of Common Stock that a share of a Converting Series of Preferred Stock
is deemed converted into, and a share treated as outstanding under Section 2.7(a), 2.8 or 2.9).

"**Restricted Cash**" means cash and cash equivalents that are not freely usable by the Company because
they are subject to restrictions or limitations on use or distribution by law, contract or otherwise,
including security deposits, collateral for letters of credit, and amounts held in escrow.

"**Transaction Expenses**" means, without duplication and to the extent not paid prior to the Closing,
(a) all fees and expenses of investment bankers, financial advisors, attorneys, accountants and other
advisors incurred by the Company in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement;
(b) all change of control, transaction, sale, retention or similar bonuses or payments payable by the
Company as a result of the Merger, including all Carve-Out Payments under the Company's Management
Carve-Out Plan (but excluding the Okafor transaction bonus and the Tanaka change-in-control bonus, each of which is governed by Section 6.10, the
Option Cash-Out Amounts and any amounts payable in respect of Company Securities under Article II); and (c) fifty
percent (50%) of the premium for the D&O Tail Policy. For the avoidance of doubt, the employer portion of
payroll Taxes on compensatory payments is borne by Parent and is not a Transaction Expense.

"**Vested Option**" means the portion of a Company Option that is vested and exercisable as of
immediately prior to the Effective Time, after giving effect to any acceleration of vesting that
occurs by its terms as a result of the Merger itself. "**Unvested Option**" means any other portion of
a Company Option.

"**Vested In-the-Money Option**" means a Vested Option whose per-share exercise price is less than the
Per Share Common Amount, determined so that it is internally consistent: a Vested Option is a Vested
In-the-Money Option if and only if its exercise price is less than the Per Share Common Amount computed
treating exactly the Vested In-the-Money Options as exercised under Section 2.7(a).

**1.2 Interpretation.** (a) The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by
"without limitation." (b) References to any agreement are to such agreement as amended and in effect.
(c) In the event of any conflict between the Company Charter and this Agreement with respect to the
allocation of consideration among holders of Company Capital Stock, the Company Charter controls. (d)
All payment amounts are rounded to the nearest whole cent only at the level of the aggregate payment to
each Equityholder; intermediate per-share amounts, conversion ratios and share numbers (including
fractional shares) are carried at full precision.

## ARTICLE II. THE MERGER; CLOSING; EFFECT ON SECURITIES; ALLOCATION

**2.1 The Merger.** Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement and the DGCL, at the
Effective Time, Merger Sub shall be merged with and into the Company, the separate corporate existence
of Merger Sub shall cease, and the Company shall continue as the surviving corporation and a wholly
owned subsidiary of Parent. All consideration payable in the Merger is payable in cash.

**2.2 Closing.** The closing of the Merger (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic
exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the
conditions set forth in Article VII (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at
the Closing), or at such other time and date as Parent and the Company may agree in writing (the date
on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Merger shall become effective upon the filing
of the certificate of merger with the Delaware Secretary of State on the Closing Date (the "**Effective
Time**").

**2.3 Certificate of Incorporation; Bylaws; Directors and Officers.** The certificate of incorporation
and bylaws of Merger Sub as in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the certificate
of incorporation and bylaws of the surviving corporation. The directors and officers of Merger Sub
immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the directors and officers of the surviving
corporation.

**2.4 Estimated Closing Statement.**

(a) Not later than five (5) Business Days before the anticipated Closing Date, the Company shall deliver
to Parent a statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting forth the Company's good faith
estimates of the balance sheet items and other amounts necessary to compute Closing Cash, Closing
Indebtedness, Net Working Capital ("**Estimated Net Working Capital**") and Closing Transaction
Expenses, together with supporting documentation. Parent may comment on the Estimated Closing
Statement, and the Company shall consider Parent's comments in good faith. If the Company delivers a
revised Estimated Closing Statement before the Closing, the revised statement shall supersede all
prior versions and shall constitute the Estimated Closing Statement for all purposes of this
Agreement. The amounts derived by applying the definitions in this Agreement to the Estimated Closing
Statement shall be binding for purposes of the payments to be made at the Closing, without prejudice to
the post-Closing adjustment under Section 2.5.

(b) Where the Estimated Closing Statement lists balance sheet line items rather than computed amounts,
the defined amounts shall be computed by applying the definitions in Section 1.1 to those line items.

**2.5 Post-Closing Adjustment.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Parent shall prepare and
deliver a closing statement setting forth its calculation of the actual Closing Merger Consideration.
Any resulting payment to Parent shall be made first from the Escrow Amount, and any resulting payment to
the Equityholders shall be allocated as Additional Consideration. The post-Closing adjustment is
outside the scope of the Closing Allocation Schedule.

**2.6 Effect on Company Capital Stock; Allocation.**

(a) The parties acknowledge that the Merger is a Deemed Liquidation Event under Section 2.6.1(a)(i) of
Article Fourth, Part B of the Company Charter, and that no election has been made under Section 2.6.1
to treat it otherwise.

(b) At the Closing, the Closing Distributable Amount shall be allocated among the holders of Company
Capital Stock (including, for this purpose, the shares deemed outstanding under Sections 2.7(a), 2.8
and 2.9) in accordance with Sections 2.1 through 2.5 of Article Fourth, Part B of the Company Charter,
as if the Closing Distributable Amount were the only consideration payable in the Merger (Section 2.6.4
of Article Fourth, Part B). The Escrow Amount and the Expense Fund Amount constitute Additional
Consideration and are allocated only if and when released, under a separate schedule that is outside
the scope of the Closing Allocation Schedule.

(c) At the Effective Time, each share of Company Capital Stock issued and outstanding immediately
prior to the Effective Time (other than shares held by the Company as treasury stock, which shall be
cancelled for no consideration, and Dissenting Shares) shall be converted into the right to receive, in
cash, the amount allocated to it under Section 2.6(b).

(d) *Dissenting Shares.* "**Dissenting Shares**" means shares of Company Capital Stock outstanding
immediately prior to the Effective Time that are held by a holder who is entitled to demand and has
properly demanded appraisal of such shares in accordance with Section 262 of the DGCL and who has not
effectively withdrawn or lost such holder's appraisal rights. Dissenting Shares shall not be converted
into the right to receive the amounts allocated under Section 2.6(b), but shall represent only the
rights provided by Section 262 of the DGCL. The allocation under Section 2.6(b) shall nonetheless be
computed as though the Dissenting Shares were not Dissenting Shares, and the amount that would have
been payable in respect of Dissenting Shares shall be retained by Parent at the Closing (and applied
toward any payment ultimately required under Section 262). If a holder of Dissenting Shares
subsequently withdraws or loses its appraisal rights, its shares shall be treated as converted at the
Effective Time into the right to receive, without interest, the amounts otherwise allocable to them,
payable after such withdrawal or loss; any such later payment is outside the scope of the Closing
Allocation Schedule.

**2.7 Treatment of Company Options.**

(a) *Vested In-the-Money Options.* For purposes of the allocation under Section 2.6(b), each Vested
In-the-Money Option shall be treated as though it had been exercised in full for cash immediately prior
to the Effective Time: (i) the shares of Company Common Stock subject to it shall be treated as
outstanding shares of Common Stock, and (ii) its aggregate exercise price shall be treated as included
in the amount being allocated (which is why the Aggregate ITM Exercise Price is part of the Closing
Distributable Amount). No exercise price is actually paid. Instead, at the Effective Time each Vested
In-the-Money Option shall be cancelled and converted into the right to receive cash equal to (A) the
excess of the Per Share Common Amount over the per-share exercise price, multiplied by (B) the number of
shares subject to such Vested In-the-Money Option (the "**Option Cash-Out Amount**"), less applicable
withholding.

(b) *Other Options.* Each Vested Option that is not a Vested In-the-Money Option, and each Unvested
Option, shall be cancelled at the Effective Time for no consideration, and shares subject to such
Options shall not be treated as outstanding for any purpose of the allocation.

(c) *Plan Actions.* The Company Board has taken all actions under the Company Equity Plan necessary to
effect the treatment set forth in this Section 2.7.

**2.8 Company Warrant.** The Company Warrant shall be treated in accordance with its terms (including
Section 1.5 thereof). Any shares of Company Common Stock issued or deemed issued upon the exercise of the
Company Warrant immediately prior to the Effective Time (including any fraction of a share, carried at
full precision) shall be treated as outstanding shares of Company Common Stock for purposes of the
allocation under Section 2.6(b).

**2.9 Convertible Notes.** Each Convertible Note shall be treated in accordance with its terms and the
Note Purchase Agreement. (a) The Repayment Amount of any Convertible Note to be repaid shall be paid by
Parent on behalf of the Company at the Closing to the holder thereof and constitutes Closing
Indebtedness. (b) Any shares of Company Common Stock issued upon the conversion of a Convertible Note
immediately prior to the Effective Time (including any fraction of a share, carried at full precision
for the allocation, notwithstanding Section 5(c) of the Convertible Notes) shall be treated as
outstanding shares of Company Common Stock for purposes of the allocation under Section 2.6(b).

**2.10 Closing Payments.** At the Closing, Parent shall pay or cause to be paid: (a) to the Paying Agent,
for further distribution to the holders of Company Capital Stock, the amounts allocated under Section
2.6(b) in respect of such Company Capital Stock (other than Dissenting Shares) (including shares issued upon conversion of the
Convertible Notes and exercise of the Company Warrant); (b) to the Company, for payment through payroll,
the aggregate Option Cash-Out Amounts, the Carve-Out Payments and (under Section 6.10) the Okafor
transaction bonus and the Tanaka change-in-control bonus; (c) on
behalf of the Company, the Closing Indebtedness in accordance with the Payoff Letter and Section 2.9(a);
(d) the Closing Transaction Expenses (other than those paid through payroll) to the payees thereof; (e)
the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent; and (f) the Expense Fund Amount to the Equityholder
Representative.

**2.11 Allocation Schedule.** No later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing, the Company shall
deliver a schedule (the "**Closing Allocation Schedule**") setting forth, for each Equityholder, the
cash amount payable at Closing under this Article II, and, for each recipient of a Carve-Out Payment or
the Okafor transaction bonus, the amount thereof. Parent and the Paying Agent shall be entitled to rely
on the Closing Allocation Schedule without further inquiry.

**2.12 Withholding.** Parent, the Paying Agent and the surviving corporation shall be entitled to deduct
and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted
and withheld under applicable Tax law. The Closing Allocation Schedule states gross amounts before
withholding.

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, the Company represents and warrants to Parent and Merger
Sub as follows:

and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has all requisite corporate power and
authority to own, lease and operate its properties and to carry on its business as currently
conducted.

**3.2 Capitalization.** (a) The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 34,000,000 shares of
Company Common Stock and 19,000,000 shares of Company Preferred Stock. The issued and outstanding
shares of Company Capital Stock are as set forth in the Company's stock ledger, which is complete and

subsidiary. (b) Section 3.2(b) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each outstanding Company Option. (c)

the Company to issue any of its securities.

**3.3 Authority; Board Approval.** The Company has all requisite corporate power and authority to
execute and deliver this Agreement and to consummate the Merger.

**3.4 No Conflict; Consents.** The execution and delivery of this Agreement do not conflict with the
Company Charter or bylaws.

**3.5 Financial Statements.** The Company has delivered audited balance sheets as of December 31, 2024
and 2025 and related statements of operations, which fairly present in all material respects the
financial position of the Company in accordance with GAAP.

**3.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the
ordinary course consistent with past practice.

**3.7 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all income and other material Tax Returns required to be
filed by it and has paid all Taxes shown as due thereon.

**3.8 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all Intellectual Property
used in the conduct of its business as currently conducted.

**3.9 Material Contracts.** Section 3.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Material Contract. Each
Material Contract is in full force and effect.

**3.10 Employee Benefit Plans.** Section 3.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Company Benefit
Plan, including the Company Equity Plan and the Management Carve-Out Plan.

**3.11 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to the Company's Knowledge, threatened against the

**3.12 Brokers.** Except for Larkspur Partners LLC, no broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to

## ARTICLE V. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, the Company shall conduct its
business in the ordinary course and shall not, without Parent's consent, issue any Company Securities
(other than Common Stock upon the exercise of Company Options outstanding on the date hereof), declare
any dividend, or amend the Company Charter.

**5.2 Access.** The Company shall afford Parent reasonable access to its properties, books and records.

**5.3 Exclusivity.** The Company shall not solicit any Acquisition Proposal.

**5.9 Payoff Letter.** The Company shall deliver a customary payoff letter from Harbor Lane Capital, LLC
not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing.

**5.10 280G.** The Company shall seek stockholder approval under Section 280G(b)(5) of the Code with
respect to any payments that would otherwise be parachute payments.

## ARTICLE VI. ADDITIONAL AGREEMENTS

**6.1 Efforts.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to consummate the Merger.

**6.8 D&O Tail.** Prior to the Closing, the Company shall bind a six-year "tail" directors' and
officers' liability insurance policy (the "**D&O Tail Policy**"), the premium for which shall be
payable at the Closing. Fifty percent (50%) of the premium constitutes a Transaction Expense and fifty
percent (50%) shall be borne by Parent.

**6.10 Okafor Transaction Bonus; Tanaka Change-in-Control Bonus.** Parent shall pay, or cause the
surviving corporation to pay, at the Closing, (a) to the extent payable under the letter dated September
30, 2026 and any waiver executed by Ms. Okafor in connection with Section 280G of the Code, the
transaction bonus payable to Dana Okafor, and (b) the change-in-control bonus payable to Mei Tanaka under
her letter dated October 2, 2026. Each such bonus is borne solely by Parent (which shall fund it to the
Company for payment through the Company's payroll), is not a Transaction Expense and shall not reduce
the Closing Merger Consideration.

**6.9 Employees.** Parent shall offer employment to the Company's employees listed on Schedule 6.9.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** No law or order shall prohibit the Merger; any
applicable waiting period under the HSR Act shall have expired or terminated.

**7.2 Conditions to Parent's Obligations.** The representations of the Company shall be true and
correct; the Company shall have performed its covenants; the Payoff Letter shall have been delivered.

**7.3 Conditions to the Company's Obligations.** The representations of Parent shall be true and
correct; Parent shall have performed its covenants.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION; ESCROW

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties shall survive the Closing for fifteen (15) months.

**8.2 Indemnification.** From and after the Closing, the Equityholders shall severally (in proportion
to their Pro Rata Shares) indemnify Parent for Losses arising from breaches of the Company's
representations and covenants, recoverable first from the Escrow Amount.

**8.3 Escrow.** At the Closing, Parent shall deposit the Escrow Amount with Citibank, N.A., as escrow
agent. Any balance remaining fifteen (15) months after the Closing (less pending claims) shall be
released for the benefit of the Equityholders as Additional Consideration.

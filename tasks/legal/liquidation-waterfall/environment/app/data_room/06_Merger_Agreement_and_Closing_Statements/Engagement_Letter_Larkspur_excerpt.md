**LARKSPUR PARTNERS LLC**

January 20, 2026

Board of Directors, Halvorsen Robotics, Inc.

**Engagement Letter (excerpt: Sections 3 and 4)**

**3. Fees.** In consideration of our services, the Company agrees to pay Larkspur:

(a) *Monthly Retainer.* A non-refundable retainer of $25,000 per month, payable in advance, beginning
February 1, 2026 and ending on the earlier of the closing of a Transaction or termination of this
engagement; *provided* that the Company's obligation to pay the Monthly Retainer shall cease after
four (4) monthly payments. The Company has paid all four Monthly Retainer payments ($100,000 in
aggregate).

(b) *Transaction Fee.* Upon the closing of a Transaction, a fee (the "**Transaction Fee**") equal to the
greater of (i) $1,250,000 and (ii) two percent (2.0%) of the Transaction Value. One hundred percent
(100%) of the Monthly Retainer payments previously paid shall be credited against the Transaction Fee.

(c) *Transaction Value.* "**Transaction Value**" means the base purchase price stated in the definitive
agreement for the Transaction (on a cash-free, debt-free basis), without regard to any adjustment for
cash, indebtedness, working capital or transaction expenses, and including any portion placed in escrow
or held back.

**4. Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Larkspur for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses; Larkspur
has confirmed that all expenses through the Closing have been invoiced and paid.

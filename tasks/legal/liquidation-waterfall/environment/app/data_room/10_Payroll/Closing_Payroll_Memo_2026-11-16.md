# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Project Aster working group
**From:** Thomas Ekwueme, Chief Financial Officer; Ridgeline Payroll Services (the Company's payroll provider)
**Date:** November 16, 2026
**Re:** Closing payroll run

1. **One supplemental run.** All Closing compensatory payments to current and former employees (Option
   Cash-Out Amounts, Carve-Out Payments, the Okafor transaction bonus and the Tanaka change-in-control
   bonus) will be paid by the Company, as employer, in a single off-cycle payroll run dated the Closing
   Date, separately identified from regular wages. Parent funds the two bonuses to the Company under
   Merger Agreement Section 6.10. The November 13, 2026 regular payroll is the last payment of wages
   before the Closing; year-to-date figures as of that payroll are in `payroll_ytd_2026-11-13.csv`.
   Federal income tax was withheld from each employee's regular wages in 2026.

2. **Withholding methods.** For federal income tax on supplemental wages the Company uses the optional
   flat rate method wherever the regulations permit it. For California personal income tax the Company
   uses the supplemental-wage flat percentage method, and Ridgeline classifies every Closing payroll
   payment (Option Cash-Out Amounts, Carve-Out Payments and both bonuses) as a bonus or stock option
   payment. Payments are sourced to each employee's work state shown in the year-to-date file. No 401(k)
   or other deferral election, and no voluntary deduction, applies to any Closing payment. Only the
   employee share of payroll taxes is withheld; the employer share is borne by Parent.

3. **Non-employees.** Option Cash-Out Amounts payable to any holder who is not and has never been an
   employee of the Company are paid by accounts payable, without withholding, and reported on Form
   1099-NEC. Each such holder has delivered a valid Form W-9.

4. **Rounding.** Each withholding amount is computed on the total Closing payroll payment to the employee
   and rounded to the nearest cent.

# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Officer's Certificate of Adjustment of Series A Conversion Price
### (delivered under Article Fourth, Part B, Section 4.7 of the Fourth Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation)

Date: September 9, 2021

The undersigned, Chief Financial Officer of Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "Corporation"), certifies
to each holder of Series A Preferred Stock as follows:

1. On September 2, 2021, the Corporation issued 5,000,000 shares of Series B Preferred Stock at a
   price of $1.20 per share (aggregate consideration $6,000,000). The Series B Preferred Stock is a
   Convertible Security, initially convertible into Common Stock on a one-for-one basis, and its
   issuance constituted a deemed issuance of Additional Shares of Common Stock for a consideration
   per share ($1.20) less than the Series A Conversion Price then in effect ($1.50). No waiver under
   Section 4.4.2 was received from the holders of Series A Preferred Stock.

2. Under the broad-based weighted average formula, CP2 = CP1 x (A + B) / (A + C), where:
   - CP1 (Series A Conversion Price in effect immediately prior to the issuance) = $1.50;
   - A (shares of Common Stock outstanding immediately prior to the issuance, treating all
     outstanding Options and Convertible Securities as exercised and converted) = 20,000,000;
   - B (shares that the aggregate consideration received would purchase at CP1: $6,000,000 / $1.50)
     = 4,000,000;
   - C (Additional Shares of Common Stock deemed issued) = 5,000,000.

3. Accordingly, effective September 2, 2021, the Series A Conversion Price was adjusted from $1.50
   to **$1.44** ($1.50 x 24,000,000 / 25,000,000). The Series A Original Issue Price remains $1.50.
   The Series Seed Conversion Price ($0.50) was not adjusted because the consideration per share
   exceeded it.

/s/ Thomas Ekwueme, Chief Financial Officer

---

*Secretary's notation (November 2, 2026):* No other adjustment to the Conversion Price of any series of
Preferred Stock has occurred since September 2, 2021. The Series C financing (February 2024) and the
Convertible Notes (2025) did not result in any adjustment.

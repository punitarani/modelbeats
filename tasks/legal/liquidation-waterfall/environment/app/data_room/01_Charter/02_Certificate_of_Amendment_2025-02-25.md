# CERTIFICATE OF AMENDMENT
# TO THE
# SIXTH AMENDED AND RESTATED CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION
# OF
# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.

Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., a corporation organized and existing under and by virtue of the provisions
of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**Corporation**"),

**DOES HEREBY CERTIFY:**

1. That the name of this corporation is Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., and that this corporation was
originally incorporated pursuant to the General Corporation Law on February 19, 2016 under the name
Halvorsen Robotics, Inc.

2. That the Board of Directors duly adopted resolutions proposing to amend the Sixth Amended and
Restated Certificate of Incorporation of this corporation (the "**Restated Certificate**"), declaring
said amendment to be advisable and in the best interests of this corporation and its stockholders, and
authorizing the appropriate officers of this corporation to solicit the consent of the stockholders
therefor, which resolution setting forth the proposed amendment is as follows:

**RESOLVED**, that the Restated Certificate be amended as follows:

(a) The first proviso of Section 2.4 of Part B of Article Fourth is hereby amended by deleting the
words "three (3) times the Series B Original Issue Price" and substituting therefor the words "two and
one-half (2.5) times the Series B Original Issue Price". The defined term "Maximum Participation
Amount" shall refer to the amount as so amended. Except as set forth in the preceding sentence,
Section 2.4 shall remain unchanged.

3. That thereafter said amendment was duly adopted in accordance with the provisions of Section 242
of the General Corporation Law, including by the written consent of the holders of the requisite
number of shares of the Corporation's capital stock and of each class and series thereof entitled to
vote separately thereon in accordance with Section 228 of the
General Corporation Law, and prompt written notice of such action has been given to the stockholders
that did not so consent.

4. That this Certificate of Amendment shall be effective upon filing.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, this Certificate of Amendment has been executed by a duly authorized officer of
this corporation on this 25th day of February, 2025.

By: /s/ Dana Okafor
Dana Okafor, President and Chief Executive Officer

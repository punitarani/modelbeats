# CERTIFICATE OF AMENDMENT NO. 2
# TO THE
# SIXTH AMENDED AND RESTATED CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION
# OF
# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.

Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., a corporation organized and existing under and by virtue of the provisions
of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**Corporation**"),

**DOES HEREBY CERTIFY:**

1. That the name of this corporation is Halvorsen Robotics, Inc.

2. That the Board of Directors duly adopted resolutions proposing to amend the Sixth Amended and
Restated Certificate of Incorporation of this corporation, as amended (the "**Restated
Certificate**"), declaring said amendment to be advisable and in the best interests of this
corporation and its stockholders, which resolution setting forth the proposed amendment is as
follows:

**RESOLVED**, that the Restated Certificate be amended as follows:

(a) Article Fourth is amended to authorize 6,500,000 shares of a new series of Preferred Stock
designated "Series D Preferred Stock," with an Original Issue Price of $2.40 per share, ranking senior
to all other series of Preferred Stock as set forth in the Series D designation attached as Annex A.

(b) Section 2.4 of Part B of Article Fourth is amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:
"2.4 Distribution of Remaining Assets. After the payment in full of all preferential amounts required
to be paid to the holders of Preferred Stock, the remaining assets of the Corporation available for
distribution to its stockholders or, in the case of a Deemed Liquidation Event, the consideration not
payable to the holders of Preferred Stock pursuant to Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3, shall be distributed
among the holders of the shares of Common Stock, pro rata based on the number of shares held by each
such holder." (The effect of this amendment is that the Series B Preferred Stock no longer
participates in distributions of remaining assets after payment of the Series B Liquidation Amount,
other than as a Converting Series under Section 2.5.)

(c) Conforming changes are made to Section 2.5(a) and (d) to reflect the foregoing.

3. That thereafter said amendment was duly adopted in accordance with the provisions of Section 242
of the General Corporation Law by written consent of the stockholders in accordance with Section 228
of the General Corporation Law.

4. That this Certificate of Amendment shall be effective upon filing.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, this Certificate of Amendment has been executed by a duly authorized officer of
this corporation on this ___ day of __________, 2025.

By: ______________________________
Dana Okafor, President and Chief Executive Officer

## ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE STOCKHOLDERS OF HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
### (September 24, 2025)

The undersigned stockholders of Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., holding (i) shares of capital stock
representing a majority of the voting power of the outstanding capital stock, (ii) a majority of the
outstanding Preferred Stock, (iii) at least sixty percent of the outstanding Series C Preferred Stock
and (iv) a majority of the outstanding Series B Preferred Stock, acting by written consent pursuant to
Section 228 of the General Corporation Law, hereby approve and adopt the Certificate of Amendment No. 2
in the form attached, in connection with, and for the purpose of, the proposed Series D Preferred
Stock financing led by Eastlake Industrial Partners (the "**Series D Financing**"), and authorize the
officers of the Corporation to file the Certificate of Amendment No. 2 with the Secretary of State of
the State of Delaware concurrently with the initial closing of the Series D Financing.

Signed by: Northgate Ventures III, L.P.; Meridian Growth Partners II, L.P.; Calloway Capital Fund II,
L.P.; Redwood Hollow Ventures, L.P.; Sandpiper Seed Fund I, L.P.; Dana Okafor; Marcus Lindqvist.

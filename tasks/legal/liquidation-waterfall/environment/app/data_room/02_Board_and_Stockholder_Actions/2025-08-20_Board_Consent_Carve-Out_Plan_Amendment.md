# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Effective August 20, 2025

**1. Amendment No. 1 to Management Carve-Out Plan.** WHEREAS, Section 9 of the Halvorsen Robotics,
Inc. Management Carve-Out Plan (the "**Carve-Out Plan**") permits the Board to amend the Carve-Out Plan
at any time prior to a Sale Event, provided that no amendment may reduce a Participant's Units without
the Participant's consent;

RESOLVED, that the Carve-Out Plan is hereby amended, effective immediately, as follows:
(a) in Section 3(a), the words "six percent (6%)" are replaced with "eight percent (8%)"; and
(b) in Section 3(b), the amount "$50,000,000" is replaced with "$75,000,000".
All other provisions of the Carve-Out Plan remain in full force and effect.

**2. Sales Commission Plan.** RESOLVED, that the FY2026 Sales Commission Plan presented to the Board is
approved.

**3. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to take all actions necessary to
carry out the foregoing resolutions.

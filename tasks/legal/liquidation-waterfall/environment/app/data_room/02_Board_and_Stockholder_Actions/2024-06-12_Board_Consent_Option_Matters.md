# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Effective June 12, 2024

The undersigned, constituting all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of
Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "**Company**"), hereby adopt the following resolutions by written
consent pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law:

**1. Quarterly Option Grants.** RESOLVED, that the Company grants the stock options listed on Exhibit A
under the Company's 2016 Equity Incentive Plan (the "**Plan**"), each with an exercise price of $1.65
per share, the fair market value of the Common Stock determined below.

**2. 409A Valuation.** RESOLVED, that the Board accepts the valuation report of Alder Valuation Group
dated May 30, 2024, concluding that the fair market value of the Common Stock was $1.65 per share as
of February 29, 2024 (giving effect to the Series C financing) and remains $1.65 per share as of May
15, 2024.

**3. Correction of Grant H-125 (Amara Diallo).** WHEREAS, on March 12, 2024 the Board approved the
grant to Amara Diallo of an option to purchase 200,000 shares of Common Stock (Grant H-125) at an
exercise price of $1.50 per share, being the fair market value under the valuation then most recently
accepted by the Board (as of November 30, 2023); WHEREAS, the valuation accepted in Resolution 2 above
establishes that the fair market value of the Common Stock on March 12, 2024 was $1.65 per share; and
WHEREAS, to avoid the option being treated as granted at a discount for purposes of Section 409A of the
Internal Revenue Code, Ms. Diallo has consented in writing to the correction set forth below;

RESOLVED, that the exercise price of Grant H-125 is hereby corrected to **$1.65 per share**, effective
as of the original date of grant, with all other terms (number of shares, vesting commencement date of
January 31, 2024, vesting schedule and expiration date) unchanged, and the officers of the Company are
directed to amend the Company's equity records accordingly.

**4. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers of the Company are authorized to take all actions
necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Exhibit A (grants): none listed. *[No grants were made under Resolution 1 this quarter.]*

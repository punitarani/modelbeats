# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Written Consent of the Stockholders
### (Section 228 of the Delaware General Corporation Law)

The undersigned stockholders of Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "**Company**"), holding shares of the
Company's capital stock sufficient to approve the actions set forth below, hereby consent to, approve
and adopt the following resolutions:

**Approval of Certificate of Amendment.** RESOLVED, that the Certificate of Amendment to the Sixth
Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation in the form approved by the Board of Directors on
January 14, 2025 (amending the first proviso of Section 2.4 of Part B of Article Fourth to reduce the
Maximum Participation Amount from three (3) times to two and one-half (2.5) times the Series B Original
Issue Price) is hereby approved and adopted; and

RESOLVED FURTHER, that this consent constitutes the approval of the Requisite Preferred Holders under
Section 3.3 of Part B of Article Fourth.

This consent may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic signature.

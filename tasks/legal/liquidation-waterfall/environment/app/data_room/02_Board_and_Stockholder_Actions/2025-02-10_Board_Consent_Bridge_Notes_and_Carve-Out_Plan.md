# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Effective February 10, 2025

The undersigned, constituting all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of
Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. (the "**Company**"), hereby adopt the following resolutions by written consent:

**1. Convertible Note Financing.** RESOLVED, that the Company is authorized to enter into a Note
Purchase Agreement (the "**Note Purchase Agreement**") providing for the issuance of convertible
promissory notes in an aggregate principal amount of up to $6,000,000, substantially in the form
presented to the Board, and to issue notes thereunder to Northgate Ventures III, L.P., Sandpiper Seed
Fund I, L.P. and such other accredited investors as the officers may approve; and that, upon receipt of
the requisite stockholder consents, the officers are authorized to file with the Delaware Secretary of
State the Certificate of Amendment to the Restated Certificate approved by the Board on January 14,
2025 (reducing the Series B Maximum Participation Amount, as requested by the Note purchasers), such
filing to occur before the first Note is issued; and that the officers are directed to obtain from the
holders of a majority of the outstanding Series C Preferred Stock a written agreement under Section
4.4.2 of Part B of Article Fourth that no adjustment to the Series C Conversion Price shall be made as a
result of the issuance or conversion of the Notes.

**2. Management Carve-Out Plan.** RESOLVED, that the Halvorsen Robotics, Inc. Management Carve-Out Plan
in the form attached as Exhibit B (the "**Carve-Out Plan**") is hereby adopted, and the following Units
are awarded under the Carve-Out Plan effective as of the date hereof: Dana Okafor, 35 Units; Priya
Raman, 25 Units; Marcus Lindqvist, 15 Units; Javier Ortiz, 15 Units; Amara Diallo, 10 Units.

**3. Venture Loan Amendment.** RESOLVED, that the Company is authorized to enter into the Second
Amendment to Loan and Security Agreement with Harbor Lane Capital, LLC extending the interest-only
period by six months.

**4. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers of the Company are authorized to take all actions
necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

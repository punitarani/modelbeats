# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Effective September 30, 2026

**1. Merger Agreement.** RESOLVED, that the form of Agreement and Plan of Merger among Aster Automation
Holdings, Inc. ("**Parent**"), Aster Merger Sub, Inc. and the Company, with the Purchase Price to be
finalized by the Pricing Committee (Ms. Okafor and Mr. Kole) within the range presented, is approved,
and the Merger is declared advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders.

**1A. Drag-Along.** RESOLVED, that the Board approves the Merger as a "Sale of the Company" under the
Amended and Restated Voting Agreement dated February 27, 2024 and specifies that Section 3 (Drag-Along
Right) of the Voting Agreement shall apply to the Merger.

**2. Treatment of Options (Plan Administrator Determination).** RESOLVED, that, acting as Administrator
of the 2016 Equity Incentive Plan, the Board elects the treatment of Options in Section 13(c)(i)-(iii)
of the Plan for the Merger, as implemented in Section 2.7 of the Merger Agreement.

**3. Carve-Out Plan.** RESOLVED, that the Board confirms that the Merger will constitute a Sale Event
under the Management Carve-Out Plan, and directs that Carve-Out Payments be calculated and paid at the
Closing in accordance with the Carve-Out Plan as amended.

**4. Director Resignations.** RESOLVED, that each director's resignation, to be effective as of the
Effective Time, is accepted.

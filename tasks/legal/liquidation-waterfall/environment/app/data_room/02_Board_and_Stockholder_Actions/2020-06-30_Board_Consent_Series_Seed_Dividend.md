# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Effective June 30, 2020

The undersigned, constituting all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of
Halvorsen Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), acting pursuant to Section 141(f)
of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopt the following resolutions by written consent:

**1. Approval of Minutes.** RESOLVED, that the minutes of the meeting of the Board held on May 21,
2020 are hereby approved.

**2. Series Seed Dividend.** WHEREAS, in connection with the bridge financing provided by the holders
of Series Seed Preferred Stock in March 2020, the Board agreed to consider a one-time dividend on the
Series Seed Preferred Stock;

RESOLVED, that a cash dividend of **$0.04 per share** is hereby declared on each share of Series Seed
Preferred Stock outstanding as of the date of this consent (4,000,000 shares; aggregate $160,000),
payable on such date as the Board may later fix and, if not earlier paid, payable as part of the Series
Seed Liquidation Amount upon any liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Company or Deemed
Liquidation Event, in each case subject to the Company's Certificate of Incorporation; and

RESOLVED FURTHER, that no dividend is declared on any other class or series of capital stock.

**3. 409A Valuation.** RESOLVED, that the Board accepts the valuation report of Alder Valuation Group
dated June 15, 2020 concluding that the fair market value of the Common Stock is $0.12 per share as of
May 31, 2020.

**4. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers of the Company are authorized to take all such
further action as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Directors: /s/ Dana Okafor  /s/ Marcus Lindqvist  /s/ Grace Whitfield (Sandpiper)

---
*Secretary's notation (November 2, 2026): No payment date was ever fixed; the dividend remains unpaid.
No other dividend has been declared on any class or series of the Company's capital stock.*

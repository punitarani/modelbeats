# HALVORSEN ROBOTICS, INC.
## Minutes of a Meeting of the Board of Directors (excerpt)
### January 16, 2026 (by videoconference)

Present: Dana Okafor (Chair), Marcus Lindqvist, Grace Whitfield, Adrian Kole, Lena Brooks, Helen Choi. Also present:
Thomas Ekwueme (CFO); Hannah Varga (Whitcombe & Varga LLP, Company counsel).

**Series D Financing.** Ms. Okafor reported that Eastlake Industrial Partners had delivered written
notice on January 9, 2026 terminating the Series D term sheet and exclusivity, citing its fund's
reallocation away from industrial automation, and that no definitive Series D agreements had been
signed. Ms. Varga noted that the stockholder consent of September 24, 2025 had authorized the
Certificate of Amendment No. 2 to be filed concurrently with the initial Series D closing. Discussion
followed regarding alternatives, including a strategic sale process.

**Strategic Alternatives.** Upon motion duly made and seconded, the Board authorized management to
engage Larkspur Partners LLC as financial advisor in connection with a potential sale of the Company.

**FY2026 Budget.** Mr. Ekwueme presented the FY2026 operating budget, which was approved.

**409A Valuation.** The Board accepted the valuation of Alder Valuation Group as of December 31, 2025
($2.10 per share of Common Stock).

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.

/s/ Hannah Varga, Secretary of the Meeting

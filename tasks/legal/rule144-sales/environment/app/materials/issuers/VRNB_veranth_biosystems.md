## Corporate and reporting status

- Delaware corporation; common stock listed on The Nasdaq Global Market. One class of common stock.
- IPO registration statement on Form S-1 effective June 17, 2021; common stock registered under
  Exchange Act Section 12(b) the same day. Subject to Section 13 reporting continuously since then.
- Every Exchange Act report due since the IPO has been filed on time, with all required Interactive Data
  Files. The Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 is not yet due.
- Operating biotechnology company (two clinical programs and a diagnostics business). Has never been a
  shell company.
- Forms S-8 cover the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan and the 2021 Employee Stock Purchase Plan only.

# Issuer file: Brisa Energy Partners, Inc. (OTCQX: BRSA)

Compiled by Cedar Hill Securities, Restricted Stock Services. Status as of October 21, 2026.

- Texas corporation. Its common stock has never been registered under Section 12 of the Exchange Act,
  and it has never filed a Securities Act registration statement; it is not required to file reports
  under Section 13 or 15(d) of the Exchange Act.
- Because a covenant in its senior notes indenture requires it, Brisa voluntarily files annual reports on
  Form 10-K and quarterly reports on Form 10-Q with the SEC on EDGAR, and has filed every such report on
  time since 2019. The common stock is quoted on the OTCQX Best Market.
- Operating company; never a shell.
- **Affiliates** (Brisa's counsel, October 12, 2026): officers and directors. The Calder Family Trust
  is not, and never has been, an affiliate.

# Issuer file: Northgate Fintech Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: NGFT)

Compiled by Cedar Hill Securities, Restricted Stock Services. Status as of October 21, 2026.

- Formerly **Ironwood Acquisition Corp.**, a special purpose acquisition company that completed its IPO
  in 2023, held only the cash in its trust account, and had no operations.
- **Business combination.** On December 10, 2025, Ironwood merged its wholly owned subsidiary with and
  into Northgate Payments, Inc., a private operating company, after Northgate Payments' stockholders
  approved the merger by written consent and Ironwood's stockholders approved it at a special meeting
  on December 8, 2025. Ironwood (renamed Northgate Fintech Holdings, Inc.) issued its common stock to
  Northgate Payments' stockholders under a registration statement on Form S-4 declared effective
  November 6, 2025. Concurrently, Ironwood sold 4,000,000 shares to PIPE investors for cash in a private
  placement under Section 4(a)(2), closing December 10, 2025.
- On **December 16, 2025** the Company filed a Form 8-K containing the information that would be
  required in a Form 10 registration statement for its common stock ("Form 10 information"), reflecting
  that it was no longer a shell company. It has been an operating company since the merger.
- The Company has been subject to Section 13 reporting since Ironwood's 2023 IPO and has filed every
  required report (other than Forms 8-K, which it has also filed) on time during the past 12 months.
- **Affiliates** (letter from Northgate's general counsel, October 15, 2026): current executive
  officers and directors and Ironwood's former sponsor. Ellis Moreau and Halcyon Credit Opportunities
  Fund are not, and never have been, affiliates of NGFT, Ironwood or Northgate Payments.

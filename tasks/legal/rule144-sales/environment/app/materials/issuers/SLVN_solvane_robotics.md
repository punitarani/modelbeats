# Issuer file: Solvane Robotics, Inc. (Nasdaq: SLVN)

Compiled by Cedar Hill Securities, Restricted Stock Services. Status as of October 21, 2026.

- Delaware corporation; privately held until its IPO. IPO registration statement on Form S-1 declared
  effective **August 3, 2026**; common stock registered under Exchange Act Section 12(b) and listed on
  The Nasdaq Global Select Market the same day. Before August 3, 2026 the Company had never been subject
  to Exchange Act reporting and had never filed any Exchange Act report.
- Since the IPO it has filed every required report on time (a Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30,
  2026 was filed on September 1, 2026, plus several Forms 8-K). Operating company; never a shell.
- Pre-IPO equity compensation was granted under the 2019 Stock Plan in reliance on Rule 701. The Company
  filed a Form S-8 on August 10, 2026 covering future grants under its 2026 Equity Incentive Plan; the
  S-8 does not cover the 2019 Stock Plan or any shares already issued under it.
- All pre-IPO preferred stock converted automatically into common stock at the IPO closing (August 5,
  2026) under the certificate of incorporation, without any payment by holders.
- **Lock-up.** The IPO underwriters released all IPO lock-up agreements in full, for all holders, on
  October 1, 2026.
- **Affiliates** (letter from Solvane's general counsel, October 14, 2026): executive officers
  (including Priya Shah, CFO) and directors. Mateo Ruiz and Granite Seed Fund II, L.P. are not, and never
  have been, affiliates.

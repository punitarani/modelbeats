## Montana Fourth Judicial District Court, Missoula County — Decree of Adoption

In the Matter of the Adoption of Benjamin Pike, a minor. Cause No. DA-20-0094. Decree entered March 16,
2020.

The Court finds that both parents of the child, Lily Rasmussen and Jordan Pike, died on August 11,
2019; that petitioners Joan Webb Carver and Richard Carver are husband and wife; that petitioner Joan
Webb Carver is the sister of the child's maternal grandmother, Nancy Webb; and that petitioners have had
custody of the child as his guardians since August 2019.

IT IS DECREED that the child is the adopted child of petitioners Joan Webb Carver and Richard Carver,
to be known as Benjamin Pike Carver.

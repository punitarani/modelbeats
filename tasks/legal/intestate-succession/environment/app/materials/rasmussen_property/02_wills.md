## Last Will and Testament of Arne Nils Rasmussen (executed April 18, 2012; admitted to informal probate, Montana Tenth Judicial District Court, Fergus County, Cause No. DP-20-0061)

I, ARNE NILS RASMUSSEN, of Fergus County, Montana, make this my will and revoke all prior wills and
codicils.

**ARTICLE I. Family.** I am married to Helen Dahl Rasmussen. I have one child, my son Mark Arne
Rasmussen. Helen's son, Peter Lund, is my stepson.

**ARTICLE II. Ranch.** I give an undivided one-half interest in my ranch in Fergus County, Montana,
described in Exhibit A, together with all improvements and water rights appurtenant to it, to my son,
Mark Arne Rasmussen.

**ARTICLE III. Ranch.** I give the other undivided one-half interest in my ranch in Fergus County,
Montana, described in Exhibit A, together with all improvements and water rights appurtenant to it, to
my stepson, Peter Lund, if he survives me.

**ARTICLE IV. North Dakota land.** I give all of my land in McKenzie County, North Dakota, described in
Exhibit B, including all oil, gas and other minerals in and under it, to my son, Mark Arne Rasmussen.

**ARTICLE V. Residue.** I give all the rest, residue and remainder of my estate, of whatever kind and
wherever located, to my wife, Helen Dahl Rasmussen. If Helen does not survive me, I give my residuary
estate to my son, Mark Arne Rasmussen.

**ARTICLE VI. Personal representative.** I nominate my wife, Helen Dahl Rasmussen, as personal
representative, and my son, Mark Arne Rasmussen, as alternate. No bond shall be required.

Signed April 18, 2012, at Lewistown, Montana. /s/ Arne N. Rasmussen. Attested by two witnesses and made
self-proved by affidavit.

*(Exhibits A and B, the legal descriptions, are the same as in the deeds in 05_title_records.md.)*

## Last Will and Testament of Mark Arne Rasmussen (executed March 3, 2021; admitted to informal probate, Montana Fourth Judicial District Court, Missoula County, Cause No. DP-24-0033)

I, MARK ARNE RASMUSSEN, of Missoula, Montana, make this my will and revoke all prior wills and codicils.

**ARTICLE I. Family.** I am married to Dana Moss Rasmussen. My daughter, Lily Rasmussen, died in 2019.
Dana's son, Kyle Moss, is my stepson.

**ARTICLE II. Disposition.** I give all of my property, real and personal, wherever located, to my wife,
Dana Moss Rasmussen. If Dana does not survive me, I give all of my property to her son, Kyle Moss.

**ARTICLE III. Personal representative.** I nominate Dana Moss Rasmussen as personal representative,
and Kyle Moss as alternate.

Signed March 3, 2021, at Missoula, Montana. /s/ Mark A. Rasmussen. Attested by two witnesses and made
self-proved by affidavit.

*Probate file note:* Neither Dana Moss nor Kyle Moss has been appointed. On the petition of Owen
Haines, the court appointed Missoula attorney Teresa Valdez as special administrator, then as personal
representative, on April 2, 2024. No codicil or later will has been found.

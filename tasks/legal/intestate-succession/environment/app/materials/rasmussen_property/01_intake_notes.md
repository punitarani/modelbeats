# Castellano & Ruiz LLP — Matter Intake Notes

**Matter:** Rasmussen family land: ownership determination (Fergus County, Montana ranch; McKenzie
County, North Dakota tract)
**Client:** Sara Lund Price
**Prepared by:** J. Okonkwo, paralegal. **Last updated:** October 9, 2026

*Working notes from client meetings and calls. Not verified against source documents.*

- Sara wants to know who owns the Rasmussen ranch near Lewistown today, because she wants to buy out
  or partition. Separately, Bakken Ridge Energy wants to lease the minerals under the North Dakota
  tract and needs to know whom to sign up. The North Dakota tract was always a separate property
  from the ranch.
- Arne Rasmussen (Sara's step-grandfather) owned both properties outright. He died in May 2020 with a
  2012 will. His widow Helen (Sara's grandmother) was his personal representative. Helen died in 2023
  before finishing. Arne's estate is still open. Nothing was ever deeded out of it.
- Sara's father Peter (Helen's son, Arne's stepson) died in 2018, two years before Arne. Helen's
  family has always assumed Peter's share of the ranch "lapsed" because the will said "if he survives
  me," and so went to Helen under the residuary clause. Helen's will left everything to her church.
- Arne's son Mark died in January 2024. Mark had a will leaving everything to his wife Dana, or to
  Dana's son Kyle. Mark and Dana divorced in 2023, but Mark never changed his will.
- Mark's daughter Lily and her partner Jordan were killed in a crash in 2019. Their little boy Ben was
  later adopted by Lily's aunt Joan and Joan's husband. Mark's half-brother Owen Haines says he is
  Mark's heir.
- Sara's brother Vince died in 2022 (no will; divorced; two kids, Leah and Eli). Her brother Tom died
  in March 2025 (no will; never married; no kids). Sara signed a disclaimer in Tom's estate. She
  thought that would send her share to Vince's kids.
- Nobody has sold, mortgaged, leased or deeded any interest in either property since Arne's death.

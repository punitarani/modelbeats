## Montana Eighth Judicial District Court, Cascade County — Judgment of Paternity and Support (certified copy)

State of Montana, Child Support Enforcement Division, ex rel. Paula C. Moreau v. Gary Wayne Pruitt.
Cause No. DR-94-0712. Judgment entered November 28, 1994.

Respondent denied that he is the father of the minor child Daniel Luis Moreau and refused to submit to
testing until ordered. Genetic testing shows a probability of paternity of 99.97%. IT IS ADJUDGED that
Gary Wayne Pruitt is the father of the minor child. Respondent shall pay child support of $240.00 per
month beginning December 1, 1994.

## Same court and cause — Order on Motion for Contempt (certified copy), entered August 9, 1999

The Court finds that respondent Gary Wayne Pruitt has had the ability to pay, has paid nothing under
the judgment of November 28, 1994, has told the Child Support Enforcement Division in writing that he
"will never pay for a child that isn't mine," and has never visited or communicated with the child.
Respondent is in contempt. Arrearage of $13,440.00 is reduced to judgment.

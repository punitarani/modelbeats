# M05 — Rivera v. Castellane Builders Corp.

**Court:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Kings, Index No. 501234/2022
**Our client:** Hector Rivera (plaintiff; judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Nature of the case

Construction-site personal injury action (fall from a scaffold, April 11, 2021). Castellane Builders
Corp. is a private New York corporation. The trial was bifurcated.

## 2. Trial history

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2024-03-06 | Jury verdict on liability: Castellane 100% at fault. |
| 2024-04-02 | Interlocutory judgment on liability entered (not appealed). |
| 2024-10-22 | Jury verdict on damages (see below). |
| 2025-01-14 | Final judgment entered. |

Damages verdict (October 22, 2024):

| Element | Amount |
|---|---|
| Past pain and suffering (to verdict) | $650,000.00 |
| Past lost earnings | $180,000.00 |
| Past medical expenses | $95,000.00 |
| Future pain and suffering | $0.00 |
| Future lost earnings | $0.00 |
| Future medical expenses | $0.00 |
| **Total** | **$925,000.00** |

No post-trial motion altered the verdict. No collateral-source reduction was sought.

## 3. Final judgment, entered January 14, 2025

> ADJUDGED that plaintiff Hector Rivera recover of defendant Castellane Builders Corp. the sum of
> $925,000.00, together with interest thereon as allowed by law to the date of entry of this judgment,
> and that plaintiff have execution therefor. Costs and disbursements waived.

The interest amount was not computed on the entered judgment.

## 4. Payments

None. Castellane's carrier has disclaimed coverage; coverage litigation is pending.

## 5. Letter from Castellane's counsel, June 9, 2026 (excerpt)

> "Pre-judgment interest can run only from the damages verdict of October 22, 2024; until then there
> was no sum awarded on which interest could run."

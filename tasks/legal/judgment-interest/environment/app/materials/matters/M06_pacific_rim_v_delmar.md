# M06 — Pacific Rim Coatings, Inc. v. Delmar Fabrication Co.

**Court:** Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles, Case No. 23STCV19876
**Our client:** Pacific Rim Coatings, Inc. (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Distribution Agreement dated March 1, 2021 (excerpts)

> **§ 5.1 Payment.** Distributor shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.
>
> **§ 5.4 Late Payment.** Any amount not paid when due shall bear interest at the rate of seven and
> one-half percent (7.5%) per annum, simple interest, from its due date until paid.
>
> **§ 14.2 Governing Law.** California law governs this Agreement.

Delmar Fabrication Co. is a California corporation; the Agreement is a commercial agreement for the
resale of industrial coatings.

## 2. The debt

Invoice PRC-88104 dated March 31, 2023 for $412,000.00, due April 30, 2023, unpaid.

## 3. Order granting summary judgment and Judgment, both entered July 15, 2024

> "Plaintiff's motion for summary judgment is GRANTED. JUDGMENT is entered for plaintiff Pacific Rim
> Coatings, Inc. and against defendant Delmar Fabrication Co. in the principal amount of $412,000.00,
> plus prejudgment interest as provided by law from April 30, 2023 to the date of this judgment. The
> judgment shall bear interest as provided by law. Each party shall bear its own costs."

No appeal. No memorandum of costs after judgment has been filed, and no levy has been made.

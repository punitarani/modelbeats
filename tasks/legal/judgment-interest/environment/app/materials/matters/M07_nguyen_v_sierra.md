# M07 — Nguyen v. Sierra Freightways, Inc.

**Court:** Superior Court of California, County of Sacramento, Case No. 34-2021-00309871
**Our client:** Linh Nguyen (plaintiff; judgment creditor)
**Assignment for this matter:** only the interest the judgment bears for the period before its entry
(the post-judgment payoff is being handled separately).

---

## 1. The claim

Personal injury action arising from a collision on Interstate 80 on September 3, 2020, between
Ms. Nguyen's car and a tractor-trailer owned by Sierra Freightways, Inc. (a private Nevada
corporation) and driven by its employee. Complaint filed August 30, 2021.

## 2. Settlement communications

| Date | Communication | Delivery | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-03-02 | Letter from plaintiff's counsel demanding $800,000 to settle all claims. The letter does not refer to Code of Civil Procedure section 998 and contains no provision for acceptance. | Email | Rejected by letter |
| 2022-08-10 | "Plaintiff's Offer to Compromise Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 998": judgment against Sierra for $1,500,000, each side to bear its own costs, with an acceptance form and signature line. | Personally served on defense counsel that day | Not accepted; expired |
| 2023-06-05 | "Plaintiff's Second Offer to Compromise Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 998": judgment against Sierra for $950,000, each side to bear its own costs, with an acceptance form and signature line. | Personally served on defense counsel that day | Not accepted; expired |
| 2023-11-14 | Defendant's offer under § 998 of $600,000 | Served by email | Not accepted |

All parties agree both of plaintiff's § 998 offers were valid in form and made in good faith.

## 3. Verdict (March 29, 2024) and judgment (entered April 22, 2024)

| Element | Amount |
|---|---|
| Past medical expenses | $160,000.00 |
| Past lost earnings | $260,000.00 |
| Past non-economic damages | $700,000.00 |
| Future damages of any kind | $0.00 |
| Compensatory total | $1,120,000.00 |
| Punitive damages (Civil Code § 3294) | $300,000.00 |

> JUDGMENT: Plaintiff Linh Nguyen shall recover from defendant Sierra Freightways, Inc. compensatory
> damages of $1,120,000.00 and punitive damages of $300,000.00, together with interest as provided by
> law. Plaintiff's costs were resolved by stipulation and are not part of this judgment.

The judgment did not compute any interest figure.

## 4. Defense position (letter of May 6, 2024)

> "No interest is owed for any period before judgment: the judgment is less than plaintiff's first
> section 998 offer of $1.5 million, so Civil Code section 3291 never came into play."

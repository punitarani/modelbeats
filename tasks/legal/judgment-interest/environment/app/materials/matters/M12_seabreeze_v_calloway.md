# M12 — Seabreeze Marine Holdings, LLC v. Calloway Yacht Services, Inc.

**Court:** Circuit Court of the Eleventh Judicial Circuit in and for Miami-Dade County, Florida,
Case No. 2023-018842-CA-01
**Our client:** Seabreeze Marine Holdings, LLC (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Yacht Management Agreement (excerpt)

> **14.3 Attorneys' Fees.** In any action arising out of this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be
> entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees from the non-prevailing party.

## 2. Final Judgment, entered November 18, 2025 (excerpts)

> 1. Plaintiff, SEABREEZE MARINE HOLDINGS, LLC, shall recover from Defendant, CALLOWAY YACHT SERVICES,
> INC., the sum of $1,265,000.00 (comprising damages of $1,102,450.00 and prejudgment interest through
> the date of this judgment of $162,550.00), which sum shall bear interest at the rate of 8.65% per
> annum, for which let execution issue.
>
> 2. Plaintiff is the prevailing party and is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees from
> Defendant under section 14.3 of the Agreement. The Court reserves jurisdiction to determine the
> amount of the fees.

## 3. Final Judgment for Attorneys' Fees, entered December 17, 2025 (excerpt)

> Plaintiff shall recover from Defendant attorneys' fees in the amount of $214,600.00, together with
> prejudgment interest on those fees as allowed by law, for all of which let execution issue. The sums
> awarded by this judgment shall bear interest at the rate of 8.65% per annum.

The amount of prejudgment interest on the fees was left blank in the signed judgment.

Neither judgment was appealed. Costs were waived by stipulation. Neither judgment was entered by the
clerk under sections 55.141, 61.14, 938.29 or 938.30.

## 4. Payments

None.

## 5. Letter from Calloway's counsel, October 5, 2026 (excerpt)

> "No interest of any kind is owed on the fees for any day before the fee judgment was entered on
> December 17, 2025, because the amount of the fees was not fixed until then; the 'prejudgment
> interest as allowed by law' in that judgment is therefore zero. And the rate on both judgments is
> the 8.65% written on their face."

# M04 — Tribeca Lofts Owner LLC v. Halvard Design Studio LLC

**Court:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of New York, Index No. 654321/2023
**Our client:** Tribeca Lofts Owner LLC (landlord; judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Lease (excerpts)

Commercial lease of the 6th floor of 88 Franklin Street, New York, for use as a design studio. Base
rent of $48,000.00 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month. The lease has no
clause providing for interest on unpaid rent. (Article 37's late charge was withdrawn at trial.)

## 2. Unpaid rent (stipulated at trial)

Halvard paid no rent for the seven months June through December 2023: seven installments of
$48,000.00, due June 1, July 1, August 1, September 1, October 1, November 1 and December 1, 2023
(total $336,000.00). Halvard surrendered the premises on December 31, 2023.

## 3. Decision after nonjury trial, dated and filed September 10, 2024

> "Plaintiff is awarded unpaid rent of $336,000.00, with statutory interest on each monthly installment
> from the date it fell due. The claim for late charges is deemed withdrawn. Submit judgment."

## 4. Judgment, entered November 25, 2024

> ADJUDGED that plaintiff Tribeca Lofts Owner LLC recover of defendant Halvard Design Studio LLC the
> sum of $336,000.00, together with interest as allowed by law to the date of entry of this judgment,
> and that plaintiff have execution therefor. No costs.

The amount of interest was not filled in by the clerk.

## 5. Payments

None.

## 6. Letter from Halvard's counsel, August 3, 2026 (excerpt)

> "Your client is entitled to 9% simple interest on each installment from its due date until the date
> of the trial decision, and to 9% on $336,000 from entry of judgment. Nothing more."

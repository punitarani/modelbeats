# M13 — Doherty v. Granite State Retail Corp.

**Court:** United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, No. 1:23-cv-10822 (removed
from the Middlesex County Superior Court, Civil Action No. 2381CV00517)
**Basis of federal jurisdiction:** diversity of citizenship (Doherty is a Massachusetts citizen;
Granite State Retail Corp. is a New Hampshire corporation with its principal place of business there)
**Our client:** Maureen Doherty (plaintiff; judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Chronology

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2022-01-15 | Ms. Doherty slips on an unremoved ice patch at the entrance of Granite State's store in Woburn, Massachusetts, fracturing her hip. |
| 2022-02-08 | Letter from her counsel to Granite State demanding $2,000,000. |
| 2023-03-03 | Complaint filed (electronically, accepted and docketed the same day) with the entry fee, in the Middlesex County Superior Court. |
| 2023-03-20 | Granite State served with summons and complaint. |
| 2023-04-12 | Granite State files its notice of removal in the District of Massachusetts. |
| 2025-08-11 | (Monday) Jury returns a general verdict for plaintiff of $1,150,000.00 for her personal injuries; the Clerk enters judgment on the verdict the same day. |

## 2. Judgment (Dkt. 112), entered Monday, August 11, 2025

> In accordance with the jury's verdict, judgment is entered for plaintiff Maureen Doherty against
> defendant Granite State Retail Corp. in the amount of $1,150,000.00, plus prejudgment interest as
> provided by law, plus post-judgment interest as provided by law. Costs waived by stipulation.

The Clerk's interest computation was not docketed. No post-trial motion was filed; no appeal.

None.

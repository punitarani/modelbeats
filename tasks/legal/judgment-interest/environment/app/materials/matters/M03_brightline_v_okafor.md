# M03 — Brightline Home Renovations LLC v. Daniel Okafor and Teresa Okafor

**Court:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Suffolk, Index No. 612345/2023
**Our client:** Brightline Home Renovations LLC (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Home Improvement Contract dated June 6, 2022 (excerpts)

> **Owners:** Daniel Okafor and Teresa Okafor, 41 Bayberry Lane, Huntington, New York (the Owners'
> residence). **Contractor:** Brightline Home Renovations LLC, Lic. No. HI-58210.
>
> **Scope of Work:** Complete renovation of the kitchen and the two upstairs bathrooms of the Owners'
> home, per the attached plans.
>
> **Contract Price:** $214,500.00, payable: $42,900 on signing; $85,100 on completion of rough-in;
> and the balance of $86,500 within fifteen days after Contractor's invoice for substantial completion.
>
> **Governing Law:** New York.

The contract has no interest or late-charge clause.

## 2. Facts found after the nonjury trial

- Brightline invoiced substantial completion on February 28, 2023; the $86,500 balance fell due on
  March 15, 2023 and was never paid. The Okafors used the renovated rooms as part of their family home
  throughout; the house has never been rented or used for business.
- The Okafors' counterclaim for defective work was dismissed.

## 3. Decision and Order after nonjury trial, dated and filed November 19, 2024

> "... Plaintiff has established its entitlement to the contract balance of $86,500.00. Plaintiff is
> awarded $86,500.00, with interest from March 15, 2023 at the statutory rate. Settle judgment on
> notice."

## 4. Judgment, entered by the County Clerk on December 20, 2024

> ADJUDGED that plaintiff Brightline Home Renovations LLC recover of defendants Daniel Okafor and
> Teresa Okafor, jointly and severally, the sum of $86,500.00, with interest as allowed by law to the
> date of entry of this judgment, and that plaintiff have execution therefor. Costs and disbursements
> waived by plaintiff.

The clerk's interest computation was left blank on the entered judgment.

## 5. Payments

None. The Okafors have paid nothing on the judgment.

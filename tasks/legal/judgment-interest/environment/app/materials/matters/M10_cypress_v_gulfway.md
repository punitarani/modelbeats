# M10 — Cypress Cold Storage, LLC v. Gulfway Electrical Contractors, Inc.

**Court:** 285th District Court, Bexar County, Texas, Cause No. 2023-CI-11406
**Our client:** Gulfway Electrical Contractors, Inc. (defendant; judgment debtor)

---

## 1. The claim

Property-damage action. On January 9, 2023, a fire destroyed refrigeration equipment and stored
inventory at Cypress Cold Storage's warehouse in San Antonio. Cypress sued Gulfway (which had
rewired the compressor room) and Bexar Sprinkler Co. (which had serviced the sprinkler system).

## 2. Chronology

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2023-03-20 | Gulfway receives Cypress's written notice of claim (certified letter, return receipt). |
| 2023-06-01 | Cypress files suit against Gulfway and Bexar Sprinkler. |
| 2024-08-15 | Bexar Sprinkler settles; Cypress receives Bexar Sprinkler's $600,000.00 settlement payment that day. Bexar Sprinkler is dismissed. |
| 2025-10-21 | Jury verdict: Cypress's property damage $1,840,000.00 (all incurred on January 9, 2023; no future damages). |
| 2025-11-06 | Final Judgment signed and rendered against Gulfway. |

No settlement offer was ever made by Gulfway. The parties stipulated (and the court ordered) that
Gulfway is entitled to a dollar-for-dollar settlement credit for Bexar Sprinkler's $600,000.00
payment, applied in the manner Texas law requires, and that each party bears its own costs.

## 3. Final Judgment (signed November 6, 2025) — decretal paragraph

> It is ORDERED that plaintiff Cypress Cold Storage, LLC recover from defendant Gulfway Electrical
> Contractors, Inc. its actual damages of $1,840,000.00 as found by the jury, less the settlement
> credit of $600,000.00, together with prejudgment interest, both computed and applied as provided by
> law, and post-judgment interest on all sums awarded as provided by law until paid. Each party shall
> bear its own costs.

The judgment leaves the computed figures blank. Cypress's proposed form of judgment (rejected for
other reasons) computed the award as $1,240,000.00 net damages plus prejudgment interest on
$1,240,000.00 from June 1, 2023.

## 4. Payments

None since judgment. Gulfway intends to pay the judgment in full on November 16, 2026.

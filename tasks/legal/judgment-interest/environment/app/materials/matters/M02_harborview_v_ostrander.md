# M02 — Harborview Capital Partners LP v. Ostrander Hospitality Group, Inc.

**Court:** United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, No. 1:23-cv-07412 (LGS);
appeal, United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, No. 24-2655
**Basis of jurisdiction:** diversity of citizenship, 28 U.S.C. § 1332
**Our client:** Harborview Capital Partners LP (lender; judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Promissory Note dated July 1, 2020 (excerpts)

> FOR VALUE RECEIVED, Ostrander Hospitality Group, Inc. ("Borrower") promises to pay to the order of
> Harborview Capital Partners LP ("Lender") the principal sum of TWO MILLION FOUR HUNDRED THOUSAND
> DOLLARS ($2,400,000.00), together with interest as provided below.
>
> **1. Interest.** Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal balance at the rate of thirteen and
> one-half percent (13.5%) per annum, computed on the basis of a 365-day year for the actual number of
> days elapsed, until the principal is paid in full. Accrued interest is payable monthly in arrears on
> the last day of each month.
>
> **2. Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal balance and all accrued and unpaid interest shall be due
> and payable on June 30, 2023 (the "Maturity Date").
>
> **7. Costs.** Borrower shall pay all costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees.
>
> **9. Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
> the State of New York.

The Note contains no other interest provision.

## 2. Payment history before suit

Borrower paid every monthly interest installment through and including the installment due June 30,
2023, which covered all interest accrued through June 30, 2023. Borrower paid no principal at
maturity and has paid nothing on the Note since then. Lender waived its claim for collection costs
and attorneys' fees by stipulation (Dkt. 40).

## 3. Judgment (Dkt. 58), entered Friday, September 20, 2024

> ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED: For the reasons stated in the Court's Opinion and Order of this date,
> plaintiff Harborview Capital Partners LP shall recover from defendant Ostrander Hospitality Group,
> Inc. the unpaid principal of $2,400,000.00, together with interest thereon as allowed by law from
> July 1, 2023 to the date of this Judgment, and post-judgment interest as provided by law.

The Opinion and Order (Dkt. 57) holds Ostrander liable on the Note and rejects every defense. It
does not compute interest.

## 4. Appeal

- Ostrander filed a notice of appeal on October 4, 2024 and posted a supersedeas bond (Dkt. 63).
- **July 23, 2025:** Second Circuit summary order: "the judgment of the district court is AFFIRMED."
- **August 13, 2025:** mandate issued.

## 6. Letter from Ostrander's counsel, September 2, 2025 (excerpt)

> "Because the Second Circuit's mandate did not issue until August 13, 2025, post-judgment interest
> can run, if at all, only from that date, at the rate for the week preceding the mandate. Interest

> 2023 and its interest clause did not survive maturity."

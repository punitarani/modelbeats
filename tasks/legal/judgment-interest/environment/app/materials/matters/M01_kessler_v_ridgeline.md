# M01 — Kessler Industrial Supply Co. v. Ridgeline Distribution LLC

**Court:** United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, No. 1:23-cv-02817 (JPC)
**Basis of jurisdiction:** diversity of citizenship, 28 U.S.C. § 1332 (Kessler is a Pennsylvania
corporation; Ridgeline is a Delaware LLC whose members are citizens of New Jersey)
**Our client:** Kessler Industrial Supply Co. (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Supply Agreement (excerpts), dated February 1, 2021

> **§ 4.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice each shipment on delivery. Distributor shall
> pay each invoice in full, without setoff, within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Each invoice
> shall state its due date.
>
> **§ 4.3 Disputed Invoices.** Distributor may dispute an invoice in good faith by written notice within
> ten (10) days after receipt, specifying the disputed items.
>
> **§ 12.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without
> regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
>
> **§ 12.9 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties ...

The Agreement contains no other provision on payment, late charges, or interest.

## 3. Opinion and Order (Dkt. 61) and Judgment (Dkt. 62), both entered Thursday, March 14, 2024

From the Opinion and Order: "Kessler's motion for summary judgment on its claim for breach of the
Supply Agreement is GRANTED. ... Under New York law, which the parties agree governs, Kessler is
entitled to prejudgment interest as of right on each unpaid invoice from the date that invoice fell
due."

Judgment (Dkt. 62), in full:

> It is hereby ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that plaintiff Kessler Industrial Supply Co. recover from
> defendant Ridgeline Distribution LLC the sum of $825,000.00, together with prejudgment interest as
> allowed by law on each unpaid invoice from its due date to the date of this Judgment, and
> post-judgment interest as provided by law. The parties shall bear their own costs.
>
> Dated: New York, New York, March 14, 2024. Entered on the docket March 14, 2024.

No appeal was taken. The Clerk never docketed a computation of the interest components.

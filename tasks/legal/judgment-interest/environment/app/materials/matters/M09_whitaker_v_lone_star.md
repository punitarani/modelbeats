# M09 — Whitaker v. Lone Star Aggregate Hauling, LLC

**Court:** 151st District Court, Harris County, Texas, Cause No. 2022-63518
**Our client:** Darnell Whitaker (plaintiff; judgment creditor)

---

## 1. The claim

Personal injury action. On November 14, 2021, a dump truck owned by Lone Star Aggregate Hauling, LLC
and driven by its employee ran a red light in Pasadena, Texas and struck Mr. Whitaker's pickup.

## 2. Pre-suit and litigation chronology

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2021-11-20 | Mrs. Whitaker telephones Lone Star's dispatcher to report the crash and her husband's injuries. Nothing in writing. |
| 2022-02-07 | Letter from plaintiff's counsel to Lone Star, sent by certified mail, return receipt requested: describes the crash, Mr. Whitaker's injuries and treatment, and states that he asserts a claim against Lone Star for his damages. |
| 2022-02-10 | USPS return receipt signed by Lone Star's office manager (delivery of the February 7 letter). |
| 2022-10-03 | Original Petition filed. |
| 2023-05-15 | Lone Star's written settlement offer of $400,000.00 cash, delivered by email to plaintiff's counsel, stating: "This offer will remain open through June 30, 2023." |
| 2023-06-30 | The offer lapses; plaintiff did not respond. |
| 2023-11-02 | Mediation. In caucus, Lone Star's adjuster tells the mediator Lone Star will pay $500,000; the mediator conveys the figure orally to plaintiff's counsel. No writing records the offer; the mediator's report states only that the case did not settle. |
| 2024-09-11 | Jury verdict (see below). |
| 2024-09-26 | Final Judgment signed and rendered. |

## 3. Verdict

| Element | Amount |
|---|---|
| Past medical expenses | $212,400.00 |
| Past lost earnings | $96,300.00 |
| Past physical pain and mental anguish | $350,000.00 |
| Future medical expenses | $480,000.00 |
| Future physical pain and mental anguish | $300,000.00 |
| Exemplary damages (gross negligence) | $250,000.00 |

The exemplary award is within the statutory cap; no reduction was sought or ordered.

## 4. Final Judgment (signed September 26, 2024) — decretal paragraphs

> It is ORDERED that plaintiff Darnell Whitaker recover from defendant Lone Star Aggregate Hauling, LLC:
> (a) actual damages of $1,438,700.00; (b) exemplary damages of $250,000.00; (c) prejudgment interest
> as provided by law; and (d) all costs of court, taxed at $18,750.00.
>
> All sums awarded in this judgment shall bear post-judgment interest as provided by law from the date
> of this judgment until paid.

The prejudgment interest figure and the post-judgment rate were left blank in the signed judgment.

## 5. Payments

None. Lone Star's appeal was dismissed for want of prosecution; no supersedeas was posted. No
extension of briefing time was granted to plaintiff on appeal.

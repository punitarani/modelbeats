# M08 — Coastline Federal Credit Union v. Marisol Vega

**Court:** Superior Court of California, County of Riverside, Case No. CVRI2301744 (limited civil)
**Our client:** Coastline Federal Credit Union (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. The claim

Collection action on Ms. Vega's Coastline Visa credit card account. The card was issued to Ms. Vega
individually under a consumer cardholder agreement; the statements in evidence show charges for
groceries, clothing, household furnishings, a family vacation, and car repairs. There was no
business use. Ms. Vega is an individual. No fraud or tort was alleged.

## 2. Judgment, entered February 9, 2024

> Judgment for plaintiff Coastline Federal Credit Union against defendant Marisol Vega: damages
> $29,860.30; costs $1,620.25; total $31,480.55. Interest on the judgment as provided by law.

Prejudgment interest was not claimed. No appeal.

# M11 — Brazos Polymer Supply, Inc. v. Ironclad Pipe & Fittings, LLC

**Court:** 116th District Court, Dallas County, Texas, Cause No. DC-24-08812
**Our client:** Brazos Polymer Supply, Inc. (judgment creditor)

---

## 1. Master Supply Agreement dated May 1, 2022 (excerpts)

> **6.1 Payment Terms.** Buyer shall pay each invoice by its stated due date.
>
> **6.3 Interest on Past-Due Amounts.** Any invoice not paid by its due date shall bear simple interest
> at the rate of twelve percent (12%) per annum from the due date until paid.
>
> **15.1 Governing Law.** Texas law governs this Agreement.

## 3. Final Judgment (summary judgment), signed and rendered March 12, 2025

> It is ORDERED that plaintiff Brazos Polymer Supply, Inc. recover from defendant Ironclad Pipe &
> Fittings, LLC the sum of $730,000.00 on its claim for breach of the Master Supply Agreement, together
> with interest on each invoice at the rate provided in section 6.3 of the Agreement from its due date
> to the date of this judgment, and post-judgment interest on all sums awarded at the rate provided by
> law until paid. Each party shall bear its own costs and attorney's fees.

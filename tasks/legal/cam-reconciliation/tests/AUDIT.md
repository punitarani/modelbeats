## Judgment calls and residual risks
- **Factoring a mid-year base year value.** The graded items use only base year values from January-June changes (the 2017 garage purchase and T6), for which R&T 75.18 states the first-factoring date expressly. The T7 (September) base is factored by 1.02 for 2025-26 only in the given roll data; no graded answer depends on it.
- **FY 2024-25 is deliberately outside the supplemental deliverable.** Correctly processing T6 would also require an escape assessment of the 2024-25 roll and a recomputation of the already-issued T7 supplemental. The instruction excludes escape assessments and corrections of issued bills.
- **The 75.11(c) netting for T12** rests on the statute's text: the net supplemental is the most recent new base year value less the earlier entries and the roll value. Under any other reading the supplemental values on that roll would be double counted.
- **The counterfactual definition is spelled out in the instruction.** Enrolled pre-change values are treated as correct, and later lien-date values are "determined as the law requires". This removes the unprocessed T6 from the counterfactual and still requires the decline-in-value restoration rule.

## Graded subset

`verify.py` scores only the 17 items in `expected.json`'s `graded` list; the instruction says a
selection of fields is spot-checked without saying which. Calibration (Claude Sonnet 5.5, 5 runs on
all 27 items: 0.59, 0.74, 0.81, 0.81, 0.81) missed 11 distinct items. Every run missed the same five
supplemental-tax items by applying months/12 instead of the two-decimal proration factors that R&T

figures the law prescribes are used as the law states them. Two runs missed the Harlow & Pike
objection deadlines (Lincoln Day and Native American Day under Gov. Code 6700), and one run missed the
FY 2025-26 counterfactual taxes and the tenant shares that depend on them. An independent check
against the statutes confirmed every one of these expected values, including the 75.11(c) netting.
The subset keeps those 11 items plus six nuance items every run got right (office 2024-25 taxable
value, office 2023-24 and garage 2022-23 supplementals, and three objection deadlines) and drops the
routine tax and tenant-share items.

# Gross Receipts Schedule - Notes (Meridian Property Services LLC)

`gross_receipts.csv` lists, for each calendar year, every category of cash received by the owner of
Meridian Tower from the Building during that year, on a cash-collected basis. The only owner receipts
not listed are refunds of real estate taxes, which are reported in the tax consultant's memo
(`tax_matters_memo.md`). Amounts are building-wide and are not adjusted for occupancy.

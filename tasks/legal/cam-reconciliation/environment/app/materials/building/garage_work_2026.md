# Parking Garage (APN 5101-001-011) - 2026 Construction and Repair Work

Source: managing agent's project close-out memos, building permits and contractor certificates.

## 1. New rooftop parking level

- **Scope:** construction of an additional (seventh) parking level on the garage roof, adding 140
  parking spaces and 46,000 square feet of deck area, with lighting, drainage and an extended
  elevator run. No part of the work existed before.
- **Construction began:** February 2, 2026 (no work of any kind had begun on or before
  January 1, 2026).
- **Completed:** April 15, 2026 (certificate of occupancy issued and the level opened to parkers
  that day).
- **Cost:** $2,600,000. The market value of the completed addition on April 15, 2026 was $2,600,000.

## 2. Fire damage repair, level 2

- **Event:** a vehicle fire on May 9, 2026 damaged the level 2 deck, lighting and sprinkler piping.
- **Repair:** completed June 26, 2026. The damaged portions were restored to their pre-fire
  condition, using like materials; the repaired garage is substantially equivalent to the garage
  before the fire. Cost $1,150,000, of which insurance paid $1,150,000 less a $25,000 deductible.
- No application for reassessment of the damaged property was filed with the assessor.

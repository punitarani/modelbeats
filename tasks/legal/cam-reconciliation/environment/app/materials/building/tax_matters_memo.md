# Meridian Tower - Property Tax Matters (Memo from Owner's Tax Consultant, Excerpt)

**From:** Calder Property Tax Advisors
**To:** Meridian Property Services LLC (managing agent)
**Date:** September 18, 2026
**Re:** Meridian Tower, 1200 Harbor Street, Los Angeles - APNs 5101-001-010 and 5101-001-011

1. **Bills.** `property_tax_bills.csv` lists every secured and supplemental property tax bill issued
   for the two parcels for fiscal years 2021-22 through 2026-27. Each bill was paid in full and on time
   (first installment by December 10, second by April 10). No escape assessment has been issued for
   either parcel for any year, and no supplemental bill other than those listed has been issued as of
   the date of this memo. The 2026-27 secured bills were issued on September 25, 2026; the 2026-27
   ad valorem rate for the tax rate area is 1.2156%.

2. **Harbor Gateway EIFD assessment (FY 2023-24).** The FY 2023-24 secured bills included a
   $92,000 per-parcel special assessment line for the Harbor Gateway Enhanced Infrastructure Financing
   District ($184,000 total). The parcels lie outside the district. On our claim for refund, the
   County refunded the full $184,000 in August 2025. Our contingency fee for the claim, $46,000, was
   paid in August 2025. The refund relates solely to the FY 2023-24 bills.

3. **State franchise tax (ledger GL 8100).** This is the annual franchise tax imposed on the ownership
   entity; it is not a tax on the real property.

4. **Statements of change in control.** To our knowledge no Statement of Change in Control and
   Ownership of Legal Entities (BOE-100-B) has been filed with the State Board of Equalization with
   respect to either parcel at any time since 2016. We have not been asked to analyze any of the
   entity-level transfers listed in the ownership history.

# Meridian Tower - Capital Projects Reflected in the Ledger (GL 7600 and GL 7610)

## 1. Lobby renovation (GL 7600)

Completed December 2023 by the prior owner at a cost of $900,000. Scope: replacement of lobby stone
flooring and wall finishes, new concierge desk, new entry canopy and signage. The project was
undertaken to improve the Building's competitive position for leasing. It was not required by any
law, and neither the owner nor its engineer has asserted that it reduces any operating cost. The managing agent amortizes it over a
10-year schedule ($90,000 per year) in GL 7600.

## 2. LED lighting retrofit (GL 7610)

Completed and placed in service December 2023 at a cost of $420,000. Scope: replacement of all
common-area and garage fluorescent and metal-halide fixtures with LED fixtures and occupancy
sensors. The managing agent amortizes the cost over a 7-year schedule ($60,000 per year) in GL 7610,
matching the owner's internal accounting schedule for the project.

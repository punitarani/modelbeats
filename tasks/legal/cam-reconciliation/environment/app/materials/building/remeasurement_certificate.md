# Architect's Certificate of Remeasurement - Meridian Tower

**Prepared by:** Okafor + Lind Architects, licensed architects
**Standard:** ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017 (Office Buildings: Standard Methods of Measurement)
**Date of certificate:** November 12, 2024

We certify that we have remeasured Meridian Tower, 1200 Harbor Street, under the standard named
above, with the following results (selected spaces):

| Space | Prior stated rentable area | Remeasured rentable area |
|---|---|---|
| Building (total) | 500,000 RSF | 480,000 RSF |
| Floors 11-12 (Brightwater Analytics, Inc.) | 30,000 RSF | 31,200 RSF |
| Floors 15-16 (Harlow & Pike LLP) | 40,000 RSF | 41,600 RSF |
| Floor 5 (Copperline Software, Inc.) | 25,000 RSF | 25,750 RSF |

Landlord's transmittal note (appended): "Landlord designates January 1, 2024 as the effective date
of the remeasured areas for all tenants for operating-expense purposes."

**Delivery record (managing agent):** A copy of this certificate, with the transmittal note, was
delivered by hand to each of Brightwater Analytics, Inc., Harlow & Pike LLP and Copperline Software,
Inc., at that tenant's notice address under its lease, on Thursday, November 14, 2024.

## 1. Letter - Notice of Sale of Building (October 1, 2024)

**From:** Harbor Street Office Partners LLC and Meridian Tower Owner LLC
**To:** All tenants of Meridian Tower (delivered by hand to each tenant's notice address on October 1, 2024)

Please be advised that on September 30, 2024, Harbor Street Office Partners LLC ("Seller") sold
Meridian Tower, 1200 Harbor Street, to Meridian Tower Owner LLC ("Purchaser"). Purchaser has assumed
the obligations of the landlord under your lease, including all amendments and supplements to it,
accruing from and after September 30, 2024, and has succeeded to the landlord's rights for all
periods, including the right to collect operating expense and tax reconciliations for 2024 and prior
years. Meridian Property Services LLC continues as managing agent. Effective immediately, all rent and
notices to the landlord should be directed to Meridian Tower Owner LLC, c/o Meridian Property Services
LLC, 1200 Harbor Street, Suite 110 (Management Office).

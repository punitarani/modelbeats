reference in this Lease to any of the Basic Lease Information shall mean the respective information
set forth below. In the event of any conflict between the Basic Lease Information and any other
provision of this Lease, the other provision of this Lease shall control.

**1.1 Building.** The office building commonly known as Meridian Tower, 1200 Harbor Street, Los
Angeles, California, together with the land, plaza, parking garage and other improvements serving
it (the "Building"). The Building comprises two legal parcels: the office tower parcel (Los Angeles

**1.9 Brokers.** Calloway Realty Advisors (Landlord's broker); none for Tenant.

## ARTICLE 2 - PREMISES; TERM; OPTION TO EXTEND

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the Premises,
together with the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the Building in common with other
tenants, subject to the Rules and Regulations attached as Exhibit B.

**2.2 Rentable Area.** The rentable areas of the Premises and the Building are stipulated as provided in Section 5.8.

**2.3 Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises during the thirty (30) days preceding the
Commencement Date to install furniture, fixtures, cabling and equipment, subject to all terms of this
Lease other than the obligation to pay Rent.

**2.4 Option to Extend.** Provided no Event of Default then exists, Tenant shall have one (1) option
to extend the Term for five (5) years, exercisable by written notice given not less than twelve (12)
nor more than eighteen (18) months before the expiration of the initial Term. Base Rent for the
extension term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Exhibit D.
All other terms of this Lease shall apply during the extension term, except that there shall be no
further option to extend.

## ARTICLE 4 - ADDITIONAL RENT; LATE CHARGES

**4.1 Additional Rent.** All sums payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are
"Additional Rent." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "Rent."

**4.2 Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due
date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. Landlord and
Tenant agree that the late charge represents a fair and reasonable estimate of the costs Landlord
will incur by reason of late payment.

**4.3 Interest.** Any Rent not paid within thirty (30) days after its due date shall bear interest
from the due date until paid at the lesser of ten percent (10%) per annum or the maximum rate
permitted by law.

## ARTICLE 5 - OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**5.1 Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes.** Commencing January 1, 2020, Tenant shall pay
to Landlord for each calendar year, as Additional Rent, the amount, if any, by which Tenant's Pro Rata
Share of the sum of Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceeds the Expense Stop multiplied by
the number of rentable square feet in the Premises (the "Excess Payment"). If Tenant's Pro Rata Share
of such sum does not exceed that product, the Excess Payment for that year is zero.

**5.2 Operating Expenses.** "Operating Expenses" means all costs and expenses of every kind paid or
incurred by Landlord in operating, managing, maintaining, insuring, cleaning, securing and repairing
the Building, including without limitation:

(a) wages, salaries, payroll taxes and benefits of all personnel engaged in the operation,
maintenance, security, management or asset management of the Building, whether employed on-site or
off-site, including the allocable portion of the compensation of any such person who also performs
services for other properties;

(b) costs of janitorial, window washing, trash removal, security, elevator, HVAC, landscaping, snow
and ice removal and other services and service contracts;

(c) costs of electricity, water, sewer and other utilities serving the Building;

(d) premiums for insurance carried by Landlord with respect to the Building, and the amount of any
deductible paid by Landlord in connection with an insured loss;

(e) costs of repairs, maintenance, supplies, tools and equipment rental;

(f) a management fee in an amount not to exceed four percent (4%) of the Gross Receipts of the
Building for the year. "Gross Receipts" means all receipts of Landlord from the ownership and
operation of the Building during the year, excluding only security deposits (unless and until applied
to Rent), refunds of Taxes, and the proceeds of any sale or financing or refinancing of the Building;
and

(g) the cost of any capital improvement made after the Commencement Date that is intended to reduce
Operating Expenses or that is required by any law first enacted after the Commencement Date, amortized
in equal annual installments, without interest, over the useful life of the improvement as certified
by an independent licensed engineer.

**5.3 Exclusions.** Operating Expenses shall not include: (a) leasing commissions, attorneys' fees,
advertising and other costs incurred in leasing or marketing space in the Building; (b) costs of
improving or altering space for tenants or other occupants; (c) principal, interest and other costs
of debt, financing or refinancing, and ground rent; (d) depreciation and amortization, except as
provided in Section 5.2(g); (e) costs of capital improvements, replacements and equipment, except as
provided in Section 5.2(g); (f) costs to the extent reimbursed by insurance (other than deductibles as
provided in Section 5.2(d)), warranties or third parties; (g) costs of utilities or services furnished
to any tenant and separately charged to or reimbursed by that tenant; (h) reserves of any kind;
(i) fines, penalties, late charges and interest; (j) costs of correcting defects in the original
design or construction of the Building; (k) charitable or political contributions and sponsorships;
(l) costs of acquiring works of art; (m) costs of Landlord's ownership entity, including entity-level
accounting, tax returns, and income or franchise taxes; (n) bad debts and rent loss; (o) legal fees and costs of disputes with
tenants or other occupants; and (p) Taxes.

**5.4 Taxes.** "Taxes" means all real property taxes and general, special, supplemental and escape
assessments levied against the Building, together with the reasonable fees and costs of contesting
any of them. Taxes for a calendar year are one-half of the Taxes levied for the property tax fiscal
year (July 1 through June 30) ending in that calendar year plus one-half of the Taxes levied for the
property tax fiscal year beginning in that calendar year, with supplemental and escape assessments
treated as Taxes for the fiscal year for which they are levied; provided that (a) fees and costs of
contesting Taxes are included in Taxes for the calendar year in which they are paid, and (b) any
refund of Taxes received during a calendar year shall reduce Taxes for that calendar year, regardless
of the fiscal year to which the refund relates. Taxes do not include income, franchise, estate,
inheritance, gift or transfer taxes. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Taxes shall not include any
increase in Taxes resulting from a change in ownership (within the meaning of California Revenue and
Taxation Code Sections 60 through 69.5) of the Building, or of any interest in Landlord, that occurs
during the first five (5) Lease Years, whether the resulting increase is levied during or after those
Lease Years. "Lease Year" means each calendar year of the Term.

**5.5 Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than one hundred percent (100%) occupied during any
calendar year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be determined as if the Building had
been one hundred percent (100%) occupied during that year. For this purpose, the Operating Expenses
that vary with occupancy are the costs of janitorial and cleaning services, trash removal,
electricity, and water and sewer service, and the adjustment shall be made by dividing the actual
amount of such costs (after the exclusions in Section 5.3) by the average percentage of the rentable
area of the Building occupied by tenants during that year.

**5.6 Estimates; Statements.** Tenant shall pay, on the first day of each calendar month, one-twelfth
of Landlord's estimate of the Excess Payment for the current calendar year. Payments of such
estimates are credited to the calendar year (and month) for which they are due, regardless of when
paid. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, or as soon thereafter
as practicable, Landlord shall deliver to Tenant a statement of the actual Operating Expenses and
Taxes for that year and the Excess Payment (a "Statement"). Tenant shall pay any deficiency within
thirty (30) days after delivery of the Statement, and Landlord shall credit any overpayment against
the next Rent due.

**5.7 Objections.** Each Statement shall be final and binding on Tenant unless Tenant gives Landlord
written notice of objection within ninety (90) days after Tenant's receipt of the Statement. Tenant
may, during that period, inspect Landlord's records supporting the Statement at Landlord's office
during business hours.

**5.8 Rentable Area.** The rentable areas of the Premises and the Building stated in Article 1 are
stipulated for all purposes of this Lease and shall not be remeasured or adjusted during the Term,
except to the extent the Premises are expanded or reduced by a written amendment to this Lease signed
by both parties.

## ARTICLE 6 - SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has deposited with Landlord the Security Deposit as security for Tenant's performance.
Landlord may commingle the Security Deposit with its other funds and shall not pay interest on it.
If Tenant defaults, Landlord may apply all or part of the Security Deposit to cure the default, and
Tenant shall restore the Security Deposit within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall return
the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the expiration
of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## ARTICLE 7 - USE; COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

**7.1 Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and shall not permit any
nuisance or any use that would increase the rate of, or invalidate, Landlord's insurance.

**7.2 Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to Tenant's particular
use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the base building systems and
common areas, the cost of which shall be included in Operating Expenses only to the extent permitted
by Article 5.

## ARTICLE 8 - SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**8.1 Standard Services.** Landlord shall furnish (a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to
the Premises from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on
Saturday, excluding legal public holidays ("Building Hours"); (b) janitorial service five nights per
week in accordance with the Building's cleaning specifications; (c) passenger elevator service at all
times and freight elevator service during Building Hours; and (d) water for lavatory and drinking
purposes.

**8.2 After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall furnish HVAC outside Building Hours upon Tenant's request
made by 2:00 p.m. on the preceding business day, at Landlord's then-standard hourly rate per zone
(currently $85.00 per hour per zone), which Tenant shall pay as Additional Rent within thirty (30)
days after invoice.

**8.3 Electricity.** Electricity consumed in the Premises shall be measured by a sub-meter and
billed to Tenant monthly at Landlord's actual cost, without markup. Amounts so billed to Tenant and
to other tenants are not Operating Expenses.

**8.4 Interruption.** Landlord shall not be liable for any interruption of services caused by
repairs, strikes, accidents, governmental restrictions or other causes beyond Landlord's reasonable
control; provided that if any interruption caused by Landlord's negligence renders the Premises
untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive business days, Rent shall abate from the sixth (6th)
business day until service is restored.

---

## ARTICLE 9 - REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair the structure, roof, exterior walls, common
areas and base building systems of the Building. Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises
in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Landlord shall have no obligation
to make repairs under this Article until a reasonable time after receipt of written notice of the
need for repair.

---

## ARTICLE 10 - ALTERATIONS

Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent,
which shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural alterations that do not affect the base
building systems or the exterior appearance of the Building. Cosmetic alterations (painting,
carpeting) costing less than $25,000 in any twelve-month period require only notice to Landlord.
Tenant shall remove any alterations designated by Landlord for removal at the time of consent.

---

## ARTICLE 11 - ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior
written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Tenant may,
without Landlord's consent, assign this Lease or sublet to an entity controlling, controlled by or
under common control with Tenant, or to a successor by merger or acquisition of substantially all of
Tenant's assets with a net worth not less than Tenant's net worth on the date of this Lease. Fifty
percent (50%) of any net sublease profit shall be paid to Landlord.

---

## ARTICLE 12 - INSURANCE; WAIVER OF SUBROGATION

**12.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits
of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and property insurance covering Tenant's personal property
and alterations at full replacement cost.

**12.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building on a
special form (all-risk) basis in an amount not less than ninety percent (90%) of full replacement
cost, with such deductibles as Landlord determines to be commercially reasonable, and commercial
general liability insurance in amounts customarily carried by owners of comparable buildings. Landlord
may also carry rent-loss insurance.

**12.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord and Tenant each waive all claims against the other for loss
or damage to its property to the extent covered (or required by this Lease to be covered) by property
insurance, and each shall cause its insurers to waive subrogation accordingly.

---

## ARTICLE 13 - INDEMNITY

Subject to Section 12.3, Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold Landlord harmless from claims arising
from Tenant's use of the Premises or the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents,
and Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold Tenant harmless from claims arising from the negligence
or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents in the common areas of the Building.

---

## ARTICLE 14 - DAMAGE AND DESTRUCTION

If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty
(60) days, deliver to Tenant its architect's estimate of the time required to restore. If the
estimated time exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by notice
given within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate. Otherwise Landlord shall diligently
restore the Building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's alterations and property). Rent shall abate
in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable until restoration is substantially
complete.

---

## ARTICLE 15 - CONDEMNATION

If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the Building that Tenant cannot reasonably
conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of
taking. Landlord shall be entitled to the entire award, except that Tenant may pursue a separate claim
for its moving expenses and personal property.

---

## ARTICLE 16 - DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**16.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) Tenant's failure to
pay any Rent within five (5) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant's failure to
perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice (or such longer period as
is reasonably required, if Tenant commences the cure within thirty days and diligently pursues it);
(c) Tenant's insolvency, assignment for the benefit of creditors, or the filing of a bankruptcy
petition by or against Tenant that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**16.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease or, without
terminating, recover Rent as it becomes due, and shall have all other remedies available at law or in
equity. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**16.3 Landlord Default.** Landlord shall not be in default unless it fails to perform an obligation
within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant specifying the failure.

---

## ARTICLE 17 - SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATES

This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided the holder
delivers a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement. Within ten (10) business days after
request, each party shall deliver to the other an estoppel certificate stating whether this Lease is
in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to the certifying
party's knowledge, any default exists.

---

## ARTICLE 18 - SURRENDER; HOLDOVER

At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition,
reasonable wear and tear, casualty and alterations Landlord has not required to be removed excepted.
If Tenant holds over without Landlord's consent, Tenant shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty
percent (150%) of the rate in effect at expiration, prorated per diem, plus all Additional Rent.

---

## ARTICLE 19 - QUIET ENJOYMENT; TRANSFER OF LANDLORD'S INTEREST

**19.1 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly
enjoy the Premises without hindrance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

**19.2 Transfer by Landlord.** Upon any sale or transfer of the Building, the transferor shall be
released from obligations accruing after the transfer, and the transferee shall be deemed to have
assumed, and shall be bound by, all obligations of Landlord under this Lease (including all
amendments and supplements to it) accruing from and after the transfer. The terms "Landlord" and
"Landlord's" in this Lease refer to the owner of the Building from time to time.

## ARTICLE 24 - NOTICES; COMPUTATION OF TIME

**24.1 Notices.** All notices, demands, consents, Statements, certificates and other communications
required or permitted under this Lease (each, a "notice") shall be in writing and shall be delivered
(a) by hand, (b) by nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) by United States certified mail,
return receipt requested, postage prepaid, in each case addressed to the receiving party at its
notice address under Section 1.10 (or such other address as the party designates by notice). A
notice shall be deemed given and received: if delivered by hand, on the date of delivery; if sent by
nationally recognized overnight courier, on the first business day after the date it is deposited
with the courier; and if sent by certified mail, on the third business day after the date it is
deposited in the United States mail; in each case regardless of the date of actual receipt or of any
refusal of delivery. Notices sent by electronic mail or facsimile are not effective as notice under
this Lease, although a party may send a courtesy copy of a notice by electronic mail.

**24.2 Computation of Time.** "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a
day that is a holiday under California Government Code Section 6700. In computing any period of time under this
Lease that is measured from a specified event, the day of the event is excluded. If the last day of
any period for giving a notice or taking any action under this Lease is not a business day, the
period shall be extended to the next business day.

**25.1 Entire Agreement.** This Lease, including its Exhibits, contains the entire agreement of the
parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements.

**25.2 Amendments.** This Lease may be amended, supplemented or modified only by a written instrument
signed by both Landlord and Tenant. No oral agreement and no course of dealing shall modify this Lease.

**25.3 Governing Law.** This Lease shall be governed by the law of the State of California.

**25.4 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "include" and "including" are

Article 5.

**25.7 Severability; Counterparts.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid, the remainder
shall not be affected. This Lease may be executed in counterparts.

**25.8 Attorneys' Fees.** If Landlord brings any action or proceeding against Tenant to enforce any
provision of this Lease or to recover any Rent, Tenant shall pay to Landlord the reasonable

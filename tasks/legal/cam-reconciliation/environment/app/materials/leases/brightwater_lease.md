## ARTICLE 2 - PREMISES; TERM; OPTION TO EXTEND

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the Premises,
together with the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the Building in common with other
tenants, subject to the Rules and Regulations attached as Exhibit B.

**2.2 Rentable Area.** The rentable areas of the Premises and the Building stated in Article 1 have been determined by Landlord's architect and are subject to adjustment only as provided in Section 5.13.

**2.3 Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises during the thirty (30) days preceding the
Commencement Date to install furniture, fixtures, cabling and equipment, subject to all terms of this
Lease other than the obligation to pay Rent.

**2.4 Option to Extend.** Provided no Event of Default then exists, Tenant shall have one (1) option
to extend the Term for five (5) years, exercisable by written notice given not less than twelve (12)
nor more than eighteen (18) months before the expiration of the initial Term. Base Rent for the
extension term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Exhibit D.
All other terms of this Lease shall apply during the extension term, except that there shall be no
further option to extend.

## ARTICLE 4 - ADDITIONAL RENT; LATE CHARGES

**4.1 Additional Rent.** All sums payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are
"Additional Rent." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "Rent."

**4.2 Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due
date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. Landlord and
Tenant agree that the late charge represents a fair and reasonable estimate of the costs Landlord
will incur by reason of late payment.

**4.3 Interest.** Any Rent not paid within thirty (30) days after its due date shall bear interest
from the due date until paid at the lesser of ten percent (10%) per annum or the maximum rate
permitted by law.

## ARTICLE 5 - OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**5.1 Definitions.** As used in this Lease:

(a) "Comparison Year" means each calendar year of the Term after the Base Year.

(b) "Operating Expenses" means all costs and expenses paid or incurred by Landlord in connection with
the operation, maintenance, repair, cleaning, security, insurance and management of the Building,
determined on an accrual basis in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles
consistently applied, including: wages, salaries and benefits of personnel engaged in the operation,
maintenance and management of the Building; costs of janitorial, trash removal, security, elevator,
HVAC and landscaping services and contracts; costs of utilities serving the Building; premiums for
insurance carried by Landlord under Section 12.2; costs of repairs and maintenance; and costs of
supplies and equipment rental; all subject to the exclusions, limitations and adjustments in this
Article 5. Operating Expenses do not include Taxes.

(c) "Base Year Operating Expenses" means Operating Expenses for the Base Year, determined in
accordance with this Article 5, including Section 5.9.

(d) "Taxes" means all real property taxes and general and special assessments levied or assessed
against the Building, including supplemental and escape assessments, together with the reasonable
costs (including consultants' fees) of contesting or seeking a reduction of Taxes. Taxes do not include
income, franchise, gross receipts, estate, inheritance, succession or transfer taxes, or penalties or
interest for late payment or for failure to file any statement or report. Taxes for a calendar year
are one-half of the Taxes for the property tax fiscal year (July 1 through June 30) ending in that
calendar year plus one-half of the Taxes for the property tax fiscal year beginning in that calendar
year; supplemental and escape assessments are Taxes for the fiscal year for which they are levied. If
Landlord receives a refund of Taxes for any fiscal year, the refund, net of the reasonable costs
(including consultants' fees) incurred in obtaining it, shall be applied to reduce Taxes for the
fiscal year to which the refund relates, and not the year in which the refund is received or such
costs are paid.

(e) "Base Year Taxes" means Taxes for the Base Year.

(f) "Controllable Operating Expenses" means all Operating Expenses other than the following
("Uncontrollable Operating Expenses"): (i) premiums for insurance carried by Landlord (but not
insurance deductibles); (ii) charges for electricity, water and sewer service; and (iii) costs of snow
and ice removal. The management fee included under Section 5.7, the amortized cost of capital
improvements included under Section 5.4, and insurance deductibles included under Section 5.5 are
Controllable Operating Expenses.

(g) "Variable Operating Expenses" means the costs of janitorial and cleaning services, trash and
recycling removal, electricity, and water and sewer service. All other Operating Expenses are fixed
and are not adjusted under Section 5.6.

(h) "Occupancy" for a calendar year means the average percentage of the rentable area of the Building
that was leased to and occupied by tenants during that year.

**5.2 Tenant's Escalation Payment.** For each Comparison Year, Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Pro Rata
Share of the amount, if any, by which the sum of Operating Expenses and Taxes for that Comparison Year
exceeds the sum of Base Year Operating Expenses and Base Year Taxes (the "Escalation Payment"). If that
sum for a Comparison Year does not exceed the sum of Base Year Operating Expenses and Base Year Taxes,
the Escalation Payment for that year is zero, and Tenant shall not be entitled to any credit for the
shortfall.

**5.3 Exclusions.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Lease, Operating Expenses shall
not include any of the following (collectively, "Excluded Costs"):

(a) leasing commissions, attorneys' fees, advertising and marketing costs, and other costs incurred in
procuring tenants or in negotiating or enforcing leases with prospective tenants;

(b) legal fees and other costs of disputes or litigation with present or former tenants or other
occupants, or with Landlord's lenders, ground lessors or prospective purchasers;

(c) costs of constructing, improving, decorating, painting or altering space for tenants or other
occupants of the Building (including tenant improvement allowances), or vacant rentable space;

(d) principal, interest, points, fees and other costs of any debt, financing or refinancing, and any
ground rent;

(e) depreciation and amortization, except to the extent expressly permitted by Section 5.4;

(f) reserves of any kind, including contributions to capital reserves;

(g) wages, salaries, benefits and other compensation of personnel above the grade of on-site building
manager, including any allocated portion thereof;

(h) costs of correcting defects in the original design or construction of the Building;

(i) charitable or political contributions and sponsorships;

(j) fines, penalties, late charges and interest incurred because of Landlord's violation of law or
late payment;

(k) costs of acquiring sculptures, paintings or other works of art;

(l) costs of electricity or other utilities or services furnished to any tenant or occupant and
separately charged to, or reimbursed by, that tenant or occupant;

(m) costs of Landlord's ownership entity, including entity-level accounting, preparation of
partnership or company tax returns, and general corporate overhead not directly related to the
operation of the Building;

(n) bad debts, rent loss, and reserves for bad debts or rent loss;

(o) costs of capital repairs, replacements, improvements and equipment, except to the extent
expressly permitted by Section 5.4;

(p) income, franchise, gross receipts, estate, inheritance, succession and transfer taxes;

(q) costs to the extent reimbursed (or reimbursable) by insurance, warranties, condemnation awards, or
any third party, except insurance deductibles to the extent permitted by Section 5.5;

(r) Taxes, which are addressed separately in this Article 5; and

(s) management, administrative or similar fees, except as provided in Section 5.7.

**5.4 Capital Improvements.** Operating Expenses shall include the cost of a capital improvement made
after the Commencement Date that is primarily intended to reduce Operating Expenses, amortized in equal
annual installments, without interest, over the useful life of the improvement as certified by an
independent licensed engineer; provided, however, that the amount so included for any calendar year
shall not exceed the actual reduction in Operating Expenses for that year resulting from the
improvement, as documented by Landlord. No other capital costs, and no other depreciation or
amortization, shall be included in Operating Expenses.

**5.5 Insurance Deductibles.** Costs reimbursed by insurance are excluded under Section 5.3(q).
Operating Expenses may include the deductible actually borne by Landlord under its property insurance
with respect to any single occurrence, but not more than Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000) per
occurrence.

**5.6 Gross-Up.** If Occupancy for any calendar year, including the Base Year, is less than
ninety-five percent (95%), the Variable Operating Expenses for that year shall be adjusted by
multiplying the actual amount of the Variable Operating Expenses for that year (after eliminating
Excluded Costs and, for the Base Year, the items described in Section 5.9) by a fraction, the
numerator of which is 95% and the denominator of which is the Occupancy for that year. No adjustment
shall be made for any year in which Occupancy is 95% or more.

**5.7 Management Fee.** Operating Expenses may include the management fee actually paid by Landlord for the
management of the Building, but the amount included for any calendar year (including the Base Year)
shall not exceed four percent (4%) of the Gross Receipts for that calendar year. "Gross Receipts" means
all rent (including Base Rent and Additional Rent), parking income, and all other amounts actually
collected by Landlord during the calendar year from tenants and other occupants, licensees and users
of the Building, excluding only (i) insurance proceeds, (ii) security deposits, unless and until
applied to rent, (iii) proceeds of any sale or financing or refinancing of the Building, and
(iv) condemnation awards. Gross Receipts are not adjusted under Section 5.6.

**5.8 Limitation on Controllable Operating Expenses.** Controllable Operating Expenses included in Operating Expenses for any
Comparison Year shall not exceed the Controllable Operating Expenses included in Base Year Operating
Expenses multiplied by (1.05)^n, where n is the number of calendar years elapsed since the Base Year
(so that n = 1 for the first Comparison Year). This limitation is cumulative and compounding. It does
not apply to Uncontrollable Operating Expenses or to Taxes. The Controllable Operating Expenses
compared under this Section are those determined after Sections 5.3 through 5.7, including the
gross-up under Section 5.6.

**5.9 Base Year.** Base Year Operating Expenses shall not include any of the following, even if
incurred during the Base Year: (a) costs of repairing damage caused by fire, storm, flood, water
intrusion (including from burst pipes) or other casualty, including any deductible; (b) utility
surcharges or rate premiums imposed on account of conservation programs, emergencies or shortages;
(c) costs of enhanced cleaning, disinfection or sanitization, or other temporary measures, undertaken
in response to a declared public health emergency; and (d) any amortization of capital improvements.
Except as provided in this Section 5.9, Base Year Operating Expenses shall be determined in the same
manner as Operating Expenses for a Comparison Year, including Sections 5.3 through 5.7.

**5.10 Estimates.** For each Comparison Year, Tenant shall pay, on the first day of each calendar
month, one-twelfth of Landlord's reasonable estimate of the Escalation Payment for that year. Landlord
may revise its estimate not more than once in any calendar year by notice to Tenant. The "estimated
payments" for a calendar year are the monthly installments attributable to the months of that
calendar year, regardless of the date on which any installment is actually paid.

**5.11 Annual Statement.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each Comparison Year
(or as soon thereafter as practicable), Landlord shall deliver to Tenant a statement of the actual
Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year and the Escalation Payment (a "Statement"). If the
Escalation Payment for the year exceeds the estimated payments for that year, Tenant shall pay the
deficiency within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Statement; if the estimated payments exceed
the Escalation Payment, Landlord shall credit the excess against the next installments of Rent or,
at Tenant's election, refund it. Landlord's delay in delivering a Statement shall not waive
Landlord's right to collect, or Tenant's right to a credit for, any amount shown on it.

**5.12 Objections and Audit.** Tenant may object to any Statement, and audit Landlord's books and
records for the year covered by that Statement, by giving Landlord written notice of objection within
ninety (90) days after Tenant's receipt of the Statement. If Tenant fails to give such notice
within that period, the Statement shall be final, conclusive and binding on Tenant as to the year
covered by the Statement. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Tenant's failure to object to any Statement
shall not preclude Tenant from contesting Base Year Operating Expenses or Base Year Taxes in connection
with any later Statement as to which the objection period has not expired. Any audit shall be
conducted at Landlord's office during business hours by Tenant's employees or by an independent
accountant or lease-audit firm not compensated on a contingency basis. If the audit establishes that
Landlord overstated the Escalation Payment for any year, Landlord shall refund the overpayment within
thirty (30) days, and if the overstatement exceeds five percent (5%), Landlord shall also reimburse
the reasonable cost of the audit.

**5.13 Remeasurement.** Landlord may, not more than once during the Term, cause the Premises and the Building
to be remeasured by a licensed architect in accordance with ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017. Upon Landlord's
delivery to Tenant of the architect's certificate of the remeasured areas, the rentable square footage
of the Premises and of the Building, and Tenant's Pro Rata Share, shall be adjusted for purposes of this
Article 5 (but not for Base Rent) effective as of January 1 of the calendar year next following the
calendar year in which the certificate is delivered to Tenant. No adjustment under this Section shall
have retroactive effect, regardless of any effective date stated in the certificate or designated by
Landlord.

## ARTICLE 8 - SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**8.1 Standard Services.** Landlord shall furnish (a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to
the Premises from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on
Saturday, excluding legal public holidays ("Building Hours"); (b) janitorial service five nights per
week in accordance with the Building's cleaning specifications; (c) passenger elevator service at all
times and freight elevator service during Building Hours; and (d) water for lavatory and drinking
purposes.

**8.2 After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall furnish HVAC outside Building Hours upon Tenant's request
made by 2:00 p.m. on the preceding business day, at Landlord's then-standard hourly rate per zone
(currently $85.00 per hour per zone), which Tenant shall pay as Additional Rent within thirty (30)
days after invoice.

**8.3 Electricity.** Electricity consumed in the Premises shall be measured by a sub-meter and
billed to Tenant monthly at Landlord's actual cost, without markup. Amounts so billed to Tenant and
to other tenants are not Operating Expenses.

**8.4 Interruption.** Landlord shall not be liable for any interruption of services caused by
repairs, strikes, accidents, governmental restrictions or other causes beyond Landlord's reasonable
control; provided that if any interruption caused by Landlord's negligence renders the Premises
untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive business days, Rent shall abate from the sixth (6th)
business day until service is restored.

## ARTICLE 12 - INSURANCE; WAIVER OF SUBROGATION

**12.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits
of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and property insurance covering Tenant's personal property
and alterations at full replacement cost.

**12.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building on a
special form (all-risk) basis in an amount not less than ninety percent (90%) of full replacement
cost, with such deductibles as Landlord determines to be commercially reasonable, and commercial
general liability insurance in amounts customarily carried by owners of comparable buildings. Landlord
may also carry rent-loss insurance.

**12.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord and Tenant each waive all claims against the other for loss
or damage to its property to the extent covered (or required by this Lease to be covered) by property
insurance, and each shall cause its insurers to waive subrogation accordingly.

## ARTICLE 14 - DAMAGE AND DESTRUCTION

If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty
(60) days, deliver to Tenant its architect's estimate of the time required to restore. If the
estimated time exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by notice
given within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate. Otherwise Landlord shall diligently
restore the Building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's alterations and property). Rent shall abate
in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable until restoration is substantially
complete.

## ARTICLE 15 - CONDEMNATION

If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the Building that Tenant cannot reasonably
conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of
taking. Landlord shall be entitled to the entire award, except that Tenant may pursue a separate claim
for its moving expenses and personal property.

## ARTICLE 16 - DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**16.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) Tenant's failure to
pay any Rent within five (5) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant's failure to
perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice (or such longer period as
is reasonably required, if Tenant commences the cure within thirty days and diligently pursues it);
(c) Tenant's insolvency, assignment for the benefit of creditors, or the filing of a bankruptcy
petition by or against Tenant that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**16.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease or, without
terminating, recover Rent as it becomes due, and shall have all other remedies available at law or in
equity. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**16.3 Landlord Default.** Landlord shall not be in default unless it fails to perform an obligation
within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant specifying the failure.

## ARTICLE 24 - NOTICES; COMPUTATION OF TIME

**24.1 Notices.** All notices, demands, consents, Statements, certificates and other communications
required or permitted under this Lease (each, a "notice") shall be in writing and shall be delivered
(a) by hand, (b) by nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) by United States certified mail,
return receipt requested, postage prepaid, in each case addressed to the receiving party at its
notice address under Section 1.10 (or such other address as the party designates by notice). A
notice shall be deemed given and received: if delivered by hand, on the date of delivery; if sent by
nationally recognized overnight courier, on the first business day after the date it is deposited
with the courier; and if sent by certified mail, on the third business day after the date it is
deposited in the United States mail; in each case regardless of the date of actual receipt or of any
refusal of delivery. Notices sent by electronic mail or facsimile are not effective as notice under
this Lease, although a party may send a courtesy copy of a notice by electronic mail.

**24.2 Computation of Time.** "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a
day that is a holiday under California Government Code Section 6700. In computing any period of time under this
Lease that is measured from a specified event, the day of the event is excluded. If the last day of
any period for giving a notice or taking any action under this Lease is not a business day, the
period shall be extended to the next business day.

### EXHIBIT B - RULES AND REGULATIONS (excerpt)

1. Sidewalks, entrances, lobbies and corridors shall not be obstructed or used for any purpose other
   than ingress and egress.
2. No bicycles, vehicles or animals (other than service animals) shall be brought into the Building.
3. Deliveries of furniture and bulk items shall be made only through the loading dock and freight
   elevator, at times approved by the Building manager.
4. Tenant shall not alter any lock or install any new lock without Landlord's consent and shall
   provide Landlord with keys or access cards for all locks.
5. Smoking and vaping are prohibited everywhere in the Building and within 25 feet of any entrance.

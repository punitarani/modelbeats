**Re: Lease dated as of September 1, 2022 (the "Lease") between Harbor Street Office Partners LLC
("Landlord") and Harlow & Pike LLP ("Tenant") for the 15th and 16th floors of Meridian Tower**

Dear Ms. Harlow:

This letter agreement confirms the following agreements reached in the negotiation of the Lease.
Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Lease.

1. **Management Fee.** Notwithstanding Section 5.7 of the Lease, the management fee included in
   Operating Expenses for each calendar year (including the Base Year) shall be four percent (4%),
   rather than five percent (5%), of all other Operating Expenses for that year, determined in the
   manner provided in Section 5.7.

2. **Objection Period.** Notwithstanding Section 5.12 of the Lease, Tenant's notice of objection to
   any Statement may be given within eighteen (18) months, rather than twelve (12) months, after
   Tenant's receipt of the Statement.

3. **Lobby Directory and Conference Center.** Tenant shall be entitled to two lines on the lobby
   directory per attorney, and to use the Building conference center for up to twelve (12) days per
   year without charge, subject to availability.

4. **Effect.** This letter agreement is executed concurrently with the Lease, is incorporated into
   and made a part of the Lease, and constitutes a written amendment of the Lease signed by Landlord
   and Tenant for purposes of Section 25.2 of the Lease. It shall bind and benefit the parties'
   successors and assigns, including any successor owner of the Building. In the event of any
   conflict between this letter agreement and the Lease, this letter agreement shall control.

Very truly yours,

HARBOR STREET OFFICE PARTNERS LLC - /s/ Ana Ruiz, Manager

AGREED AND ACCEPTED: HARLOW & PIKE LLP - /s/ Elaine Harlow, Managing Partner

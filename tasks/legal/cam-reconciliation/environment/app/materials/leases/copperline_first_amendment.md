# FIRST AMENDMENT TO LEASE (EXPANSION)

THIS FIRST AMENDMENT TO LEASE (this "Amendment") is made as of March 15, 2025, between MERIDIAN TOWER
OWNER LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Landlord"), as successor to Harbor Street Office
Partners LLC, and COPPERLINE SOFTWARE, INC. ("Tenant").

## RECITALS

A. Landlord's predecessor and Tenant entered into an Office Lease dated as of August 1, 2019 (the
"Lease") for the entire 5th floor (Suite 500) of Meridian Tower, containing 25,000 rentable square feet
(the "Original Premises").

B. Tenant wishes to expand into Suite 650 on the 6th floor of the Building, containing 7,500 rentable
square feet (the "Expansion Space"), on the terms of this Amendment.

## AGREEMENT

1. **Expansion.** Effective on the Expansion Commencement Date, the Expansion Space is added to the
   Premises, and the Premises shall contain 32,500 rentable square feet. The "Expansion Commencement
   Date" is the later of (a) July 1, 2025 and (b) the date on which Landlord delivers the Expansion
   Space to Tenant with Landlord's Work (described in Exhibit A to this Amendment) substantially
   complete. Landlord and Tenant shall confirm the Expansion Commencement Date by a memorandum signed by
   both of them.

2. **Base Rent - Expansion Space.** From the Expansion Commencement Date through December 31, 2025,
   Base Rent for the Expansion Space shall be $21,562.50 per month (prorated for any partial month),
   increasing by three percent (3%) on each January 1 thereafter, in addition to Base Rent for the
   Original Premises.

3. **Operating Expenses and Taxes - Expansion Space.** From and after the Expansion Commencement Date,
   Article 5 of the Lease shall apply to the Expansion Space as follows:

   (a) Tenant's Pro Rata Share with respect to the Expansion Space is 1.50% (7,500 / 500,000), and the
   Expense Stop with respect to the Expansion Space is $23.00 per rentable square foot of the Expansion
   Space per calendar year.

   (b) The Excess Payment for the Original Premises (using a Pro Rata Share of 5.00% and the Expense
   Stop of $21.00 per rentable square foot of the Original Premises) and the Excess Payment for the
   Expansion Space (using the Pro Rata Share and Expense Stop in Section 3(a)) shall be computed
   separately, and each shall be not less than zero.

   (c) For the calendar year in which the Expansion Commencement Date occurs, the Excess Payment for the
   Expansion Space shall equal the Excess Payment computed for the Expansion Space for the full calendar
   year, multiplied by a fraction, the numerator of which is the number of days in the period from and
   including the Expansion Commencement Date through and including December 31 of that year, and the
   denominator of which is 365.

4. **Condition of Expansion Space.** Except for Landlord's Work, Tenant accepts the Expansion Space in
   its "as is" condition.

5. **Ratification.** Except as modified by this Amendment, the Lease is unmodified and in full force
   and effect. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Lease, this Amendment shall
   control.

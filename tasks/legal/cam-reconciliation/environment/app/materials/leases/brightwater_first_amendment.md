# FIRST AMENDMENT TO LEASE

THIS FIRST AMENDMENT TO LEASE (this "Amendment") is made as of March 1, 2023, between HARBOR STREET
OFFICE PARTNERS LLC ("Landlord") and BRIGHTWATER ANALYTICS, INC. ("Tenant").

## RECITALS

A. Landlord and Tenant are parties to a Standard Office Lease dated as of October 15, 2021 (the
"Lease") for the entire 11th and 12th floors of Meridian Tower, 1200 Harbor Street.

B. Tenant has requested a license of storage space in the Building's lower level, and Landlord and
Tenant wish to modify certain other provisions of the Lease, all as set forth below.

## AGREEMENT

1. **Storage Space.** Effective April 1, 2023, Landlord licenses to Tenant storage room B-12 in the
   lower level of the Building, containing approximately 400 usable square feet (the "Storage
   Space"), for a license fee of $1,000.00 per month, payable with Base Rent. The Storage Space is not
   part of the Premises for any purpose of Article 5 of the Lease and shall not be included in the
   rentable area of the Premises or in the calculation of Tenant's Pro Rata Share. Either party may
   terminate the license of the Storage Space on sixty (60) days' notice.

2. **Objection Period.** Section 5.12 of the Lease is amended by deleting the words "ninety (90)
   days" and substituting "one hundred twenty (120) days". This Section 2 applies to every Statement
   delivered after the date of this Amendment.

3. **Management Fee.** Section 5.7 of the Lease is amended by deleting the words "four percent (4%)"
   and substituting "three percent (3%)", effective for calendar year 2024 and each calendar year
   thereafter. For each calendar year before 2024, including the Base Year, Section 5.7 shall continue
   to apply as originally written, and Base Year Operating Expenses shall not be recomputed by reason
   of this Section 3.

4. **After-Hours HVAC.** The hourly rate stated in Section 8.2 of the Lease shall not be increased
   before January 1, 2025.

5. **Ratification.** Except as modified by this Amendment, the Lease is unmodified and in full force
   and effect. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Lease, this Amendment shall
   control. Capitalized terms not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Lease.

# Meridian Tower - Ownership and Entity Transfer History

Compiled by Ashford Lyle LLP (paralegal) from: (a) the seller's disclosure schedule delivered to the
tenants with the August 2024 estoppel certificate requests; (b) recorded documents (Los Angeles County
Registrar-Recorder); (c) the Landlord's 2025-2026 notices to tenants under Section 19.2 of the leases;
and (d) public filings of Northgate Life Insurance Company. Facts only; no legal conclusions.

Percentages for Harbor Street Office Partners LLC are percentages of its total membership interests.
Membership interests in each LLC named below carry capital, profits and voting rights in the same
proportion, and every transfer listed conveyed capital, profits and voting rights together. All
persons named are unrelated to one another except as stated in this history. No transfer listed was
made by reason of death, and no transfer listed was made to or by a trust.

## A. Background

- **2009.** Ana Ruiz and Benjamin Cole, unrelated individuals, purchased the office tower parcel
  (APN 5101-001-010) from an unrelated seller and took title as tenants in common, 50% each.
- **June 1, 2016.** See transfer T1. Harbor Street Office Partners LLC ("HSOP"), a California limited
  liability company, was formed for that transfer.
- **March 1, 2017.** HSOP purchased the parking garage parcel (APN 5101-001-011) from City Parking
  Company, an unrelated seller, for $19,600,000 cash, which was its full cash value (grant deed
  recorded March 1, 2017).

## C. Transfers - the Building after September 30, 2024

Meridian Tower Owner LLC ("MTO"), a Delaware limited liability company, was formed in August 2024 for
the T7 acquisition and owned no real property before T7. Its membership interests are denominated in
units.

| No. | Date | Transfer | Ownership immediately after |
|---|---|---|---|
| T7 | September 30, 2024 | HSOP sells both parcels by grant deed (recorded September 30, 2024) to MTO for $366,000,000 (office tower parcel $342,000,000; garage parcel $24,000,000; each allocation equals that parcel's full cash value). | MTO units: Pacific Rim Realty Fund IV LP 450, Sutter Family Holdings LLC 300, Northgate Life Insurance Company 250 (1,000 units outstanding) |
| T8 | May 20, 2025 | Sutter Family Holdings LLC sells 200 MTO units to Northgate Life Insurance Company for cash. | Pacific Rim 450, Northgate Life 450, Sutter 100 (1,000 outstanding) |
| T9 | January 12, 2026 | MTO redeems 150 of Pacific Rim's units for cash, at Pacific Rim's request, and cancels them. No other member acquires or transfers any unit. | Pacific Rim 300, Northgate Life 450, Sutter 100 (850 outstanding) |
| T10 | March 3, 2026 | Northgate Life Insurance Company assigns all 450 of its MTO units to Northgate Realty Holdings, Inc., a corporation all of whose stock is, and at all times since its formation has been, owned directly by Northgate Life Insurance Company. | Pacific Rim 300, Northgate Realty Holdings 450, Sutter 100 (850 outstanding) |
| T11 | July 1, 2026 | MTO conveys both parcels by grant deed (recorded July 1, 2026) to Meridian Tower Propco LLC ("Propco"), a newly formed Delaware limited liability company of which MTO is, and has always been, the sole member, and assigns the landlord's interest under the three leases to Propco. | Both parcels: Propco (100% owned by MTO) |
| T12 | August 14, 2026 | Ostrander Mutual Holding Company acquires, by merger of its acquisition subsidiary into Northgate Life Insurance Company, all of the outstanding stock of Northgate Life Insurance Company, which before the merger was widely held with no shareholder owning more than 5%. No interest in MTO, Northgate Realty Holdings, Inc. or Propco is issued or transferred. | MTO units unchanged; Propco unchanged |

## D. Other facts

- Pacific Rim Realty Fund IV LP, Sutter Family Holdings LLC, Northgate Life Insurance Company, Kestrel
  Capital LLC, Lumen Real Estate Fund II LP, Brandt Holdings LLC and Ostrander Mutual Holding Company
  are unrelated to one another and to every individual named above.
- HSOP has never owned any real property other than the two parcels. MTO and Propco have never owned
  any real property other than the two parcels.

# United States v. Patricia Halloran, No. 4:25-cr-00033 (D. Frank.)

**Client:** Patricia Halloran
**Date of birth:** February 18, 1966
**Sentencing:** October 28, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Information, Count 1: wire fraud, 18 U.S.C. § 1343. Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Halloran pleaded guilty on June 9, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- As the outside bookkeeper for two regional distributors, Halloran diverted $310,400 to
  herself from February 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024 by sending false vendor invoices by email.
- Both victims are corporations with annual revenues above $40 million. Their losses were
  largely covered by insurance. The draft PSR finds that no victim suffered substantial financial
  hardship. It considered the §2B1.1 Application Note 4(F) factors, and neither the government
  nor the victims dispute the finding.
- Chapter Two and Three adjustments in the draft PSR: §2B1.1(b)(1)(G) (+12 for loss) and §3B1.3
  (+2, abuse of a position of trust). No other adjustments. No violence or threats, no death or
  bodily injury, no firearm or other weapon. No adjustment under §3A1.1, §3A1.4, §3A1.5 or §3B1.1.
  No continuing criminal enterprise. The offense is not a sex offense and is not covered by §2H1.1.

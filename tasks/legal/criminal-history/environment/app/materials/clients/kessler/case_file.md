# United States v. Joanna Kessler, No. 4:24-cr-00266 (D. Frank.)

**Client:** Joanna Kessler
**Date of birth:** May 5, 1979
**Sentencing:** October 27, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Indictment, Count 1: distribution of fentanyl, 21 U.S.C. § 841(a)(1), (b)(1)(C).
  Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Kessler pleaded guilty to Count 1 on June 30, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- Kessler sold fentanyl pills to a group of regular customers in Ashford. The draft PSR finds
  that her relevant conduct began on October 1, 2023, with her first sale, and continued until
  her arrest on April 18, 2024.
- No firearm, no violence, no role adjustment.

# United States v. Rafael Ochoa, No. 4:25-cr-00290 (D. Frank.)

**Client:** Rafael Ochoa
**Date of birth:** February 14, 1981
**Sentencing:** October 30, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Indictment, Count 1: distribution of methamphetamine, 21 U.S.C. § 841(a)(1), (b)(1)(C).
  Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Ochoa pleaded guilty to Count 1 on July 7, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- On April 10, 2025, Ochoa sold 28 grams of methamphetamine to an undercover officer in
  Fremont County. He was arrested on May 2, 2025 and released on federal pretrial conditions on May 5, 2025.
- The draft PSR finds that his relevant conduct is limited to the April 10, 2025 sale. It finds
  that the cocaine case in Lassen County (CR-2024-2210, see the prior record) is not relevant
  conduct. It involved a different drug, a different supplier and a different county, and it
  is not part of the same course of conduct or common scheme. The parties agree.
- No firearm, no violence, no role adjustment.

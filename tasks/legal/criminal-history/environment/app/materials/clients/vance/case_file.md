# United States v. Colin Vance, No. 4:25-cr-00211 (D. Frank.) (defendant 1 of 3)

**Client:** Colin Vance
**Date of birth:** March 3, 1988
**Sentencing:** October 29, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Indictment, Count 1: conspiracy to distribute methamphetamine, 21 U.S.C. §§ 846, 841(a)(1),
  (b)(1)(C). Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Vance pleaded guilty to Count 1 on June 25, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- From September 2024 through March 2025, Vance bought methamphetamine from a supplier in
  Arizona and resold it in Fremont County. He directed two couriers, his co-defendants, telling
  them when and where to make deliveries and paying them per trip.
- The draft PSR applies a 2-level increase under §3B1.1(c) because Vance supervised the two
  couriers. The plea agreement stipulates to that adjustment, and the defense does not object.
- Vance was not charged under 21 U.S.C. § 848. The draft PSR finds that he did not engage in a
  continuing criminal enterprise: he organized or supervised only two people, and his income from
  the operation was modest.
- No firearm or other weapon, no violence or threats, no death or serious bodily injury. No
  adjustment under §3A1.1, §3A1.4 or §3A1.5. The offense is not a sex offense and is not covered
  by §2H1.1. No victim suffered financial hardship.

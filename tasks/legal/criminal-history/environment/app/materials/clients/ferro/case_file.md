# United States v. Nicholas Ferro, No. 4:25-cr-00402 (D. Frank.)

**Client:** Nicholas Ferro
**Date of birth:** November 3, 1983
**Sentencing:** October 26, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Indictment (one count): possession with intent to distribute cocaine, 21 U.S.C.
  § 841(a)(1), (b)(1)(C). Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Ferro pleaded guilty on June 23, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- On August 2, 2025, Ferro sold 62 grams of cocaine to a DEA confidential source in a
  controlled buy in Ashford and was arrested at the scene. He has been in custody since then.
- The offense of conviction and all of Ferro's relevant conduct took place on August 2, 2025.
- The plea agreement stipulates, and the draft PSR finds, that the methamphetamine sale charged
  in Lassen County case CR-2025-1130 (see the prior record) is not relevant conduct to the
  federal offense. It involved a different drug, a different buyer, a different supplier and
  a different county, and it is not part of the same course of conduct or common scheme.
- No firearm, no violence, no role adjustment.

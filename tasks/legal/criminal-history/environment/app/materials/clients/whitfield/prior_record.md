## CR-2016-1402: Fremont County Circuit Court

- **Charge:** Robbery, F.P.C. § 13-301. Elements: a person "takes property from the person or
  presence of another, against that person's will, by force or threat of force." The statute
  defines force as force sufficient to overcome the victim's resistance. Class 3 felony,
  2 to 10 years.
- **Offense / arrest:** April 9, 2016. Whitfield pulled a purse from a woman's grip after a
  brief struggle. Arrested the same day.
- **Sentence (June 13, 2016):** 12 months' probation. No jail.
- **Later events:** Probation completed successfully on June 13, 2017. On August 1, 2017 the
  court granted Whitfield's application under F.P.C. § 13-907, which lets a court set aside a
  conviction for an offender who has completed his sentence. The order sets aside the judgment
  of guilt, dismisses the information, and restores all civil rights, including the right to
  possess firearms; it contains no firearm restriction, and no other Franklin law limits the
  firearm rights of a person whose conviction is set aside under § 13-907. Under § 13-907 the
  record is not sealed or destroyed, and the conviction may be pleaded and proved in any later
  prosecution. The set-aside was granted as a matter of rehabilitation. Whitfield did not claim
  innocence or legal error.

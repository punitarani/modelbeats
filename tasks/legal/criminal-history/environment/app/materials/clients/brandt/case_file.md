# United States v. Lyle Brandt, No. 4:25-cr-00177 (D. Frank.)

**Client:** Lyle Brandt
**Date of birth:** July 15, 1975
**Sentencing:** October 28, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Indictment (one count): felon in possession of a firearm, 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(1).
- The government filed notice that it will seek an enhanced sentence under 18 U.S.C. § 924(e).
- Brandt pleaded guilty to the § 922(g)(1) count on May 19, 2026 without a plea agreement. The
  factual basis admits only the elements of § 922(g)(1). He did not admit any fact bearing on
  § 924(e) and intends to contest the enhancement.

## Offense facts (draft PSR, not disputed)

- On March 8, 2025, Franklin probation officers searched Brandt's apartment, as his state
  probation terms allowed. In the bedroom closet they found a Mossberg 500 12-gauge pump shotgun.
  Its barrel had been sawed off to 14 inches, and its overall length is 24.5 inches.
- The offense began and ended on March 8, 2025. No drugs, no other weapons, and no other
  criminal conduct were connected to the shotgun. The draft PSR identifies no other relevant
  conduct.

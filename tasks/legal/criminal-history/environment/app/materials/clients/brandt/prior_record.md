### Joint revocation: M-17-1204 and CR-2017-1702

On October 15, 2018, at one hearing, the court found that Brandt had absconded from supervision.
That single violation breached his probation in both M-17-1204 and CR-2017-1702. The court
revoked probation in both cases and imposed one 12-month term of imprisonment "in satisfaction
of the revocations in M-17-1204 and CR-2017-1702." It did not allocate the term between the
cases. Brandt was released on August 20, 2019, and supervision in both cases terminated.

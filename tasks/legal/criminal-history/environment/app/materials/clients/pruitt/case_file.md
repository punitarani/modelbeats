# United States v. Marcus Pruitt, No. 4:25-cr-00051 (D. Frank.) (defendant 6 of 9)

**Client:** Marcus Pruitt
**Date of birth:** September 30, 2001
**Sentencing:** October 29, 2026

## Charge and plea

- Superseding indictment, Count 1: conspiracy to distribute and possess with intent to
  distribute fentanyl, 21 U.S.C. §§ 846, 841(a)(1), (b)(1)(C). The count charges a conspiracy
  "beginning in or about June 2022 and continuing through on or about January 22, 2025."
  Statutory maximum 20 years.
- Pruitt pleaded guilty to Count 1 on July 14, 2026 under a plea agreement.

## Offense facts (plea agreement factual basis, adopted by the draft PSR)

- The Delgado organization distributed fentanyl pills in Fremont County from June 2022 until
  the takedown arrests on January 22, 2025.
- Pruitt joined the conspiracy on March 1, 2024, when Ruben Delgado recruited him as a courier.
  He had no involvement in the organization's drug activity before that date. The government
  does not attribute to him any conduct before March 1, 2024, and the draft PSR holds him
  accountable only for pills he carried, or reasonably foreseeable distributions by others,
  from March 1, 2024 through January 22, 2025.
- Pruitt was arrested on January 22, 2025.
- No firearm, no violence. The draft PSR applies no role adjustment.

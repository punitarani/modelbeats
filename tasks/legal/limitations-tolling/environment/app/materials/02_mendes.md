# Matter 02 — Dario Mendes (bar assault)

**Client:** Dario Mendes, 34, of Astoria, Queens, NY.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Queens County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), October 1, 2026.

## Intake notes

On Friday, June 13, 2025, at about 10:40 p.m., Mr. Mendes was attacked inside the Lantern Tap
Room, 31-08 Ditmars Boulevard, Astoria (operated by Lantern Tap Room LLC). Two men, Bryce
Kessler and Owen Voigt, punched and kicked him after an argument at the bar. He suffered a
fractured jaw and two broken ribs (hospitalized June 13–16, 2025; jaw wired). Both men were
arrested at the scene by NYPD that night.

He wants to sue (1) Kessler and (2) Voigt for assault and battery, and (3) Lantern Tap Room LLC
for negligent security — the bar's own door staff had ejected Kessler for fighting earlier that
night and then let him back in, and the bartender kept serving him.

Criminal cases (from the Queens County Criminal Court docket printouts in the file):
- *People v. Bryce Kessler*: arraigned June 14, 2025 on a misdemeanor complaint charging assault
  in the third degree (Penal Law § 120.00(1)). Pleaded guilty to assault in the third degree on
  October 2, 2025. Sentenced November 13, 2025 to 60 days' jail and three years' probation, with
  an order of protection for Mr. Mendes. No appeal.
- *People v. Owen Voigt*: arraigned June 14, 2025 on a misdemeanor complaint charging assault in
  the third degree. On January 21, 2026 the People moved to dismiss in the interest of justice
  (the complainant's identification of Voigt at the lineup was weak); the court granted the
  motion and dismissed all charges that day. The People did not appeal.

Mr. Mendes did not consult a lawyer until September 2026. No action has been filed against
anyone. No notice of claim issue (no public defendant).

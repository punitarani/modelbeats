# Matter 22 — Marisol Duarte v. Brightwell Pharmacy (refiling in state court)

**Client:** Marisol Duarte, of Manhattan, NY. She uses a wheelchair.
**Defendant:** Brightwell Pharmacy Corp., which operates a pharmacy at 2160 Broadway, Manhattan.
**Forum for the new action:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, New York County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), October 6, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Friday, March 8, 2024: the portable aluminum ramp Brightwell placed over its entrance step
  buckled as Ms. Duarte rolled up it; her wheelchair tipped and she fractured her shoulder.
- Monday, July 15, 2024: her prior counsel filed a complaint in the United States District Court
  for the Southern District of New York against Brightwell asserting (1) a claim under Title III
  of the Americans with Disabilities Act for an injunction requiring an accessible entrance, and
  (2) a New York common-law negligence claim for her personal injuries, under the court's
  supplemental jurisdiction.
- In 2025 Brightwell rebuilt its entrance with a permanent, code-compliant ramp.
- Friday, August 21, 2026: the court entered an Opinion and Order and a Judgment that
  (1) dismissed the ADA claim as moot, and (2) declined to exercise supplemental jurisdiction
  over the negligence claim and dismissed it without prejudice. No appeal was taken.
- Ms. Duarte wants to sue Brightwell for negligence in state court.

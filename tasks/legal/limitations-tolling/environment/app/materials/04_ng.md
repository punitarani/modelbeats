# Matter 04 — Thomas Ng (misplaced hip screw)

**Client:** Thomas Ng, 61, of Flushing, Queens, NY.
**Proposed defendants:** Dr. Arjun Bhatt (orthopedic surgeon) and Bayside Orthopedic Associates, P.C.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Queens County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), October 5, 2026.

## Intake notes

- January 10, 2023: Mr. Ng fractured his left hip (fall on ice). Dr. Bhatt performed an open
  reduction and internal fixation the same day at Bayside General Hospital, using a sliding hip
  screw and side plate. One of the lag screws was placed so that its tip protruded through the
  femoral head into the hip joint.
- Post-operative course with Dr. Bhatt, all for the hip fracture and its fixation, each visit
  scheduled at the previous one:
  - February 7, 2023 — wound check; x-rays; return in 3 months.
  - May 16, 2023 — x-rays; complains of groin pain; "hardware in good position" (it was not);
    return in 4 months.
  - September 19, 2023 — x-rays; pain persists; physical therapy renewed; return in 6 months.
  - March 12, 2024 — x-rays; return in 6 months for final visit.
  - September 12, 2024 — final scheduled post-operative visit; x-rays; note reads "Fracture healed.
    Discharged from orthopedic follow-up. Return PRN." Mr. Ng has not seen Dr. Bhatt or anyone at
    Bayside Orthopedic since.
- April 2026: worsening pain; saw a new orthopedist, Dr. Celia Moreau (not a defendant), who
  ordered a CT on May 19, 2026 showing the screw tip penetrating the joint with severe cartilage
  destruction. Dr. Moreau told him on May 19, 2026 that the screw had been misplaced at the
  original surgery. Hip replacement and hardware removal performed July 2026.
- No other treatment by the defendants.

## Documents

### Dr. Moreau — clinic note (excerpt)

> 05/19/2026. CT left hip reviewed with patient: intra-articular penetration of the superior lag
> screw (approx. 4 mm) with chondrolysis. In my opinion the screw was placed intra-articularly at
> the index procedure (01/10/2023). Plan: hardware removal and THA.

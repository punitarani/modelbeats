# Matter 25 — Carrow Precision Plastics, Inc. v. Tallis Machinery Corp. (warranty)

**Client:** Carrow Precision Plastics, Inc., a New York corporation, Bronx, NY.
**Proposed defendant:** Tallis Machinery Corp., a New York corporation, Long Island City, NY.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Bronx County.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 9, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Equipment Purchase Agreement dated January 15, 2025, signed by both companies' presidents, for
  one 500-ton injection-molding machine, price $612,000. Both companies are merchants dealing in
  this kind of equipment. Terms excerpted below.
- Monday, April 7, 2025: Tallis's truck delivered the machine to Carrow's Bronx plant; Tallis's
  driver tendered it and Carrow's plant manager signed the delivery receipt (below). Under the
  agreement Carrow was responsible for installation; Carrow's own riggers set and installed the
  machine April 9–11, 2025, and Carrow began production April 14, 2025.
- Tuesday, February 3, 2026: the moving platen cracked through; a metallurgist found a casting
  defect present at manufacture. Carrow notified Tallis in writing on February 5, 2026. Tallis
  refused to repair or replace the machine.
- Carrow wants to sue Tallis for breach of warranty. No action has been filed, and there has
  been no tolling agreement or other agreement about time to sue beyond the contract itself.

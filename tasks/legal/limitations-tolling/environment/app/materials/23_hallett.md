**Forum:** New York State Court of Claims.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 2, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Monday, December 2, 2024: Mr. Hallett slipped on a freshly mopped, unsigned floor inside a New
  York State Department of Motor Vehicles office in a State-owned building in Albany, and broke
  his ankle. Personal-injury claim for the State's negligence.
- He hired a lawyer who served a notice of intention to file a claim on the Attorney General by
  certified mail, return receipt requested, mailed Tuesday, February 18, 2025 (AG's return
  receipt signed February 21, 2025). The notice stated the time when and place where the claim
  arose and the nature of the claim, and was properly verified.
- That lawyer then retired. No claim has been filed or served.
- What is the last day to file and serve the claim?

# Matter 08 — Marguerite Lindqvist (unpaid promissory notes)

**Client:** Marguerite Lindqvist, of Scarsdale, NY.
**Proposed defendant:** Peter Vanterpool, of Rye, NY (her former business partner).
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Westchester County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), October 7, 2026.

## Intake notes

Ms. Lindqvist lent Mr. Vanterpool money twice. Both notes are signed by him, unsecured, and
governed by New York law. He has never paid any principal or interest on either note.

- **Note A** — dated Tuesday, February 26, 2019, $85,000, 5% interest. See text below. On June 2,
  2025 Ms. Lindqvist sent a written demand for payment of Note A. No payment, no written response.
- **Note B** — dated September 9, 2019, $40,000, 5% interest, "due and payable in full on
  September 9, 2020." Not paid at maturity.
- On March 18, 2025, Mr. Vanterpool mailed Ms. Lindqvist a handwritten letter, signed by him,
  about Note B (text below). The letter does not mention Note A.
- In a phone call on May 30, 2025, Mr. Vanterpool told her he "owed her on both notes and would
  make it right." Nothing in writing followed.

## Documents

### Note A (complete operative text)

> $85,000.00 — New York, New York — February 26, 2019
> ON DEMAND, for value received, the undersigned promises to pay to the order of Marguerite
> Lindqvist the principal sum of Eighty-Five Thousand Dollars, with interest at five percent per
> annum. — /s/ Peter Vanterpool

### Note B (operative text)

> $40,000.00 — Rye, New York — September 9, 2019
> For value received, the undersigned promises to pay to the order of Marguerite Lindqvist the
> principal sum of Forty Thousand Dollars, with interest at five percent per annum, due and payable
> in full on September 9, 2020. — /s/ Peter Vanterpool

### Vanterpool letter dated March 18, 2025 (complete text)

> Marguerite — I know I still owe you the $40,000 on the note I signed in September 2019, plus the
> interest. I am not disputing it and I will pay it. — Peter Vanterpool (signed)

# Matter 19 — Ethan Lim, a minor (missed elbow fracture)

**Client:** Ethan Lim, a minor, by his guardian ad litem (to be appointed), and his parents,
Grace and Daniel Lim, of Pasadena, California.
**Proposed defendants:** QuickCare Urgent Care of Pasadena, Inc. and Dr. Peter Vance
(private; not public entities).
**Forum if suit is filed:** Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles.
**Our role:** consulting with the family's California counsel, Reyes & Tan LLP.

## Intake notes

- Ethan was born on June 6, 2021 (birth certificate below).
- Friday, February 10, 2023: Ethan (then 20 months old) fell from playground equipment. At
  QuickCare that afternoon Dr. Vance ordered elbow x-rays, read them himself as normal, and sent
  Ethan home with a sling. The x-rays showed a displaced supracondylar fracture.
- Friday, February 24, 2023: a pediatric orthopedist (not a defendant) diagnosed the fracture,
  already healing in a malunited position, and told the parents that day that the fracture was
  visible on the QuickCare x-rays and should have been treated. Corrective osteotomy followed;
  Ethan has a permanent elbow deformity.
- The family did not consult counsel until September 2026. No notice of intention to sue has been
  served on anyone.
- The question for this item is Ethan's own claim against the providers (not the parents'
  claims).

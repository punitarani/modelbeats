# Matter 16 — Isabel Vega v. Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Client:** Isabel Vega, of Glendale, California.
**Proposed defendant:** Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority (LA Metro), a
California public entity.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles.
**Our role:** consulting with her California counsel, Reyes & Tan LLP, who will file.

## Intake notes

- Thursday, April 2, 2026: an LA Metro bus ran a red light at Colorado Street and Brand
  Boulevard in Glendale and struck the car Ms. Vega was driving. Injuries: fractured pelvis.
- Reyes & Tan presented a written claim to LA Metro on May 20, 2026 (hand-delivered to the
  Board Secretary; receipt stamped that day).
- LA Metro sent a Notice of Rejection of Claim, dated September 9, 2026, with a proof of service
  by mail. The envelope reached Reyes & Tan's Los Angeles office on Tuesday, September 15, 2026.
- No suit has been filed.

# Matter 09 — Julia Ferraro v. Corvane Analytics, Inc. (Title VII)

**Client:** Julia Ferraro, of Hoboken, NJ.
**Proposed defendant:** Corvane Analytics, Inc., Manhattan (about 400 employees).
**Forum if suit is filed:** United States District Court for the Southern District of New York.
**Responsible attorney:** Helen Marchetti (this firm has represented Ms. Ferraro since her EEOC
charge was filed).

## Intake notes

- Sex-discrimination and retaliation claims under Title VII arising from her termination on
  February 27, 2025. Charge of discrimination filed with the EEOC New York District Office
  on June 10, 2025 (Charge No. 520-2025-04417), dual-filed with the New York State Division of
  Human Rights. This firm (Helen Marchetti) is listed on the charge as her representative.
- The EEOC issued a Determination and Notice of Rights through its online Public Portal.
  The portal sent its standard notification email to Ms. Marchetti's address at 4:12 p.m. on
  Wednesday, August 5, 2026. Our paralegal, M. Quist, logged into the portal and downloaded the
  notice at 4:31 p.m. the same afternoon and saved it to the file.
- The EEOC sent the same email notification to Ms. Ferraro. She was traveling and did not open
  the portal or look at the notice until Sunday, August 9, 2026.
- Ms. Ferraro wants to sue on her Title VII claims only (no state or city claims in this suit).

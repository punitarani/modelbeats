# Matter 21 — Corwin Crane Rental Corp. v. Bellmore Site Works LLC (refiling)

**Client:** Corwin Crane Rental Corp., a New York corporation with its principal place of
business in Yonkers, NY.
**Defendant:** Bellmore Site Works LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.
**Forum for the new action:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, New York County.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 7, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Thursday, January 19, 2023: at a construction site on West 34th Street, Manhattan, a Bellmore
  excavator operator swung the excavator's boom into Corwin's parked 90-ton crawler crane,
  wrecking the boom. Corwin's crane was there under a rental to the general contractor, not to
  Bellmore; Corwin and Bellmore have no contract with each other. Corwin's claim is negligence
  (property damage: $1.3 million repair cost and lost rentals).
- Corwin's prior counsel sued Bellmore in the United States District Court for the Southern
  District of New York, invoking diversity jurisdiction, by filing a complaint on Monday,
  December 1, 2025. Bellmore was served December 3, 2025.
- Bellmore moved to dismiss for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction: one of its members is a
  New York citizen, so there was no complete diversity. The court granted the motion. The Opinion
  and Order and the Clerk's Judgment dismissing the action without prejudice for lack of
  subject-matter jurisdiction were both entered on Tuesday, April 14, 2026. No appeal was taken.
- Corwin now wants to bring the same negligence claim against Bellmore in state court.

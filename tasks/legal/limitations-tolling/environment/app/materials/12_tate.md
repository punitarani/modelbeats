# Matter 12 — Gerald Tate v. United States (FTCA, postal vehicle collision)

**Client:** Gerald Tate, of Norfolk, Virginia.
**Forum if suit is filed:** United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia,
Norfolk Division.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 9, 2026. (Mr. Tate handled the administrative claim
himself.)

## Intake notes

- Tuesday, September 3, 2024: a U.S. Postal Service delivery vehicle, driven by a letter carrier
  on her route, ran a stop sign at Granby Street and W. 26th Street in Norfolk and struck
  Mr. Tate's car. He was injured (fractured wrist, neck injury) and taken to the hospital that
  day.
- He completed a Standard Form 95 claiming $180,000 and mailed it to the USPS. The USPS
  National Tort Center date-stamped it received on Monday, August 25, 2025.
- USPS denial letter dated January 8, 2026. It went by certified mail; the USPS tracking
  printout shows it accepted at the post office on Friday, January 9, 2026 and delivered to
  Mr. Tate on January 13, 2026.
- Mr. Tate wrote to the USPS asking it to reconsider, enclosing new medical records. The USPS
  National Tort Center date-stamped his request received on Monday, March 30, 2026.
- USPS letter dated May 26, 2026 denying reconsideration. Certified mail; tracking shows it
  accepted at the post office on Wednesday, May 27, 2026 and delivered on Monday, June 1, 2026.
- No suit has been filed.

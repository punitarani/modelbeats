# Matter 05 — Abena Osei (missed breast cancer)

**Client:** Abena Osei, 52, of Yonkers, NY.
**Proposed defendants:** Dr. Martin Greaves (radiologist) and Hudson Imaging Partners, P.C.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Westchester County.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 2, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Thursday, February 4, 2021: screening mammogram at Hudson Imaging Partners, read the same day
  by Dr. Greaves as "BI-RADS 1 — negative." Ms. Osei never returned to Hudson Imaging or to
  Dr. Greaves; there was no follow-up or further treatment by either of them.
- She skipped screening during 2022–2024. On September 15, 2025 she had a mammogram and
  ultrasound at a different practice (Riverside Breast Center), which found a 3.1 cm invasive
  carcinoma with axillary node involvement.
- Monday, September 22, 2025: the Riverside radiologist obtained the 2021 images, compared them,
  and told Ms. Osei in a consultation that day that the 2021 mammogram showed a 9 mm spiculated
  mass in the same location that should have been called suspicious. That was the first time she
  had any reason to think the 2021 study had been misread.
- Treatment ongoing (chemotherapy, mastectomy planned).

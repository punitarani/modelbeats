# Matter 14 — Ana Lucero (injection injury at a community health center)

**Client:** Ana Lucero, of Sunset Park, Brooklyn, NY.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), September 28, 2026. (New client; her former lawyer withdrew.)

## Intake notes

- Friday, January 12, 2024: at Sunset Park Family Health Center, Inc., a nurse practitioner
  employed by the Center gave Ms. Lucero an intramuscular injection in the buttock. The needle
  struck the sciatic nerve. She felt immediate shooting pain and had foot drop by that evening;
  the next day an ER physician told her the injection had injured the nerve.
- The Center is a federally funded community health center. HRSA's deeming notices (below)
  cover the Center and its employees for all of calendar years 2023 and 2024.
- Her former lawyer did not check for federal status. On Thursday, November 20, 2025 he filed a
  medical-malpractice action in Supreme Court, Kings County against the Center and the nurse
  practitioner. He never presented any administrative claim to any federal agency.
- The U.S. Attorney for the Eastern District of New York certified that the defendants were
  deemed federal employees acting within the scope of their employment, removed the action to
  the Eastern District of New York, and substituted the United States as the sole defendant.
- Thursday, September 10, 2026: the EDNY entered a Memorandum and Order and a Judgment
  dismissing the action without prejudice because Ms. Lucero had not presented an administrative
  claim before suing. No appeal.
- We will present an administrative claim (SF-95) to the U.S. Department of Health and Human
  Services. When is the last day for HHS to receive it so the claim is timely?

# Matter 11 — Rosa Cruz v. Pacific Tile Distributors, Inc. (Title VII)

**Client:** Rosa Cruz, of Long Beach, California.
**Defendant:** Pacific Tile Distributors, Inc., Commerce, California.
**Court:** United States District Court for the Central District of California, No. 2:26-cv-02871.
**Our role:** Ms. Cruz's California counsel, Reyes & Tan LLP (Los Angeles), has asked this firm to
review the employer's pending motion to dismiss the complaint as untimely.

## Background (from Reyes & Tan's file)

- Pregnancy-discrimination claim under Title VII. EEOC Los Angeles District Office charge filed
  May 2025 by Reyes & Tan as her representative.
- The EEOC issued a Determination and Notice of Rights through the Public Portal on Wednesday,
  December 31, 2025. The portal notification email reached Reyes & Tan that day, and attorney
  Marisol Reyes opened and read the notice in the portal at 11:20 a.m. on December 31, 2025. The
  notice carries the EEOC's standard language that suit must be filed within 90 days of receipt
  and that receipt generally occurs on the date the charging party or her representative views
  the document.
- Ms. Cruz herself first viewed the notice on Saturday, January 3, 2026.
- Reyes & Tan filed the complaint (Title VII only) in the Central District on Wednesday, April 1,
  2026.
- Pacific Tile has moved to dismiss, arguing the complaint was due no later than March 31, 2026.
- Nothing else bears on timeliness: no request for an extension, no EEOC reconsideration, no
  other filing.

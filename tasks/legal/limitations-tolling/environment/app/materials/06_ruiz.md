# Matter 06 — Mateo Ruiz, an infant, and his parents

**Clients:** Mateo Ruiz (born March 2, 2017), by his parents Lucia and Daniel Ruiz; and Lucia and
Daniel Ruiz individually. Family lives in Hempstead, NY.
**Proposed defendants:** Dr. Steven Albrecht (otolaryngologist) and Long Island Ambulatory
Surgery Center, LLC (private).
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Nassau County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), September 30, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Monday, August 16, 2021: outpatient tonsillectomy on Mateo (then 4) performed by Dr. Albrecht at
  Long Island Ambulatory Surgery Center. Discharged home at 2:15 p.m.
- That evening he had a massive post-tonsillectomy hemorrhage and was taken by ambulance to
  Nassau Children's Hospital (not a defendant), where he suffered a hypoxic brain injury during
  resuscitation. Our expert believes the bleeding came from an arterial injury during the surgery
  that Dr. Albrecht failed to recognize and control before discharge.
- Mateo was never seen again by Dr. Albrecht or at the surgery center; all later care was at
  Nassau Children's and with other providers.
- The parents knew of the injury and suspected the surgery from August 2021, but did not consult
  counsel until September 2026.
- The family wants (1) an action on Mateo's own claim, and (2) the parents' own claims for loss
  of Mateo's services and for the medical expenses they have paid.

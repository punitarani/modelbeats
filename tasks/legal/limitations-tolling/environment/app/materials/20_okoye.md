# Matter 20 — Chidi Okoye (false arrest)

**Client:** Chidi Okoye, of the Bronx, NY.
**Proposed defendants:** the City of New York and NYPD Police Officers Ryan Castellano and
Dimitri Haas.
**Forums:** a federal civil-rights action (42 U.S.C. § 1983) in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York; a state-law false arrest / false imprisonment action
against the City in Supreme Court, Bronx County.
**Intake by:** T. Feld (associate), October 9, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Monday, September 15, 2025, at about 10:40 p.m.: Officers Castellano and Haas arrested
  Mr. Okoye outside his building on Grand Concourse, saying he matched the description of a
  robbery suspect. He had been at work across town at the time of the robbery (time-stamped
  badge records). No warrant.
- He was held overnight at the precinct and at Bronx Central Booking.
- Tuesday, September 16, 2025: arraigned at about 2:30 p.m. in Bronx County Criminal Court on a
  felony complaint charging robbery in the second degree. The court released him on his own
  recognizance at arraignment; he walked out of the courthouse at about 3:15 p.m. that day. He was
  not detained again.
- March 4, 2026: all charges dismissed on the People's motion.
- Nothing has been served or filed on his behalf: no notice of claim, no suit, no application
  for leave to serve a late notice of claim.
- The items for this matter concern only his claims for false arrest / false imprisonment (not
  malicious prosecution or any other claim).

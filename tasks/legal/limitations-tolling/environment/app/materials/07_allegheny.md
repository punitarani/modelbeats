# Matter 07 — Allegheny Fastener Corp. v. Hudson Industrial Supply Co.

**Client:** Allegheny Fastener Corp., a Pennsylvania corporation. Its headquarters, officers,
plant, and sales operations are all in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania; it has no office or employees
in New York.
**Proposed defendant:** Hudson Industrial Supply Co., a New York corporation, Albany, NY.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Albany County.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), October 6, 2026.

## Intake notes

- Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated March 1, 2020 (written; signed by both parties): Hudson
  is Allegheny's exclusive distributor for New York and New England; five-year term; either party
  may terminate without cause only on 180 days' written notice. Hudson's minimum purchase
  commitments were the main source of Allegheny's revenue in that territory. The agreement is
  for distribution services and territorial rights; Allegheny's claim is for lost profits on
  the minimum purchase commitments, not for the price of goods delivered.
- Friday, November 18, 2022: Hudson sent a letter terminating the agreement "effective
  immediately," with no notice period, and stopped placing orders that day. Allegheny's claim is
  breach of the 180-day notice provision and of the minimum purchase commitments.
- Allegheny felt the loss at its Pittsburgh headquarters (lost revenue and idle production
  capacity there).
- No tolling agreement. Settlement talks in 2023 went nowhere.

## Documents

### Exclusive Distribution Agreement, § 18 (Governing Law)

> 18. Governing Law. This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it shall be
> governed by, and construed and enforced in accordance with, the laws of the State of New York,
> without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

(The Agreement contains no forum-selection clause, no clause about the time within which suit
must be brought, and no arbitration clause.)

### Hudson letter dated November 18, 2022 (excerpt)

> Hudson hereby terminates the Exclusive Distribution Agreement effective immediately. Hudson will
> place no further orders.

# Matter 18 — Danielle Brooks (missed lung nodule)

**Client:** Danielle Brooks, 58, of Culver City, California.
**Proposed defendants:** Dr. Howard Lisle (radiologist) and Westside Diagnostic Imaging Medical
Group, Inc. (private; not a public entity).
**Forum if suit is filed:** Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles.
**Our role:** consulting with her California counsel, Reyes & Tan LLP, who will file.

## Intake notes

- Wednesday, October 2, 2024: chest CT at Westside Diagnostic for a persistent cough, read by
  Dr. Lisle as "no acute cardiopulmonary findings." The study in fact showed a 1.4 cm nodule in
  the right upper lobe. No follow-up was recommended. She had no further dealings with Dr. Lisle
  or Westside Diagnostic.
- She had no reason to suspect anything was wrong until Thursday, November 20, 2025, when a new
  pulmonologist, Dr. Nina Ruiz-Abbott (not a defendant), performed a CT that showed a 4.2 cm
  mass, compared it the same day with the October 2, 2024 images, and told Ms. Brooks at their
  appointment that afternoon that the nodule had been visible and missed on the 2024 study.
  Biopsy confirmed stage III adenocarcinoma.
- Tuesday, September 1, 2026: Reyes & Tan personally served on Dr. Lisle and on Westside
  Diagnostic a written notice of intention to commence an action for professional negligence,
  stating the legal basis of the claim and the type of loss sustained, including the nature of
  the injuries suffered.
- No suit has been filed.

# Matter 17 — Lorna Whitfield v. City of Oakland (sidewalk fall)

**Client:** Lorna Whitfield, of Oakland, California.
**Proposed defendant:** City of Oakland.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Superior Court of California, County of Alameda.
**Our role:** consulting with her California counsel, Reyes & Tan LLP, who will file.

## Intake notes

- Wednesday, February 12, 2025: Ms. Whitfield tripped on a lifted sidewalk panel (a 2½-inch
  vertical offset caused by a City street-tree root) on Grand Avenue, Oakland, and broke her
  wrist and hip. She knew of her injuries and their cause that day.
- Her claim was presented to the Oakland City Clerk on Monday, June 30, 2025 (City Clerk's
  received stamp).
- Friday, August 1, 2025: the City's Risk Management Division sent the letter below by
  first-class mail. Nothing else was ever received from the City.
- No suit has been filed.

## Documents

### City of Oakland letter dated August 1, 2025 (complete text)

> Re: Claim of Lorna Whitfield, Claim No. C25-04482, Date of Incident 02/12/2025.
> Dear Ms. Whitfield: The City of Oakland has completed its review of the above claim. Your
> claim is denied. Thank you. — Risk Management Division, City of Oakland

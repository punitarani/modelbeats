# Matter 01 — Ellsworth Cold Storage LLC (construction defects)

**Client:** Ellsworth Cold Storage LLC, a New York limited liability company, 88 Ferris Street,
Brooklyn, NY (Red Hook). Contact: Dana Ellsworth, managing member.
**Forum if suit is filed:** Supreme Court of the State of New York, Kings County.
**Intake by:** R. Okonjo (paralegal), September 29, 2026.

## Intake notes

Ellsworth owns two buildings at the Red Hook site and has problems with both.

**Project 1 — the warehouse.** Ellsworth hired Ridgeline Builders, Inc. (a New York general
contractor; not a design professional) under a written lump-sum construction contract dated
June 3, 2019 to build a 60,000 sq. ft. refrigerated warehouse. Ellsworth wants to sue Ridgeline
for breach of contract: the roof membrane was installed without the specified vapor barrier and
the freezer slab was poured without the specified insulation. The contract has no clause about
when claims accrue, no clause shortening or lengthening the time to sue, and no arbitration
clause. Ridgeline's warranty period (one year) ended in 2021 and is not the basis of the claim.

Timeline from the project file (see documents below):
- Ridgeline's crews finished all of the work under the contract, including every punch-list
  item, on Wednesday, March 4, 2020. The architect's certificate of substantial completion and
  the final punch-list sign-off both bear that date. Ridgeline demobilized the same day and did
  no further work at the site.
- Temporary certificate of occupancy: March 27, 2020. Permanent C of O: August 14, 2020.
- Final payment (retainage) to Ridgeline: May 18, 2020.
- First signs of trouble (frost heave in the freezer slab; condensation in the roof deck):
  January 21, 2025. Engineer's report identifying the missing vapor barrier and insulation:
  April 9, 2025.

**Project 2 — the rooftop addition.** Separate written contract dated October 2, 2021 with Pardo
Ashe Architects, P.C. (licensed New York architects) for the design of a two-story office
addition on the roof of the older building, and construction administration. The addition was
built by a different contractor (not a target). The construction work was completed on
Wednesday, August 9, 2023; Pardo Ashe made its final site inspection and issued its final
certificate for payment that same day, and performed no services for Ellsworth after that date.
In June 2026 the addition's steel framing was found to be undersized for the rooftop snow-drift
load because of a calculation error in Pardo Ashe's drawings. Ellsworth wants to sue Pardo Ashe
"for breach of the design contract, and for malpractice if we need to plead it that way too."

Paralegal's preliminary note (not verified): "Contract claims — six years? Ridgeline maybe
expired March 2026; Pardo Ashe OK until 2029?"
